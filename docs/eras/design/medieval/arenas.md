# Medieval: twelve arenas and the binding mission ladder (synthesizer part 1)

Verified against the code: sizes are `small 128 / medium 192 / large 256` cells at 0.5 u per cell = **64 / 96 / 128 u** (`world/arena.js SIZES`; nav cells are 1 u), MAX_H 120 steps (60 u), one global water plane per arena (`lava` is a boolean on that plane, so an arena has water OR lava, never both), props <= 1500, 16 materials exist (`grass dirt sand stone snow mud marble lava planks brick cobble ash sandstone savanna moss blood`), weathers `clear cloudy rain storm snow sandstorm fog`. Default zones are 0.22W wide centred at +-0.28W, so the default medium edge-to-edge gap is 32.6 u; this era overrides zone placement per recipe (below) because a 44 u longbow must not start the battle in range.

## 1. Conventions that apply to all twelve

- **Ids** are `med_<name>` (a superset of the AR2 prefix list; the Ancient recipe ids are bare, so a prefix removes any chance of a registry collision). `env.era = 'medieval'`. Theme vocabulary (one canonical set, plan W7): `meadow ford village abbey castle moor mountain`.
- **Materials.** Three appended (ids 16-18, REGISTRY assigns): `lists_sand` (pale tourney sand, speed 0.95), `heather` (moor, 0.90), `scree` (mountain, 0.85). Proposal A asked for five; `flagstone`, `mud_deep` and `ash_scree` duplicate the shipped `cobble` (1.12), `mud` (0.72) and `ash` (0.90) and are not added.
- **Scale and tempo (plan W11).** Foot 1.9-2.9 u/s, mounted 3.2-3.5 (run x2.4), siege 0.7-1.5. Ranges: melee 1.8-3.4 u, poacher 28, crossbow 30, longbow 44, mangonel 46, springald 50, trebuchet 72 (min 26), Lady Counterweight 90 (min 30). Zone placement (edge to edge, inner sides): small 28 u (zones centred +-0.34W, width 0.2W), medium 52 u (+-0.36W, 0.18W), large 74 u (+-0.38W, 0.18W); depth 0.7W everywhere. Resulting targets with both sides advancing: first damage (a bow or bolt exchange, a first shell) 6-10 s; first melee contact 12-20 s (a held pike line stretches it to about 20 s, which is the point); small arenas 8-12 s (declared exception for Quick Duel). Artillery starts with its cooldown at 60 percent so the first shell lands about 7-10 s in. No unit starts inside its own weapon's range of an enemy except trebuchets and Lady Counterweight, which are range 72/90 by design; the placement rule forbids siege machines within 20 u of their zone's front edge.
- **Walls follow the Troy pattern** (touching circle props on a levelled strip, chain of `med_curtain_wall`), not oriented rectangles; moats and rivers are channels carved under the single water plane (deep = impassable); a drawbridge is a soft prop that blocks its lane until a script event lowers it (`editTerrain` lays the deck) or collapses into the moat (plan M12).
- **Vehicle corridors (plan W10): every lane a machine uses is >= 6 u wide** (rams, trebuchets, the Rolling Keep, the Plague Cart; gate and portcullis openings are 6 u, bridge decks 6 u); a 6-ram column pass test runs on every castle and bridge arena. Foot-only lanes (village alleys) may be 4 u.
- **Readability (W8/W9):** at most 35 visible (type, variant) prop batches per recipe; buildings and walls use voxel 0.2, everything else 0.1 (props.md); tall props (windmill 9 u, bell tower 12 u, keep 16 u) keep >= 75 percent of 200 head-height points visible from the default camera (yaw -0.7, pitch 0.65).
- Campaign uses 9 of 12; `med_mount_perpetual`, `med_siege_camp` and `med_moat_keep` serve Quick, Survival, Daily, the Arena Builder presets and puzzles 5 and 6 (Quick-only extras are the first rung of the cut ladder).

### Layout, three sentences each

1. **`med_pageant_green`.** A gently rolling green with a maypole on a 12 u dais at the centre and 6 u dirt paths crossing it east-west and north-south. Two grandstands face each other across the green, each backed by a pavilion, and bunting strings run along both touchlines on poles. The west zone sits behind a hedge row and the east zone on a slight rise, so the pikes have a ground-level view of the charge run-up; the main lane for the dragon float and machines is the east-west path. Vehicle needs: the 6 u paths.
2. **`med_tourney_field`.** Two lists run east-west, 20 u wide each and divided by a continuous `med_tilt_barrier` line, so a charge stays in its lane for 52 u. Striped pavilions close both ends (the generals' tents), grandstands with crowd props line both flanks, and hedge strips on the outer margins hide the flanking skirmishers. The lists floor is `lists_sand`, which is faster than grass and tells the camera exactly where the charge will go. Vehicle needs: the lists are 20 u wide.
3. **`med_ford_of_dithering`.** A river runs north-south with a 14 u shallow ford at the centre and a stone bridge 18 u upstream (6 u deck, parapets). The west bank is 2 u higher with a hedge, willows and a row of spike stakes (the pike side); the east bank is flat open meadow with a 30 u run-up (the cavalry side). The hold marker (r7) sits on the west lip of the ford, and a windmill on the north rise gives the camera a landmark. Vehicle needs: the bridge deck and the ford (both >= 6 u).
4. **`med_mizzlemoor`.** A shallow valley holds three low hills in a triangle 30 u apart, each crowned with a `med_beacon_brazier` (the capture points). Firm heather ridgelines of 6 u connect the hills (the cavalry roads) while mud bogs fill the lowlands between them, slowing foot to 0.72. Standing stones give cover on the ridges and a default fog of 0.25 keeps long-range volleys honest. Vehicle needs: the ridgelines.
5. **`med_castle_dour`.** A 54 x 54 u curtain-wall square with four round corner towers sits on the east half of the map behind an 8 u deep moat, with the gatehouse (gate plus portcullis, 6 u opening) in the west wall and a drawbridge across the moat. Inside the cobble bailey a keep stands on a 4 u motte in the north-east, hoardings crown the wall segments flanking the gate, and six oil cauldrons wait on the wall walk. West of the moat lies a 44 u open meadow with mantlets and pits for the besiegers, with two tree lines north and south hiding flank approaches. Vehicle needs: the approach lane (8 u), the drawbridge deck (6 u), the gate and portcullis (6 u), the bailey lanes (6 u).
6. **`med_bellfount_abbey`.** A 60 x 44 u walled precinct holds a flagstone cloister with arches in the middle, a bell tower in the north-east corner and herb beds and a fish pond along the south side. The abbey gate (a `med_castle_gate` instance, team-owned) stands in the west wall and a postern lets small groups through the east wall. Three capture spots (herbs, well, bell) are marked, and a mint-green gas cloud is a scripted set-piece that drifts through the cloister. Vehicle needs: the gate and the aisle behind it (6 u).
7. **`med_pennywhistle`.** A high street 6 u wide runs east-west through a cobble market square with a well and stalls, flanked by fourteen thatched cottages and a barn with 4 u foot-only lanes between them. A windmill with animated sails crowns a rise in the north; haystacks and hedges fill the hayfields on the east side. The stone lanes (cobble) do not burn while the thatch and hay do, which is the route puzzle of the fire mission. Vehicle needs: the high street and square (>= 6 u).
8. **`med_long_bridge`.** A 40 u stone bridge, 6 u wide with parapets, crosses a 14 u deep river that nothing can wade, with a toll booth at mid-span and a bridgehead on each bank. Two shallow fords 40 u upstream and downstream give a 25 s flanking detour so the bridge is a funnel and not a cage. The deck section under the toll booth is the `editTerrain` target for the bridge-drop set-piece. Vehicle needs: the deck (6 u, the narrowest vehicle corridor of the era, exercised by the 6-ram column test) and the fords.
9. **`med_dour_courtyard`.** An enclosed cobble bailey 70 x 60 u with a keep at the north end, a bell tower, a grandstand on the east side and market stalls on the west, with a maypole and bunting around a central dais. Two 6 u gate gaps (no gate props) in the east and west curtain walls admit ground forces; the sky is open for the dragon. Crowd props panic at fire and wyvern dives. Vehicle needs: open bailey (>= 6 u everywhere).
10. **`med_mount_perpetual`.** A 128 u slope climbs 18 u from the camp at the foot to a ledge with the lair mouth at the top, with a switchback road 6 u wide. A lava creek (the arena's single plane) crosses the road with two 6 u causeways; hoard heaps glint on the slopes and a dragon skull marks the previous tenant. Ash drifts in a cloudy sky. Vehicle needs: the switchbacks and causeways.
11. **`med_siege_camp`.** A palisade-ringed camp of tents and supply carts stands in the west with four engine pads (levelled 8 x 8 u squares) on a raised bank. A ditch and spike stakes face the castle stub (a gate and a wall segment) on the east edge, which is the sally source. Pitch barrels are stacked near the pads, a fire hazard for both sides. Vehicle needs: the pads and the lanes between (6 u).
12. **`med_moat_keep`.** A round motte 20 u across carries a small keep and one tower, surrounded by a ring moat 4 u wide with a single drawbridge. The courtyard around the keep is mud and trampled grass, with spike stakes at the bridge. Small arena, quick fights, good for duels and for the destroy objective in Quick. Vehicle needs: the drawbridge (6 u).
