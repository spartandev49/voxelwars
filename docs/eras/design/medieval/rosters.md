# Medieval: the final 34-unit roster (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Currency groats (Ancient hoplite = 100). All numbers are design intent for BALANCE and are never measured here; directions of counters are written BEFORE measuring (plan ER7: asserted rows need >= 200 battles per pair, Wilson 95% lower bound >= 55 percent and point >= 65 percent, mechanic-off control). Ids are unique within the era and distinct from the 43 Ancient ids and 7 Ancient faction ids (checked against `src/content/era_ancient/stats.js`) and from every id in the Modern and Sci-Fi proposals (grep, no hit). Every id is lower_snake, immutable once shipped.

Legend. **Role** (sim role): melee ranged cavalry siege support hero monster beast swarm. **Height class**: S under 2.0 u, M 2.0-3.0 u (a hum1 soldier is about 2.3 u), T 3.0-4.5 u (riders, banners, tall staffs), L 4.5-7 u, XL over 7 u (an `A` after the class marks the air layer). **HP class**: XS <= 70, S 71-140, M 141-250, L 251-450, XL 451-900, XXL over 900. **Speed class**: crawl <= 1.5 u/s, slow 1.6-2.2, mid 2.3-2.9, fast 3.0-4.2, air (flies, 4-5 u/s). **Cost class**: S <= 100, M 101-180, L 181-300, XL over 300. **Model recipe**: BP = hum1 blueprint through `compileSoldier`; BM = `buildMounted` (quad1 mount + hum1 rider); BB = bespoke builder on the `LG/SLG` toolkit (`beasts/*.js`); BS = hum1 blueprint scaled with bespoke parts. Lite crew = 6 parts per crew member (decision D12), not the 10-13 parts of the shipped Ancient crews (which is why Ancient machines are 26-43 parts and ours are 20-30). **Tracer** T = built early: all M1 plumbing-slice units plus one unit per rig family (`xbow` pose, trebuchet1, ram1, dragon1).

Engines: **E1** The Pageant Goes Wrong; **E2** The Rule Book Taken Literally (chivalry, oaths, tolls, contracts: loud and sincere, never deadpan memo-speak); **E3** Heavy Things Arrive Late (weight, mud, siege logistics, doom by pigeon, Cassandra's plague). Bark roles are the nine existing roles of `humor/barks.js`. First mission codes follow the binding ladder in `arenas.md` section 3 (P player roster, E enemy).
