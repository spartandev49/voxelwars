# Modern: twelve arenas and the binding mission ladder (synthesizer part 1)

Verified against the code: sizes are `small 128 / medium 192 / large 256` cells at 0.5 u per cell = **64 / 96 / 128 u** (`world/arena.js SIZES`; nav cells are 1 u), `MAX_H` 120 steps, **one global water plane per arena** (a `lava` boolean on that plane, so an arena has water OR lava; deep water is impassable, shallow x1.7 cost), props <= 1500, 16 materials exist (`grass dirt sand stone snow mud marble lava planks brick cobble ash sandstone savanna moss blood`), weathers `clear cloudy rain storm snow sandstorm fog`, `sanitizeEnv` slices `env.theme` to **16 characters**, one height per cell (no overpasses, tunnels or interiors). Default zones are 0.22 W wide centred at +-0.28 W, so the default medium edge-to-edge gap is 32.6 u, which would put a 28 u rifle in range at tick 0: this era overrides zone placement per recipe (section 1).

## 1. Conventions that apply to all twelve

- **Ids** are `mod_<name>` (a superset of the AR2 prefix list; the Ancient recipe ids are bare, so a prefix removes any chance of a registry collision). `env.era = 'modern'`. **Theme vocabulary** (one canonical set, plan W7, all <= 16 characters): `mod_field mod_trench mod_city mod_industrial mod_harbour mod_air mod_desert`. Audio: ambience and step tables are keyed by these themes and by the biomes in the table (`step_concrete`, `step_asphalt`, `step_metal` are new rows).
- **Materials.** Seven appended (ids 16-22, REGISTRY assigns, frozen once shipped): `asphalt` (road, speed 1.10), `concrete` (slabs, dam, yard, 1.05), `paving` (sidewalks and plazas, 1.08), `ballast` (rail, 0.90), `steel_deck` (bridge and quay plates, 1.00), `lino_tile` (greenhouse floor, 1.05), `road_stripe` (runway and road paint, 1.10). Proposal A asked for `duckboard`, `trench_mud`, `stubble`, `felt_green`; B for `churned_mud`, `gravel`, `tarmac`, `paint_yellow`: they duplicate the shipped `planks`, `mud`, `savanna`, `grass` and are not added (the Medieval pack made the same call). Materials carry `era: 'modern'` and per-row `foot` data for the step table.
- **Scale and tempo (plan W11).** Foot 2.4-3.6 u/s, wheels 3.6-6.5, tanks 1.9-2.6, guns 1.2-2.4, air 1.4-5.8. Ranges: melee 1.4-2.4 (blade 6), SMG and pea 18, dynamite 20, rifle 28 (clerk 32), MG 36, AT 26, tank cannon 42-50, parasol 46, mortar 56 (min 14), sharpshooter 64 (min 10), howitzer 76 (min 26), Final Notice 110 (min 35). **Zone placement (edge to edge, inner sides):** small **48 u** (zones 8 u wide), medium **64 u** (16 u), large **92 u** (18 u), depth 0.7 W everywhere. Resulting targets with both sides advancing: first damage 6-12 s on medium and large (median 8-10), 10-16 s on the large missions where the enemy holds, small arenas 4-8 s (declared exception, the enemy `holds` and the player decides when to close). Artillery and snipers start with 60 % of their cooldown and are placed >= 14 u behind their zone's front edge (the Final Notice >= 20 u): howitzer 76 vs 64 + 14 = 78, sharpshooter 64, Final Notice 110 vs 92 + 20 = 112. No unit starts inside its own weapon range of an enemy.
- **Vehicle corridors (plan W10): every lane a machine uses is >= 6 u wide**; bridge decks and quays 8 u, the dam crest road **10 u** (the Grand Teapot has radius about 3 u), downtown streets 8 u with a 10 u main avenue, rail aisles 7 u, roadworks lanes >= 8 u; a 6-tank column pass test runs on every arena with vehicles (W10); gates in the desert compound are 6 u openings. Foot-only lanes (greenhouse aisles, trenches) may be 3-4 u.
- **Readability (W8/W9):** at most 35 visible (type, variant) prop batches per recipe; buildings use voxel 0.2, everything else 0.1 (props.md); tall props (office block 10 u, apartment 8 u, cranes 12 u, control tower 9 u) stay at map edges or <= 4-5 u in the fight corridor and keep >= 75 % of 200 head-height points visible from the default camera (the Teutoburg "wall around a glade" pattern); no tunnels or overpasses (the roadworks "interchange" is a flat junction).
- **Hazards and markers.** Only existing hazard kinds plus the M11 `mine_*` kinds (placed by `lay_mine`, by scripts and by the Minefield god power; the sanitizer keeps the extra fields, plan M11); the garden centre's wet patches reuse the `quicksand` kind. Markers: `hill`, `exit`, `vip_start`, `general_spawn`, `waypoint` plus capture posts (`mod_signal_post` props) and the core object (`mod_switchboard_tower`), all within |x|,|z| <= 0.25 x size as the validator requires.
- **Symmetry and fairness (S22):** symmetric recipes set `g.sym` and must show 45-55 % mirror fairness; asymmetric ones join the exemption list (35-65 %): bridge gorge, harbour, dam, desert outpost.
- **Campaign uses 9 of 12;** `mod_desert_outpost`, `mod_roadworks` and `mod_garden_centre` serve Quick, Survival, Daily, the Arena Builder and puzzles. If the cut ladder (rung 1) takes arenas, those three go first; the nine mission arenas are never cut.

### 2.1 `mod_parade_yard` Parade Yard (small, mx)

Signature features: a flat drill square of concrete edged with grass, nine `mod_target_dummy` pop-up cutouts in a firing line, a loudspeaker post (a `mod_lamp_post` variant with speaker boxes), a windsock pole, tyre-course obstacles (`mod_crate_stack` and `mod_hay_bale` rows), a `mod_sandbag_wall` demo bay. Layout: The player zone sits at the south end behind a painted firing line and the enemy zone at the north end behind two rows of hay bales, with the dummies strung across the middle third as set dressing. A sandbag demo bay at each flank gives the cover lesson a preview and the tyre course gives the runabout something to hop. The whole yard is flat, so the sight lines are long and the lesson is magazines, not terrain. Objective suitability: eliminate and puzzles; the stage for the live-fire set-piece. First contact: 48 u gap, enemy `holds`, 4-8 s.

### 2.2 `mod_hedgerow_meadow` Hedgerow Meadow (medium, mx)

Signature features: a field patchwork split by hedgerow lines into a cover lattice (`mod_hedge_row`, low cover, flammable), hay bales, a farm lane, the picnic hill (a 2 u rise) with `mod_picnic_hamper` and a blanket decal at the centre, oaks as shared `any` props. Layout: Hedges run in three staggered rows across the meadow with 6 u gaps so a line of riflemen can step from one to the next while a runabout can still pass on the cart tracks. The picnic hill rises exactly at the centre and is overlooked by the middle hedge, so holding it means standing behind a hedge, not on it. Both flanks end in tall hedges that funnel the second and third waves toward the hill. Objective suitability: hold_hill and capture; the hill marker is the `hill` marker. First contact: 64 u, 7-11 s.

### 2.3 `mod_trench_line` The Long Trench (medium, mx)

Signature features: two carved trench systems (depth 1 u steps with ramp ladders at 6 u spacing, plank duckboards), a no-man's land of old craters (pre-battle `arena.crater` calls) and `mod_barbed_coil` lines, three `mod_signal_post` capture posts (one in each trench, one in the middle crater field), `mod_dugout_roof` command dugouts, `mod_sandbag_nest` rings. Layout: Two parallel trenches face each other across a 20 u cratered no-man's land, each with a lip of sandbags and two vehicle ramps wide enough for a runabout. The middle post sits in the largest crater so capturing it costs cover, and the two outer posts sit in the enemy trench with dugout roofs that collapse under a mortar. A shallow ridge behind the enemy trench hides the reinforcement route. Objective suitability: capture, destroy, kill_general. First contact: 64 u, 8-12 s with the defenders holding.

### 2.4 `mod_bridge_gorge` Bridge Gorge (medium, asymmetric start)

Signature features: one 44 u steel-deck bridge (8 u wide, planks and steel_deck cells) over a 40 u gorge (4 u of deck rest on each bank), `mod_bridge_pier` piers under the deck, `mod_guard_rail` and `mod_lamp_post` along both edges, `mod_road_barrier` chokes at the far-bank exit, a shallow ford 30 u downstream as the flank route, stone cliffs on both sides, a river (deep channel impassable). Layout: The player starts on the near bank in a car-park zone with a clear run to the deck; the far bank has the exit marker behind a road barrier, with ambush spots on both sides. The ford is the only other crossing, so a flanking force wades (x1.7 cost) while the column is committed to the deck. Walking off the deck is a fall into the deep channel (hazard), which is the arena's fairness note: the guard rails snap in the set-piece and not before. Objective suitability: escort, kill_general, destroy. First contact: the van is exposed at 14-18 s when it reaches the far-bank ambush. Exempt from the S22 mirror band (asymmetric by design).

### 2.5 `mod_rail_yard` Rail Yard (large, mx)

Signature features: five parallel tracks (`mod_rail_track` flat strips) with 7 u aisles, rows of `mod_boxcar_cargo` wagons as cover (four of them the destroy targets), two `mod_signal_post` lamps, a loco shed (`mod_warehouse`), a turntable (a painted ring), `mod_fuel_tank` pairs at the throat. Layout: The player zone is the throat of the yard at the south; the wagon rows run north in staggered lines, so every aisle is a cover maze; the four target boxcars are in the farthest siding behind an MG nest line. Fuel tanks at the throat are a friendly hazard for anyone who shells carelessly. The big open space at the east end is the only place the Behemoth can turn. Objective suitability: destroy and capture. First contact: 92 u, 10-16 s.

### 2.6 `mod_downtown` Downtown (large, mxz)

Signature features: a 5 x 4 block grid, streets 8 u wide with a 10 u main avenue, shop rows and offices at the map edges (the tall-building rule), apartment blocks as block filler up to the 4-5 u corridor height, parked cars (low cover that vehicles shove), `mod_road_barrier` chokes, lamp posts, a central plaza of paving; the mission adds the `mod_switchboard_tower` (the core) at the centre-left plaza. Layout: The grid makes four lanes for a vehicle column and a dozen cut-throughs for infantry; the plaza is the only open ground. Buildings are circle chains on the block corners so alleys stay passable (plan W2: no oriented footprints needed if the chains are laid on the corners; the throwaway prototype of the spike decides). Shells crater the asphalt and the block fronts catch fire. Objective suitability: defend_core, eliminate, survive_waves; the perf scenario "tank column in a city with artillery" lives here. First contact: 92 u, 10-16 s.

### 2.7 `mod_airfield` Airfield (large, mx)

Signature features: a 70 u runway (concrete with road_stripe markings), two 7 u taxiways, three `mod_hangar` buildings on the north edge, a `mod_control_tower` landmark, `mod_parked_plane` toy airliners and gliders, six `mod_fuel_tank` (explosive), windsock poles. Layout: The flat runway is a bowling alley for ground units and a landing strip for nothing; the hangars and tower are the only cover. The control tower stands at the north-east, and the general holds under it. Parked planes and fuel tanks are the cover and the chain-reaction toys. Objective suitability: kill_general, destroy, survive. First contact: 92 u, 8-12 s (air units close 2x faster).

### 2.8 `mod_harbour` Harbour (medium, asymmetric)

Signature features: a concrete quay with three `planks` piers into a deep-water sea, two `mod_crane_gantry` on the edge, `mod_shipping_container` stacks forming a maze, `mod_warehouse` backdrops, `mod_barge` floating props, a landing ramp (a terrain slope into the shallows) for the barge set-piece, shallow and deep water. Layout: The sea runs along the north edge; zone B sits on the landing ramp and in the shallows (units cannot stand in deep water), so waves arrive down the ramp and along the pier roots and the quay is a gauntlet. Container stacks make the maze that favours dynamite and mines; the piers are one-lane bottlenecks of 8 u. The barge floats at the ramp and rams in for wave four. Objective suitability: survive_waves, defend_core. First contact: waves spawn at the sea edge, 7-10 s.

### 2.9 `mod_dam` The Dam (large, asymmetric)

Signature features: a concrete dam wall across a valley with a **10 u crest road**, a **drained reservoir basin** upstream (a dry muddy lakebed, a joke sign: the water plane cannot be both a full reservoir and a dry channel, so the reservoir is "drained for maintenance"), a dry spillway channel downstream with two `mod_dam_gate` sluice gates set into the wall, a river at the valley floor (the arena's one water plane, low), `mod_bridge_pier` supports under the crest, `mod_guard_rail`, `mod_lamp_post`. Layout: The player zone is the downstream valley floor looking up at the wall; the enemy holds the crest road and the basin behind it, so the fight climbs a service ramp (8 u wide) at each end of the dam. The sluice gates are destroyable (1800 hp) and the scripted burst sends a white-water particle sheet down the spillway (visual only). The basin is wide open ground for the Chandelier's arrival and the Final Notice's emplacement. Objective suitability: kill_general, destroy. First contact: 92 u, 12-18 s. Exempt from the S22 band.

### 2.10 `mod_desert_outpost` Desert Outpost (medium, asymmetric)

Signature features: a walled compound (container chains and sandbag walls) with two 6 u gates, an animated `mod_radar_dish` (spins when the prop-spin row exists, static otherwise), a `mod_warehouse`, dunes, `any` cacti and palms. Layout: The compound sits on a slight rise with a flat approach on two sides, so the defender has cover and the attacker has dust. The dish is the landmark and the hold-hill marker; the gates are the vehicle chokes. The dunes on the other two sides hide a flanking route for a runabout. Objective suitability: defend_core, capture, hold_hill. First contact: 64 u, 7-11 s. Puzzle 3 (armour faces) lives here.

### 2.11 `mod_roadworks` Roadworks Without End (large, mxz)

Signature features: a flat four-way junction with a roundabout (no overpass: one height per cell), `mod_traffic_cone` fields, `mod_road_barrier` chicanes, lanes >= 8 u, `mod_hay_bale` variant 3 painted as a cow standing in a lane (the E3 engine's mascot), parked cars, `mod_lamp_post`. Layout: The junction forces any column through the roundabout where the cow stands; the cone fields scatter with a confetti puff, which is the only cosmetic destruction on the map. Four exits make the map good for the Daily. The cow never moves and is never a unit. Objective suitability: escort, capture, survive_waves. First contact: 92 u, 11-17 s.

### 2.12 `mod_garden_centre` Garden Centre (medium, mxz)

Signature features: a greenhouse grid (`mod_greenhouse`: glass panes pop, no cover), `mod_hedge_row` flower-bed borders, a potting shed (`mod_shipping_container` variant), `lino_tile` floors, wet-paving patches (the `quicksand` hazard kind). Layout: A 3 x 3 grid of greenhouses with 3-4 u aisles between them and a 6 u main path; units fight through glass that provides no cover and shatters into voxel confetti. Wet patches slow anyone who cuts a corner. The potting shed at the north end is the only hard cover, so every fight there is a decision about glass. Objective suitability: survive_waves, eliminate, capture. First contact: 64 u, 6-10 s. Puzzle 4 lives here.

## 3. The binding mission ladder (Medieval's arenas.md set the precedent; part 2 builds `missions.json` from these rows)

Acts: **I "Basic Forms"** (reload, cover, pin), **II "Combined Arms"** (armour, shells, mines), **III "Full Spectrum"** (air, heal and repair, everything). One new mechanic taught per mission (M9 teaches none); a star 3 tests only mechanics taught strictly earlier, or none; the finales (M3, M6, M9) combine two or more earlier mechanics. Star 1 = win; star 2 = win with >= 50 % of the army cost alive (generic, as Ancient); par is in rq (`fmtCost`). Enemy cost : player budget is the pressure curve (0.55 → 1.1). Every mission has a set-piece package `{event, shot{from,to,hold,ease}, announcer slot, stinger, sfx}`; shots run 3-6 s in real time with the sim at 0.5x, skippable, Reduce Motion = cut. All enemy groups may be cross-faction ("hired"). Currency `rq`.

### 3.6 Closed star-helper vocabulary used by the ladder

From the plan: `usedMechanic(id, n)`, `suppressed(n)`, `noLoss(defs)`, `thrift(par)`, `underTime(s)`, `shieldsBroken(n)` (unused here). Registry appends (each with its tracker counter reading sim events, no new state): `coverFracAtLeast(f)` (share of own unit-seconds with `inCover`), `hitsWhileReloadingAtMost(n)` (damage events on a unit in its reload state), `usedAll(ids, n)` (every id used >= n times), `keptAlive(def, n)`, `coreHpAtLeast(f)`, `noFriendlyFire`. `usedMechanic` ids for this era: `reload`, `cover`, `pin`, `armour` (hits on a side or rear face), `shells` (indirect-shell kills and craters), `mines` (mine kills), `air` (air kills by AA), `repair` (hp repaired plus healed), `strike` (call_strike hits). Pin and strike counters are shared with the achievements.
