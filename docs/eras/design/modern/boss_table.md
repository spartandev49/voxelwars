# Modern: the five survival bosses (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Rules applied. Five bosses, five different silhouettes, **none a scaled hum1 soldier** (the plan asks for a non-scaled-hum1 silhouette per era boss; plan D21 gives the one suited giant to Sci-Fi, so proposal A's Site Foreman was dropped and Caution has no boss). The five sit on four rigs (tank1, car1 twice, heli1, gun1) and each demands a different answer from the player army, which is what makes the Survival cycle replayable: **a mower you must out-range, a mobile you must shoot up, a wall you must walk round, a broadcast truck you must kill fast, and a gun you must run at.** Every boss carries a mechanic joke and fits `MAX_PARTS` 48 with room to spare. Triangle budgets are set against the measured Ancient references (war_elephant 36 parts: near 55,044 / far 14,168; chariot_archer 43 parts: near 32,088 / far 8,724; plan s5: boss far <= 15 K, vehicle far <= 6 K per vehicle, far mesh judged at the model's own switch distance and at 80 u). Boss waves are every 5th wave in this fixed cycle (the order is also the difficulty ramp); after wave 25 the cycle repeats with the budget scaled by the normal wave formula (enemy budget 2400 + 900 n, the boss's cost comes out of it, the rest is an escort of the same faction). Boss HP and cost are design intent for BALANCE; bosses are judged in Survival, not on the 30-62 % field-win-rate band.

The far-mesh rule (plan s5): the defining feature keeps at least 60 % of its near silhouette pixels at the model's switch distance and at 80 u. The defining feature per boss is below; the `downsample2` lint is the arbiter. Every boss needs a `reveal` clip (or, for the Mower, `rev`) on its rig because the existing `summon_on_death` plays a 1.6 s reveal channel (map 03, `abilities/summon_on_death.js`); the new clips are listed per boss and registered per rig by ANIM-CLIPS with `DEFAULT_META` rows.

## 1. Grand Mower (wave 5, `shed`)

- **Silhouette:** a lawn tractor the size of a house: a three-blade deck as wide as the vehicle, a high seat nobody has sat in, a driver the size of a garden gnome in it, an exhaust stack puffing, a striped mint-and-dark lawn trailing behind as a ground decal. Height class XL (about 8 u with the stack). The deck skirt in team colour is the tint carrier (the faction's mower deck skirt). **Defining feature:** the deck (three blades each at least 2 voxels thick, driven parts) plus the stack.
- **Signature animation:** `deck_spin` (the three blades are `meta.driven` parts at a rev rate that climbs with `rage`), `stripe_trail` (a render decal behind it), `rev` (the pull-cord roar before charging, 0.5 s wind-up as a readable telegraph, the same clip drives `rage`'s roar), `mulch_blast` (the cone shotgun), death: the blades fly off one by one, the seat pops upward, the driver lands in the grass and the engine sighs and conks out.
- **Entrance beat (Survival wave 5; campaign M8 wave 4 off the landing barge):** the pull-cord roars (2 s), the mower mows its first stripe across the spawn zone; announcer `boss_enters` (Brutus: "A MOWER! The size of a BUNGALOW! Please keep all feet inside the garden!"); shot "low rise following the deck", 3.5 s; stinger `mod_stg_engine_rev_brass`; sfx pull-cord, engine roar.
- **Demands and notes:** deck sweep `slash` 36 aoe 3.5 range 6 cd 1.6 plus a `bullet` cone 12 x 6 at 14 u; nothing reaches beyond 14 u, so the whole fight is "how do you spend the 14 u". `rage` below 50 % hp (+50 % damage, +30 % speed) is the clock. Weak to drainpipes and dynamite on the rear, mines at a choke (x1.5 vs vehicles), kiting in open arenas; strong against swarms and packed lines. Clips new on `car1`: `rev`, `strike_mow` (maps to the melee style), `death_back` with part scatter; the rest are the car1 base set.

## 2. Chandelier Gunship (wave 10, `skyclub`)

- **Silhouette:** a hovering rotor with six cardboard toy planes hanging below it like a baby mobile, glowing crystal drops on strings, a small cabin on top with a goldfish bowl canopy (a nod to the Fishbowl Chopper that makes the family relation obvious). Height class XL-A (rotor at about 9 u, shadow blob large). **Defining feature:** the six planes (each at least 3x3 voxels, thick wings) and the rotor disc; the strings are 1 voxel and may vanish in the far mesh (the planes still read as a floating cluster).
- **Signature animation:** `mobile_sway` (the planes swing on their arms with a lag, crystals tinkle), `strafe` (the air AI's banked pass, root pitch), `volley` (four pods fire in sequence, the planes flare), `reveal` (at 50 % hp the mobile shakes violently and four hobby drones drop off the arms, 1.6 s channel), death: the rotor slows, the planes tangle, it spins down and lands in a cloud of confetti (heli spin-crash with floor-fit, then a wreck prop).
- **Entrance beat:** it drifts in from the top edge swaying, the tinkle of crystals arrives before the shadow blob does; announcer `boss_enters` (Cassandra: "I filed a risk assessment on 'mobile'. It came back stamped APPROVED. By the mobile."); shot "low-up" 4 s; stinger `mod_stg_bigband_sting`; sfx rotor flyby, crystal chimes.
- **Demands and notes:** four homing pods (55 dmg, aoe 2.2, ap .6, volley of 4, range 40) and a nose MG sweep; it strafes armour and artillery, so ground-only units (mortar, howitzer, wheelbarrow, Final Notice, tank cannons) cannot answer it. Weak to parasols, MG teams and massed rifles; the AA guarantee in armygen and campaign validation covers it (M9 and Survival). 50 % summon: 4 `hobby_drone` (existing `summon_on_death`, `spawn` param). Clips new on `heli1`: `mobile_sway`, `volley` (maps to the proj clip), `reveal`.

## 3. Grand Teapot (wave 15, `marmalade`; campaign M9 general)

- **Silhouette:** a whole tea service on a tea trolley: a teapot the size of a bandstand as the hull (lid = hatch, lid knob, spout = main gun, handle loop at the rear), a sugar-bowl turret and a milk-jug turret on the shoulders with small MGs, tea-cosy bumps, trolley wheels (tracks hidden under a skirt). Height class XL (about 8 u). **Defining feature:** spout barrel (>= 2x2), handle loop (>= 2 voxels thick, it also tells the player where the rear is), lid knob.
- **Signature animation:** `steam_build` (puffs from the spout and lid that grow while the shell loads; `meta.emitters` render only), `whistle` (the kettle whistle when ready, 0.8 s, the audible telegraph), `fire_spout` (recoil on the barrel, the whole hull rocks back), `reveal` (at 50 % hp the lid pops up on a hinge, four tin_hat_troopers climb out, 1.6 s channel), death: the lid shoots off and sails away, a long sigh of steam, the sugar bowl rolls off.
- **Entrance beat:** the whistle, then steam from the spout, then the trolley squeaks into view; announcer `boss_enters` (Brutus: "A TEAPOT! Tea is served and the tea is ARTILLERY!"); shot "column dolly" 5 s; stinger `mod_stg_brass_stab_boom`; sfx kettle whistle, squeaky wheels, tin bonk.
- **Demands and notes:** shell `explosive` 120 aoe 3.6 ap .6 with a crater, cd 5, range 50, groundOnly; frontal armour .90 (rifles bounce for 1 damage), side .70, rear .35. The answer is the handle side: drainpipes and dynamite from the flank (a rear hit does about six times the damage of a front hit for small arms: 9 x (1 - .35) against the 1-damage floor), mines at a choke (x1.5), chopper rockets. Weak to being walked round. Clips new on `tank1`: `whistle`, `reveal`, `fire_spout` (maps to the launch clip), `death_back` with part scatter.

## 4. Outside-Broadcast Behemoth (wave 20, `briefing`; campaign M5 mini-boss)

- **Silhouette:** a broadcast truck as big as a bus-plus-a-half: a satellite dish the size of a roof (rim = tint carrier), a mast, a light bar, a cable reel unspooling behind, four roof MG turrets in the corners, two rear doors. Height class XL (about 8.5 u with the mast). **Defining feature:** the dish (a disc at least 3 voxels wide on its rim, solid back) and the mast (>= 2x2).
- **Signature animation:** `dish_track` (the dish slews to face the camera as an aim part, `meta.aimParts`), `live_pulse` (every 6 s the light bar flashes and a ring expands: the pin-field pulse), `reveal` (at 50 % hp the rear doors burst open and four Cub Reporters pile out with flash-bulb packs, 1.6 s channel), `cable_drag` (the reel unspools while it moves), death: the dish droops, the light bar flickers off, the doors swing open on an empty truck, a single microphone rolls out.
- **Entrance beat:** the dish unfolds from its travelling position as it rolls on, the red lamp (no text) comes on, a radio squelch arrives with it; announcer `boss_enters` (Brutus: "WE GO LIVE IN FIVE! FOUR! TECHNICAL DIFFICULTIES!"); shot "crane-down" 4 s; stinger `mod_stg_hold_music`; sfx squelch, dish motor, door clang.
- **Demands and notes:** `aura:pinfield` Live Feed r16 pulses every 6 s (+0.5 pin gain); the roof MGs `suppress`; the confuse pulse every 10 s; air and vehicles are pin-immune, so choppers and tanks are the natural counters, while a pure infantry army is pinned flat. Weak to strikes (`call_strike` 45 x 3), rear doors, mines and air. 50 % summon: 4 `cub_reporter`. Clips new on `car1`: `dish_track` (aim), `live_pulse`, `reveal`, `cable_drag` (driven).

## 5. The Final Notice (wave 25, `directorate`; campaign M9 scripted emplacement)

- **Silhouette:** an enormous barrel on a quarry-trolley carriage, the muzzle brake shaped like a rubber stamp, two crew on a platform (lite, 6 parts each) with fingers in their ears, a rear prop-up leg that drops on setup. Height class XL (about 8 u at full elevation). **Defining feature:** the barrel (>= 3x3 voxels, the longest object in the game) and the stamp brake (>= 4x4).
- **Signature animation:** `setup` (4 s: the leg drops, the carriage sags, the barrel elevates; the 2 s ground ring on its target appears at the end of setup), `fire_notice` (huge recoil on `t.z`, the carriage hops, a ring of dust, the stamp brake slams), `reload` (the crew heave a shell the size of a person up the platform), `reveal` is not used, death: the barrel droops, the stamp brake falls off with a "thud", the crew sit down on the platform and the carriage rolls back a metre.
- **Entrance beat:** it does not walk in: it is placed at the far end of the arena and begins `setup`; the first telegraph ring appears on the player's artillery position and the announcer reads the notice; announcer `boss_enters` (Cassandra: "There have been fourteen. This is the fifteenth. I will not read the paragraph."); shot "shell-cam" (muzzle to crater) 5 s; stinger `mod_stg_full_fanfare` (low variant); sfx stamp thump, shell whistle, crater thud.
- **Demands and notes:** notice shell `explosive` 180 aoe 7 with a crater, cd 9, range 110, minRange 35, groundOnly, telegraph ring 2 s, `setup` 4 s; HP 3000 armour .60. It erases packed artillery lines and clumps beyond 35 u; inside 35 u it cannot shoot at all, so a runner group (cub reporters, trolleys, runabouts) or air beats it, and the counter-battery rule is "do not stand in the ring": the telegraph is the whole fairness contract. Position rule: >= 20 u behind its zone front (placement rule in arenas.md). Termination: it is ground-only, so a battle where only it and air units remain ends by the air remnant rule. Clips new on `gun1`: `setup`, `fire_notice` (maps to the launch clip), `reload`, `crew_heave`.

## 6. Cross-checks

- **Five different outlines at 40 px:** a lawn tractor with a deck (wide, low, round blades), a hanging mobile (a rotor over a cluster), a tea service on a trolley (a tall lidded dome with two small domes), a truck with a roof dish (a long box with a big disc), a giant barrel (a long diagonal line). No two share a rig family AND a build: the two car1 bosses differ in proportion (square mower vs long truck), colour (mint vs pink) and moving parts (deck blades vs dish).
- **Five different demands:** out-range (Mower), shoot up (Chandelier), walk round (Teapot), kill fast (Behemoth), run at (Final Notice). The Survival boss fits any player army with at least two of those tools.
- **Campaign membership (UC):** `grand_mower` M8 (W4 off the barge), `broadcast_behemoth` M5 (mini-boss), `grand_teapot` M9 (general), `chandelier_gunship` M9 (90 s arrival), `final_notice` M9 (placed emplacement; the 150 s sluice-gate burst is its shell). No boss is cut by a ladder rung 1-4; `spotter_balloon`'s aura and the Behemoth's pin-field fallback (confuse pulse only) are the cuttable parts.
- **Perf:** the Chandelier and the Behemoth are the two heavy models (near <= 46-48 K, in line with the Ancient elephant's 55 K); LOD switches at the model's own `lodK2` (floor .2 = 25 u); bosses appear one at a time in Survival (never two in the campaign except M9 where the Chandelier arrives while the Teapot holds the crest: tri budget checked by the `view.update` threshold in plan s9).
- **Tone:** deaths are comic (lids, confetti, a sigh); no boss has a face that suffers; the goldfish survives every crash (a wreck-prop gag).
