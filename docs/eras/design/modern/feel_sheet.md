# Modern: feel sheet and judgement of the two proposals (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Era id `modern`, id prefix `mod_`, currency **requisitions** (`rq`, through `fmtCost`; Ancient hoplite = 100), HUD tag `[data-era=modern]`. Written against plan.md v3.1 (s1, s4, s5, s7, s8, s10, s11, s13) and the code (checks in section 13). It needs only SIM modules #1-#16 (the Modern E-FREEZE prefix: M0 M1 M3 M2 M2b M10 M6a M7 M12 M14 M13 M15 M17e M8 M9 M11); it never touches regenerating shields, cloak, EMP, blink, hover, mechs or the suited giant. Companion files in this folder: factions.md, rosters.md (34 units, contract checks, counters), boss_table.md, arenas.md (12 arenas plus the **binding nine-mission ladder**), props.md (38), puzzles.md (6).

## 1. One-line conceit

A tabletop war fought with toy-box combined arms in retro-appliance pastels (a teapot with a cannon, a lunchbox that carries troops, a filing cabinet that shells you from three streets away), run from a briefing room by people who have never met, and called live over headsets that nobody trained the three commentators to use.

## 2. Tempo targets (the "Pop": fast, long-reach, windowed; fights are decided by who is reloading, who is pinned and who is standing in the wrong face of a tank)

The Ancient feel is a fast javelin ballet; Medieval is a slow moshpit with a flag on top; **Modern is a firefight with a clock in it**: every strong thing has a visible window (a reload, a setup, an arming delay, a strike whistle) and the game is won by being there during the window.

## 3. Camera defaults

The default camera rig is **unchanged** (the angles and field of view in `cameras.js`; `game.js` frames the arena at `worldSize x 0.55`, which fits the 64 u medium gap at the default zoom). Ancient pixels stay bit-identical (G8); proposal B's lowered pitch (0.75), tilt-shift vignette and shutter frame, and proposal A's +12 % pull-back were dropped: plan s6 gives the camera only `CameraRig.shot(spec)`. What changes is what the camera is aimed at: tall things (drawers, rotor discs, pushpins above heads, ground rings). Era shot vocabulary for `CameraRig.shot` (real time, sim at 0.5x, skippable with Esc or a click, Reduce Motion = cut, 3-6 s each): **shell-cam** (muzzle to crater), **heli-cam** (side follow at altitude), **column dolly** (low tracking along a convoy), **drone-top** (straight-down pan), **crane-down** (high to low over a gantry), **low-up** (low angle tilting to a giant), **orbit** (around a tower), **radio push-in** (slow punch on a commentator caption). Mission openers never cover the first volley.

## 4. Signature mechanic and its on-screen tell: THE PIN (suppression against cover)

Bullets that land near a unit fill an invisible meter; a full meter pins it: speed x0.5, spread x1.8, no advancing, morale drain, fire rate halved. Pins last 2.5 s after the last near-miss, so the answer to a pin is a flanker or a shell, never "wait it out"; a second pin inside 5 s fills at half rate (diminishing returns, no permanent lock); tanks, air and drones are pin-immune (`pin_immune`); crews and infantry are not. **Only units with a `suppress` row pin**: the Tripod MG Team (strong), the sharpshooter (light), the two pin-field auras (Spokesperson, Behemoth) and a token amount on the vehicle guns, so M1 and M2 are pin-free and M3 is where the player first sees it.

**The tell, readable from 60 u in a 600-unit battle:** (1) a **ground ring of amber and black segments** (plain chevrons, never three bands) that winds like a clock as the meter fills; (2) when it closes, a **red pushpin drops onto the unit's head with a "thunk"** and stays as a small tack mark until the pin ends; (3) the unit drops into the `pinned_crouch` clip with the helmet jolting down, dust puffs at its boots; (4) near-miss "snap" streaks and a rising `bullet_snap` sound near the camera; (5) the kill-feed verb "pinned" and, on the selection card, a PIN meter beside the AMMO pips. The pushpin is also the campaign map's pin, the Codex bookmark and the achievement icon: the whole chrome is built from the same object.

Secondary tells, each a read-at-a-glance event (CU5 teaches them one per mission): **AMMO** (pips under every shooter, 3-8 ticks; kneel and a readable reload clack; a RELOAD ring) ; **COVER** (four cyan corner brackets on the prop and a cyan helmet pip; "x0.5" on the first absorbed hit; a red "flanked" tick when the cover no longer faces the shooter); **ARMOUR** (a grey "tink" with a white spark and a "1" on the front face, an orange BONK flash and a rear-arc wedge on the damage ring for side and rear; hull and turret arrows on the selection card); **SHELL** (a red ground ring and a rising whistle for 2.2 s before every indirect hit, minimum-range hatching around artillery, craters that stay); **MINE** (a blinking amber light on a yellow tape ring, visible to its owner; the enemy sees the ring for 0.4 s as it springs); **AIR** (shadow blob that shrinks with altitude, banking on `vx`, a dotted lock-on line from every homing missile, a "GROUND ONLY" icon on artillery); **HEAL vs REPAIR** (a mint sparkle ring for organics (no cross, no plus sign), a spanner glint for machines).

## 5. Sound palette: poppy, clacky, crackly

Guns are 0.15-0.35 s cap-gun pops with a cork-and-tin edge and short tails, never a movie boom; reloads, UI and clips are clacks (typewriter, stamp, stapler, bolt); everything the commentators say, and every engine and rotor bed, arrives with a crackle (radio squelch before each commentator line). Forbidden timbres: orchestral trailer hits, real-siren recordings, screaming, anything that sounds like footage. Hot families (AU0 and the AU1 hunt; realism processing = pops cut to 0.15-0.35 s, shortened tails, less low end): `rifle_pop`, `mg_stutter` (3-round typewriter rattle, max 5 voices), `sniper_crack` (plus a bolt clack; the red dot is silent so the crack is the surprise), `bullet_snap` (the near-miss that teaches pins by ear), `reload_clack` (readable at 35 u: the tell that a unit is vulnerable), `cannon_bonk` (tin bonk over a low thump), `shell_whistle` (rising, then a hush before the blast), `blast_sml/med/lrg` (balloon pop with a cymbal tail, never a film explosion), `plink_tink` (the era's laugh sound, louder than a rifle pop on armour), `engine_tread_bed` (aggregate bed, clattery sewing-machine feel), `rotor_bed` (audible overhead before visible), `mine_blip_boing`, `helmet_bonk` (the knock-out: ring, dazed ding, no scream; machines pop a lid and sigh steam). UI: rubber stamp thud, stapler tick, typewriter ding, pen click. Music (seven tracks, CC0 first, CC BY with credit, synth fallback, never a trailer orchestra; battle tracks `loop:true` or a passing seam check): menu "Briefing Room Lounge" 96 bpm (vibraphone, upright bass, brushed kit, organ swells); map bed "On Hold" 84 (elevator lounge, phone clicks, voice-less synth melody: the best joke in the sound list); battle low "The Quiet Before the Memo" 108 (muted pizzicato, rim clicks, finger snaps); battle mid "Combined Arms Swing" 124 (funk drums, brass stabs, baritone sax riff); battle high "Danger Close (Respectfully)" 148 (breakbeat snare, fuzz guitar, brass blasts); victory "Stamp of Approval" 104 (big-band fanfare ending on a typewriter ding); defeat "Request Denied" 72 (muted trombone, slow typewriter).

## 6. Silhouette language: a retro-appliance showroom

Everything is a household or office object with a weapon bolted on, drawn round, handled and lidded: **teapots, lunchboxes, biscuit tins, filing cabinets, goldfish bowls, lawn tractors, trolleys, toast racks, telephoto lenses, umbrellas, lecterns**. Per faction (factions.md): Marmalade ROUND and LIDDED (kitchenware on wheels); Directorate TALL and DRAWERED (cabinets, antennas, paper fans); Paper Plane Flying Club DISCS and DANGLES (rotors, balloons, strings); Caution Tape SLABBED and CHEVRONED (blades, plywood, hard hats); Garden Shed WIRY and POTTED (terracotta, hoses, barrows); Briefing Room Brigade DISHED and BELTED (satellite dishes, lenses, ammo belts). The skyline against Ancient's round shields and Medieval's vertical poles is **lids, handles and discs**. Far-mesh rules every model respects (lint: `downsample2`): barrels, antennas, lens hoods, drainpipes and umbrella tubes are at least 2x2 voxels; antenna bulbs and dynamite sparks at least 2x2; belts and cloth at least 3 wide; rotor discs dense or a far blur part; the goldfish bowl at least 2 voxels thick; balloon envelope solid. Team tint sits where the eye goes (the tint carrier in factions.md: helmet band, turret ring, tab dividers, drawer fronts, rotor rim, balloon segments, beacon lamp and hat brim, hose coil and deck skirt, dish rim and lectern front). 19 of the 34 units are non-hum1 silhouettes (floor 15); the five bosses are five different outlines and none is a scaled soldier.

## 7. Humour engines (three; Ancient's bureaucratic understatement is NOT primary; forms and stamps are chrome, not the joke)

**E2 Toy-Box Literalism** (a war fought with household objects and narrated with total seriousness; "what is it for?"; Plato-led). Plato: "The Teapot Heavy has not poured. It has only aimed. I find that more menacing." Brutus: "FLOWERPOT HELMET takes a LIGHT hit! The sprig is fine! THE SPRIG IS FINE!" Plato: "A trolley with a plank. **E3 The Convoy Is Late** (logistics slapstick: traffic, detours, wrong exits, cows, parking, deliveries, refuelling; Cassandra-led doom about mundane delays). Brutus: "A TANK COLUMN stuck behind a COW! The cow has right of way! The cow has always had right of way!" Cassandra: "The convoy will take the wrong exit. The third time. I drew them a map. Why not Ancient again: Ancient's joke is a form filed in silence while something terrible happens; Modern's jokes are loud (E1), literal about objects (E2) or about things being late, lost or in the wrong lane (E3). The intern says this is the right century. He has pressed 'hold' on the next one.").

## 9. What this era must NEVER feel like

1. Ancient with arrows renamed to bullets: infantry standing in a line trading volleys with nobody ducking. (Fingerprint: engagement distance, cover occupancy, indirect share.)
2. A grim, realistic war game: no camo, no national palettes, no flags, no real kit, no blood, no pain, no screaming, no stoic pathos; units are knocked out, machines pop lids.
3. A grey-brown palette: this is a saturated-pastel plastic toy box (the faction primaries are pastels and near-blacks by measurement, factions.md).
4. A twitch aim test or a camping game: every long-range killer has a visible tell and a window (the red dot, the reload, the setup); the sniper has a minimum range and a 5.5 s cycle.
5. A stat race or a list of vehicle skins: a tank is not "a hoplite with more hit points", it has faces; every vehicle has a silhouette you can name at 40 px (teapot, lunchbox, filing cabinet, goldfish bowl, lawn tractor).
6. Sci-Fi-lite: no energy weapons, shields, cloak, hover, EMP, mechs or aliens; gadgets are mundane (radios, drones that drop parcels, smoke).
7. A trailer: no orchestra, no slow-mo for its own sake.
8. Bureaucracy: that is Ancient's trick; here forms are chrome and the jokes are loud, literal or late.
9. A pin stalemate: pins decay in 2.5 s, diminishing returns, the stalemate watchdog still applies.

## 11. Binding decisions (what was taken, dropped, fixed)

**Taken from A:** the retro-appliance silhouette language and 32 of its 34 units (Teapot Heavy, Lunchbox APC, Filing-Cabinet Howitzer, Fishbowl Chopper, Spotter Balloon, Trolley Rammer, Tripod MG, Long-Lens Sharpshooter, the mower family and the rest, re-balanced; only the Site Foreman and the Leaf-Blower Hover were dropped), the five-boss idea (re-cast as five non-hum1 outlines with five different demands), the Pin tell as a ground ring, the faction lore jokes, the hot sound families (13), the chrome vocabulary (corkboard, pushpins, headset), 11 of its 12 arenas (Building Site gave way to B's Roadworks) and 35 of its 38 props, the Plato-muted and helicopter arc, the `mod_red_tape` mutator, the star-helper vocabulary, the `cover` and `lay_mine` abilities as written, puzzle ideas 1, 2 and 6.
**Taken from B:** the ladder discipline (one new tell per mission, star 3 tests an earlier mechanic, the composition question, the blind-bot column), reload as the one idea of mission 1, the pushpin that drops on a full pin, the PLINK/BONK vocabulary for armour faces, the window numbers (reload 3 s, setup 1.2-4 s, arming delay, minimum ranges), the asserted counter matrix, the fingerprint targets (engagement distance, ranged share, cover occupancy), the Final Notice as a boss and the Drainpipe Launcher's role (B's Tube Trooper, the AT infantry A lacked), the Roadworks arena and the cow, the logistics-slapstick engine, the teaching beats and lesson detectors tied to scout codes, the boarding-pass arrival card and the Parcel payoff, `mod_airmail` as the second mutator, puzzle ideas 3, 4 and 5 and their titles.
**Fixed because infeasible or off-plan (each verified against the plan or the code):**
1. **Suited giant (A's Site Foreman).** Plan D21 allows at most one suited giant (hum1 x2.4); both Sci-Fi proposals use it (`hauler_suit`, `loader_suit`). Dropped. The plan also asks for one non-scaled-hum1 silhouette per era boss, so all five Modern bosses are tank1, car1, heli1 and gun1 outlines (boss_table.md). Caution has no boss.
2. **Hover (A's Leaf-Blower Hover).** Hover is Sci-Fi's NEVER-cut headline; a hovercraft in Modern is Sci-Fi-lite. Dropped (it also paid for the 34-unit count); the gust-shove idea is lost, `kb` on the Ride-On Mower and the Trolley Rammer remains.
3. **quad1 `mule` species and the resupply aura (B's Pack Mule).** A new species costs about +7 clips, animals are other eras' rigs, and a reload multiplier is not a `banner` parameter. Dropped.
4. **Teapot rear-vent fire cone (A).** No cone projectile and no rear-conditioned area hook in M0..M19; steam is a render effect on the reload (PROJ_FX/emitter row).
5. **Mower 160-degree sweep (A).** `melee.cleave` is declared but never read (map 02); the sweep is a short-range area spray (`slash` aoe 1.8-3.5, range 4.5-6) that reads as a sweep.
6. **Two-threshold summons at 66 % and 33 % (A's Chandelier).** `summon_on_death` fires once, at 50 % hp, when three enemies are within 5 u, 25 s after first contact, or on death (read in `abilities/summon_on_death.js`; the bosses set `onContact` high and SIM adds an `hpOnly` flag in the same M13 param family); the Chandelier, Grand Teapot and Behemoth each get that single trigger and need a `reveal` clip on their rig (boss_table.md). B's `lift_whirly` landing-on-contact is cut.
7. **Mine defusing and plough mine clearing (A).** M11 specifies lay, arm delay, owner, trigger; nothing clears a mine. The Dozer Plough is `mine_immune` (a flag on the trigger rule); the Sapper does not defuse.
8. **`spot` aura (A's Spotter Balloon, accuracy x0.6 / range +10 %).** `aura:banner` carries {dmg, moraleRate, lossMul, radius}; the Balloon gives +8 % damage in 16 u (cuttable aura, never the unit, per plan s13 rung 5).
9. **Spokesperson taunt (A).** Nothing but `cluck` sets TAUNT; "No Comment" is `cc_field:confuse`, which also fits the joke. The pin-field aura is a new effect VALUE inside the existing `aura` id (map 02 seam 26: new values, not new ids); fallback if SIM declines: the confuse pulse alone.
10. **Camera (B).** Lowered pitch, tilt-shift and a shutter frame move Ancient pixels (G8) and are not in plan s6; dropped.
11. **Theme strings.** `modern_industrial` is 17 characters; `sanitizeEnv` slices `env.theme` to 16 (`world/arena.js:167`). Themes are `mod_city mod_field mod_trench mod_industrial mod_harbour mod_air mod_desert` (all <= 16).
12. **The dam.** One global water plane per arena (map 05): A's reservoir upstream plus dry spillway below cannot exist. The reservoir is **drained for maintenance** (a dry basin, a joke sign), the river runs downstream at low level.
13. **Materials.** A's `duckboard`, `trench_mud`, `stubble`, `felt_green` and B's `churned_mud`, `gravel`, `tarmac`, `paint_yellow` duplicate shipped `planks`, `mud`, `savanna`, `grass`; seven new materials only (arenas.md).
14. **Colours.** Eleven of the twelve primaries in the two proposals sat between 5.6 and 13.4 CIEDE2000 from an Ancient or Medieval primary (marmalade vs Marrowby 5.6, teal vs Egyptians 6.8, cobalt vs Hellenes 6.5, lime vs Yeomen 6.0-8.6, silver vs Gatehouse 9.0, violet vs Persians 10.5, terracotta vs Romans 12.2, hazard yellow vs Mythic 13.4); the twelfth, B's cyan (17.1), is the Sci-Fi accent family; the free regions of the wheel are pastels and near-blacks, which is also what makes Modern look like Modern (factions.md has the measured table).
15. **Zones.** The default medium gap is 32.6 u, which puts a 28 u rifle in range at tick 0; this era overrides zone placement (section 2).
16. **Pack-in features cut or flagged.** `spawnProp` sandbag walls (B) cut, mines only for the Sapper; smoke (Signal Officer) is a cuttable rung-5 secondary and no star or mission depends on it; the spinning radar dish and the drawer animation need the `meta.driven` / prop `spin` table and degrade to static.
17. **Rig budget.** `car1` carries five units (runabout, APC, mower, two bosses) with five distinct builders (the ER3 silhouette hash must see them as different); `gun1` carries five (mortar, howitzer, tripod, wheelbarrow, Final Notice); no new rig family is requested beyond plan s5.
