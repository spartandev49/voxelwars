# Modern campaign: the nine missions (binding design, DESIGN-ERA-MODERN part 2)

Status: binding for CAMPAIGN-MOD (`missions.json`), COMEDY-MOD, AUDIO, UI (teaching data) and the MS lint. It expands the **binding ladder** of `arenas.md` section 3 (mission ids, titles, objectives, arenas and seeds, rosters, budgets, enemy composition, taught mechanic, star 3, set-piece id, reward) into full mission blocks, and uses only unit and faction ids from `rosters.md` / `factions.md` (34 units, six factions: `marmalade directorate skyclub caution briefing shed`), arena ids from `arenas.md` (12) and prop ids from `props.md` (38). Unit, arena and prop ids are therefore **final**, not provisional (part 1 was on disk before this file was written; proposal A/B ids that did not survive the merge, such as `flowerpot_rifleman`, `field_medic`, `site_foreman`, `leafblower_hover`, are not used). Where this document differs from the ladder or the roster, section 0.1 lists the difference and the reason. Written against plan.md v3.1 sections 4 (modules), 5 (rigs), 8 (MS schema and curve rules), 10, 11, 13.

Synthesis: the ladder, arena set, rosters and teaching order are part 1's (B's discipline: one new tell per mission, star 3 tests an earlier mechanic, a blind-bot column; A's skin: the retro-appliance factions, the set-pieces and the Plato-muted / helicopter arc). Star-3 wording, set-piece lines, three-voice copy, teaching beats beyond the nine binding ones, the Parcel thread and the Plato-muted progression are this document's.

Modern needs only modules #1..#16 (E-FREEZE prefix: M0 M1 M3 M2 M2b M10 M6a M7 M12 M14 M13 M15 M17e M8 M9 M11). It never touches M4 shields, M5 cloak, M6b EMP, blink or hover.

## 0. Conventions used in every block

### 0.1 Differences from the ladder and the roster files (each with its reason)

1. **Brutus's one-caps rule.** Two binding teaching-beat texts of `arenas.md` 3.4 carry several ALL-CAPS words (`mod_b_window`: "A TROLLEY! ... DO NOT ..."; `mod_b_pin`: "A PUSHPIN! He is STUCK ..."). Ids, triggers, voices and hints are kept; the texts are reduced to one emphasis word per line (the voice law of `spec/humor.md` s2): `mod_b_window` "A TROLLEY! Fast and wobbly! Bring a runabout and do not stand in a row!"; `mod_b_pin` "A PUSHPIN! He is stuck to the map! He cannot advance! He cannot aim!". The `mod_b_window` hint no longer says "pin" (pin is taught in M3, two missions later): "Trolleys outrun bullets. Shoot the front rank, or meet them with wheels." Every other beat text is verbatim.
2. **Star 3 of M9** is `usedAll([cover, pin, armour, shells, mines, air, repair], 3)` exactly as the ladder says; `reload` is left out on purpose (every shooter reloads, so it would be free) and the curve audit therefore says "seven of the eight taught mechanics".
3. **The god power Minefield, Gift Wrapped** (slot 3, `god_powers.md`) is disabled by mission rule in M1 to M6 (`powers.disable`, request R4): mines are taught in M6 and a fourteen-mine disc in M2 would be an untaught mechanic; it is enabled from M7. The shared Tea Break (heal and repair) is NOT disabled in M1 to M7 (it is a power with its own tutorial caption, not a unit lesson); M8 states that the unit healers split people and machines and the power does not.
4. **Cameos before their lesson** (the only exceptions to "an untaught mechanic is absent or inert", each declared in the mission's `teaching.exceptions`): the scripted unarmed Fishbowl Chopper flyover in M6 (air layer, passive, untargetable, request R3); the Dynamite Thrower in M3 (an area thrower with a 5 u minimum range, no `groundOnly` and no crater: the ladder's M3 enemy and the "grenades punish clumping" foil, not the M5 lesson); the cover props of the parade yard in M1 (previewed, never used by the fight, section 0.3). Nothing in M1 or M2 carries a `suppress` amount above .1 (`rosters.md`: the runabout's token .04 cannot fill a meter alone; the first real pin source is the M3 Tripod).
5. **Plus-items** (rewards beyond the ladder's list) are marked `+`; every ladder reward appears unchanged. Quick-unlock plus-items name the three Quick-only arenas (`mod_desert_outpost`, `mod_roadworks`, `mod_garden_centre`) and are valid only if Quick locks arenas (CU11 reward ledger; if it does not, the codex page of the same mission replaces them, no hollow reward).
6. **Currency** is requisitions (`rq`, `fmtCost`). **Seeds and times** are the ladder's (hours 7 to 21, weather per row).
7. **Reference armies** are sketches against the `rosters.md` costs (85 to 100 percent of budget, <= 16 types); CAMPAIGN-MOD fixes them against `stats.js` after BALANCE.
8. **Trigger of `mod_b_window`** is `first_charge` (the first unit with a charge factor starts its run, i.e. the trolley wave), not the ladder's `first_contact`: `first_contact` already fires at about 9 s when the first peas land and drives `b_powers`; the text, voice and hint are the ladder's (with the caps fix of item 1).
9. **Names**: factions are written in prose as the Marmalade Motor Pool, the Directorate, the Paper Plane Flying Club (id `skyclub`), the Caution Tape Company, the Briefing Room Brigade and the Garden Shed Auxiliary; data uses the ids.

### 0.2 Schema choices (MS fields this document defines once; identical to the Medieval pack so the lint has one meaning)

- **par**: `{type:'cost'|'time'|'none', value}`. `cost` = the spend ceiling of the thrift star (briefing shows "Spend at most X"); `time` = the median clear time of the `reference` army, shown on briefing and results as "Par 150 s"; `none` where the mission is a fixed-duration hold or a wave count. The ladder's par column (2,250 / 3,200 / 4,500 / 6,400 / 7,200 / 7,600 / 6,000 / 8,000 / none) is the **cost** the reference army spends; it is stored as `par {type:'cost', value}` and displayed as "Reference army: 3,200 rq"; only M1 uses it as a star.
- **Star 1** = win. **Star 2** = `{id:'half', helper:'aliveCostFrac', args:[0.5]}` (the Ancient generic test: at least half the army by cost alive at the end; summoned and bailed-out units are excluded from both numbers). **Star 3** = the mission's own test.
- **Closed vocabulary used**: `thrift(par)`, `noLoss(defs)`, `usedMechanic(id, n)`. In the vocabulary but not needed: `shieldsBroken`, `suppressed`, `underTime`, `keptAlive`, `gateBrokenBy`, `vipSurvived`, `killedFirst`.
- **Helpers proposed (4 of the allowed 6; names already in `arenas.md` 3.6 except `aliveCostFrac`, which the Medieval pack proposes with the same meaning)**: `aliveCostFrac(f)` (star 2); `coverFracAtLeast(f)` (share of the player's own unit-seconds with `inCover`, counted from battle start to the last tick); `hitsWhileReloadingAtMost(n)` (event `reload_hit_taken`: damage events taken by the player's units while the target unit is in its reload state; the opposite direction of the `reload` counter below); `usedAll(ids, n)` (every listed mechanic id has counter >= n). `coreHpAtLeast` and `noFriendlyFire` from the `arenas.md` list are NOT used by any star here and are not registered for Modern (the M6 results screen shows the core's remaining hit points as a flourish line, not a star).
- **`usedMechanic` ids** and the counter `n` counts (each is a generic `MissionTracker` counter that reads sim events; the same counters feed the achievements in `mutators_achievements.md`):

| mechanic id | taught in | counter |
|---|---|---|
| `reload` | M1 | `reload_hit`: damage events dealt by the player's side to an enemy unit whose reload clip is running |
| `cover` | M2 | `cover_hit`: damage events on the player's units that `lowCover` halved |
| `pin` | M3 | `pin_applied`: distinct enemy units that reached a full pin (first time per unit) |
| `armour` | M4 | `armour_flank_hit`: damage events on an enemy vehicle or armoured unit whose resolved face is side or rear |
| `shells` | M5 | `shell_hit`: indirect-fire damage events (`arc:'high'`, `call_strike`) that hurt at least one enemy; craters made are a second counter |
| `mines` | M6 | `mine_trigger`: `hazard_trigger` events of `mine_*` kinds that hit at least one enemy |
| `air` | M7 | `air_kill`: kills of air-layer units by the player's side |
| `repair` | M8 | `heal_pulse` casts that healed or repaired at least one unit (events, not hit points; the hit-point total feeds `modHealedHp`) |

- **Set-piece package** = `{id, sim event, camera shot {from, to, hold, ease}, announcer slot, three sample lines, stinger kind, sfx[]}`. Slots are the ladder's (`campaign_<missionId>/start`, `hold_hill/mid`, `capture/first`, `escort/mid`, `destroy/complete`, `defend_core/low`, `mission_start`, `boss_enters`, `finale`), priority 5, `bypassAlternation`. Shots run in real time while the sim runs at 0.5x; possession, aim, photo mode and cinematic mode defer the shot; Esc or click skips it; Reduce Motion turns every dolly into a cut. Camera anchors are named (`firing_line`, `tower`, markers); heights are in units above ground. Stinger kinds (AUDIO taxonomy): `comic`, `hit`, `swell`, `dread`, `fanfare`; each ducks music 6 dB for 700 ms; only the M9 finale fanfare and the Final Notice bypass the limiter.
- **Voice rules for every line below**: Brutus = one ALL-CAPS emphasis word per line (a place or title may add a second), exclamation marks; Plato = ends on a question or a measured understatement; Cassandra = short flat sentences, "I said". Briefing lines are the three-voice opener; set-piece lines are the live announcer lines for the moment. **Plato's briefing, booth and set-piece lines carry the muted-speaker glyph and the label "PLATO (MUTED)" in M1 to M8** (they stay fully legible; the label never explains itself); in M9 the label is simply "PLATO" (see `humour.md` 3).
- **Teaching beats** use CU5 fields; `basics:true` beats belong to the once-ever layer (`seen.basics`, the same five ids as every era) and are skipped for a returning player; every other beat is an era beat (`seen.beats[id]`, once per era). Modern beat ids are `mod_*`; the nine first-sight beats of the ladder (`mod_b_*`) keep their ids.
- **Marker coordinates** are in units on the x axis (the player deploys at -x, the enemy at +x unless stated); |x|,|z| <= 32 (small), 48 (medium), 64 (large).
- **Enemy `groups` fields**: `{defId, n, at{x,z}, order advance|hold, squad, formation}`; "hired" groups belong to another faction by contract (`arenas.md` 3).

### 0.3 Constraints this ladder puts on rosters, props and the sim (the `untaught` lint; checked by CAMPAIGN-MOD and BALANCE)

The MS lint gets one new rule, `untaught(mission)`: a def carrying a field of mechanic X must not appear before the mission that teaches X, unless the mission lists it in `teaching.exceptions`. Field sets: `pin` = any `suppress` row with `amt` > 0.1 or `aura:pinfield`; `cover` = a `cover:'low'` prop inside the playfield; `armour` = `armorFace`; `shells` = `arc:'high'`, `minRange`, `crater` or `call_strike`; `mines` = `lay_mine` or a `mine_*` hazard; `air` = layer `air`, `homing` air-only weapons or a visible `groundOnly` icon with an air unit on the field; `repair` = `heal_pulse`; `reload` = none (every shooter has it).

| mission | what is absent or inert by construction |
|---|---|
| M1 | cover is **previewed, not taught**: `arenas.md` 2.1 puts hay rows behind the enemy zone and a sandbag demo bay on each flank, >= 20 u off the lanes, and cover-seeking AI looks only 8 u around itself, so nobody fights behind them (declared in M1 `teaching.exceptions`; before M9 lands, in the plumbing slice, the props are inert). No armour faces (the runabout is flat .15), no pin (runabout and peashooter `suppress` below .1), no shell, mine, air or repair. The trolleys arrive as a scripted wave (existing `ScriptedWaves`), not as a held group released by an event, so M1 needs no M14 script event |
| M2 | cover is the lesson; the runabouts are flat armour; still no pin above the token amount, no `groundOnly` |
| M3 | pin is the lesson; the dynamite thrower is the declared exception; no armour faces, no `groundOnly`, no mine |
| M4 | armour faces are the lesson; the Biscuit-Tin Tank's cannon carries `groundOnly`, which is inert here (no air unit on the field in M1 to M5) and is first shown with its icon in M7 |
| M5 | shells are the lesson; no mine, no air, no repair |
| M6 | mines are the lesson; the scripted chopper flyover is the declared exception |
| M7 | air is the lesson; no repair pulse in the player roster |
| M8 | repair/heal is the lesson; the Grand Mower's `rage` is an existing ability |
| M9 | nothing new |
