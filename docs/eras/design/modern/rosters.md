# Modern: the final 34-unit roster (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Currency requisitions (`rq`; Ancient hoplite = 100). All numbers are design intent for BALANCE and are never measured here; directions of counters are written BEFORE measuring (plan ER7: asserted rows need >= 200 battles per pair, Wilson 95 % lower bound >= 55 %, point >= 65 %, mechanic-off control). Ids are unique within the era and distinct from the 43 Ancient ids (checked against `src/content/era_ancient/stats.js` by script), from the Medieval roster and from every id in both Sci-Fi proposals (grep, no hit); every id is lower_snake and immutable once shipped. The roster merges proposal A (32 of its 34 units) with B (the Final Notice and the Drainpipe Launcher, B's Tube Trooper) under the fixes in feel_sheet.md section 11: no suited giant, no hover, no animals, no unit that needs a mechanic outside plan s4.

Legend. **Role** (sim role): melee ranged cavalry siege support hero monster beast swarm; vehicles are the role plus the tag `vehicle` (map 02 seam 16), air is `ranged`/`swarm`/`support` plus the tag `air`. **Height class**: S under 2.0 u, M 2.0-3.0 u (a hum1 soldier is about 2.3 u), T 3.0-4.5 u (tanks, antennas, tall packs), L 4.5-7 u, XL over 7 u; an `A` marks the air layer (altitude 3-9 u on top). **HP class**: XS <= 70, S 71-140, M 141-250, L 251-450, XL 451-900, XXL over 900. **Speed class**: crawl <= 1.5 u/s, slow 1.6-2.2, mid 2.3-3.0, fast 3.1-4.2, rush 4.3-6.5 (wheels, reporters, drones), air (flyers; the u/s is in section 3). **Cost class**: S <= 100, M 101-180, L 181-300, XL over 300. **Model recipe**: BP = hum1 blueprint through `compileSoldier`; BB = bespoke builder on the `LG/SLG` toolkit (`era_modern/beasts/*.js`); lite crew = 6 parts per crew member (decision D12). **Tracer** T = built early: all M1 plumbing-slice units plus one unit per rig family (hum1 rifle and smg, car1, tank1, gun1, heli1, drone1, ram1, and the lens-style hitscan spike). `*` after an id = bespoke silhouette (non-hum1 rig). `[B]` = survival boss, `[H]` = hero.

Engines: **E1** Live From The Front (Technical Difficulties); **E2** Toy-Box Literalism; **E3** The Convoy Is Late (feel_sheet.md section 7). Bark roles are the nine existing roles of `humor/barks.js`; the vehicle, air, drone, crew and officer sets that are silent today are listed in section 7. First-mission codes follow the binding ladder in arenas.md section 3 (an unmarked code is the player's roster; "(enemy)" is the enemy roster).

## 7. Barks, composition and termination

**Bark roles:** the nine existing roles plus the class sets the Modern roles need (UC: no silent role). Vehicle: "Engine noises!", "Is that my rear?"; air: "Rotor, rotor, rotor!", "Altitude is a state of mind."; drone: "Piloted remotely. Panicking locally."; crew: "Loading! Loading! Hold your ears!"; officer: "Reading my notes!". Status barks: pinned "Not lifting my head!", reloading "Click! Hold on! Click!", mine "Was that tape?", flanked "Why is the wall behind me?". Death lines: units are knocked out ("My hat! Somebody get my hat!"), machines sigh ("Tea is... served."); no pleading, family or pain.

**Armygen and AA guarantee.** Every Modern faction pool contains weapons that hit air (rifles, SMGs, MGs; Skyclub brings parasols). `groundOnly` units are mortar, howitzer, wheelbarrow, Final Notice, tank cannons and the drainpipe; a player army of only those cannot end a battle against drones, so the AA guarantee in armygen AND in campaign validation applies (every mission with air has an air-capable weapon on the other side), and the termination rule for unhittable remnants (M7) is exercised by an all-ground player army in M7's validation run. The parasol is air-only (cannot hit ground); an enemy of parasols alone cannot lose its way out, so the watchdog's "advance" phase handles it and the mission scripts never leave one alone.
