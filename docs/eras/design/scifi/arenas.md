# Sci-Fi: twelve arenas and the binding mission ladder (synthesizer part 1)

Verified against the code: sizes are `small 128 / medium 192 / large 256` cells at 0.5 u per cell = **64 / 96 / 128 u** (`world/arena.js SIZES`; nav cells are 1 u), `MAX_H` 120 steps, **one global water plane per arena** (a `lava` boolean on that plane, so an arena has water OR lava; deep water is impassable to ground units, shallow costs x1.7), props <= 1500, 16 materials exist (`grass dirt sand stone snow mud marble lava planks brick cobble ash sandstone savanna moss blood`), weathers `clear cloudy rain storm snow sandstorm fog`, `sanitizeEnv` slices `env.theme` to **16 characters**, one height per cell (no overpasses, tunnels or interiors: every "interior" below is an open-top level with walls as props). Default zones are 0.22 W wide centred at +-0.28 W, so the default medium gap is 32.6 u, which would put a 22 u rifle in range after one step: this era overrides zone placement per recipe (section 1). The Ancient `random` preset picks from a fixed list of 8 Ancient recipes; adding picks would change it, so Sci-Fi gets its own `sf_random` over its 12 recipes.

## 1. Conventions that apply to all twelve

- **Ids** are `sf_<name>` (a superset of the AR2 prefix list; Ancient recipe ids are bare, so a prefix removes any chance of a registry collision). `env.era = 'scifi'`. **Theme vocabulary** (one canonical set, plan W7, all <= 16 characters): `sf_moon sf_lava sf_industrial sf_crash sf_retail sf_neon sf_jungle sf_hive sf_station sf_ice sf_crystal sf_dome`. Audio ambience and step tables are keyed by these themes and by the biomes in the table (new rows: `step_regolith`, `step_hull`, `step_neon`, `step_moss`, `step_ice`, `step_goo`).
- **Materials.** Nine appended (REGISTRY assigns the ids after Modern's 16-22, frozen once shipped): `regolith basalt ice hull_plate neon_pad resin goo glow_moss tile_floor` with speeds and emissive flags in props.md section 5. Duplicates of shipped materials (`snow`, `moss`, `stone`, `sand`) are not added; `lava` is reused for the lava world.
- **Liquids.** The single water plane per arena is the only liquid; its **look** comes from `THEME_LOOK` (R7): lava (orange, existing), coolant (teal, shallow), acid river (lime, a deep channel with one shallow ford), bile (green, shallow), void (black, deep, along the station rim), rift (violet, deep), pond (clear, shallow). If R7 cannot tint the plane, the fallback is the default water colour and every jump still works (the jokes survive, the tint does not).
- **Env.** `sky` presets (W4 whitelist, with `gravity` and `era`): `space_planet`, `ember`, `teal_strip`, `dusk_smoke`, `retail_bright`, `neon_night`, `jungle_dusk`, `cavern_open`, `space_ring`, `aurora`, `violet`, `noon_calm`. **Gravity** is the multiplier on every gravity use (arc landing tested at x0.4, solved range capped at `range`): moon x0.4, station x0.6, everything else x1.0. New weather rows: `spores` (jungle, hive), `neon_rain` (neon city), `ember_ion` (lava, reactor).
- **Scale and tempo (plan W11).** Foot 2.4-3.6 u/s, hover/bikes/buggies/drones 4.0-6.0, mechs/crawlers 1.4-2.4, air 4.2-5.5. Ranges: melee 1.4-2.4, rifle 22, rivet 24, valet 20, Dustpan 28, Crawler 30, Concierge mortar 38, Signer 58, Salvo 58 (min 12), Deep Clean 40. **Zone placement (edge to edge, inner sides):** small **40 u** (zones 10 u wide, centres +-25), medium **56 u** (14 u wide, centres +-35), large **80 u** (16 u wide, centres +-48), depth 0.7 W everywhere. Both sides advancing at 3.0 u/s gives first damage at 5-9 s (small), 7-11 s (medium) and 9-14 s (large), median 9-11 s, hard ceiling 20 s. The Signer, the Salvo Cart and Housekeeper are placed >= 14 u behind their zone's front edge, bosses >= 12 u; no unit starts inside its own weapon range of an enemy.
- **Vehicle corridors (plan W10): every lane a machine uses is >= 6 u wide**; boss lanes (Rex, Concierge, Hummock) >= 10 u; bridges 6 u (decks) to 8 u; the lava causeway is exactly 6 u by design; a 6-tank-column pass test runs on every arena with vehicles. Foot-only lanes (the crash site crawl lane, the hive side tunnels) may be 3-4 u.
- **Readability (W8/W9):** at most 35 visible (type, variant) prop batches per recipe (the real maximum is 9 types x 2 variants = 18, props.md section 6); buildings use voxel 0.2, everything else 0.1; tall props (tower 12 u, tree 9 u, spires 6-7 u) stay on map edges or at objective sites, <= 4.5 u in the fight corridor, and every recipe keeps >= 75 percent of 200 head-height points visible from the default camera.
- **Hazards and markers.** Hazard kinds used are the existing `geyser` (lava world x3, ice world cryo vents x2); no mines, no `quicksand`; the **goo** and **ice** effects are materials (slow), not hazards. Markers: `hill`, `exit`, `vip_start`, `general_spawn`, `waypoint` plus capture posts (`sf_holo_beacon` props) and objective props (`sf_reactor_core`, `sf_shield_pylon`, `sf_heart_tree`), all within |x|, |z| <= 0.25 x size as the validator requires, and **derived from the generated arena, never typed as absolute coordinates** (map 05 risk: golden hashes freeze a recipe).
- **Symmetry and fairness (S22):** symmetric recipes set `g.sym` and must show 45-55 % mirror fairness; asymmetric ones join the exemption list (35-65 %): lava world, crash site, jungle. The other nine are symmetric.
- **Campaign uses 9 of 12;** `sf_iceworld`, `sf_chasm` and `sf_biodome` serve Quick, Survival, Daily, the Arena Builder and puzzles (the chasm and the biodome each host a puzzle, so only the ice world is Quick-only). If the cut ladder (rung 1) takes arenas, the ice world goes first; the nine mission arenas and the two puzzle arenas are never cut.

## 2. The twelve arenas

### 2.1 `sf_moonbase` Dome Sweet Dome (medium, seed 7, sym mx)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_moon`, regolith; time 22, clear, sky `space_planet` (black sky, a big blue home planet), gravity x0.4, fog 0. **Signature features:** three domes (hab, mess hall, hangar) on the flanks and the rear, a ring of shallow craters at 18 u from the centre, solar arrays, antenna masts with a spinning dish, a central airlock lane between two airlock doors.
- **Layout:** A 12 u central lane runs between two `sf_airlock_door` props, with a hab dome on each flank and one at the rear, so the middle is for the line and the flanks are for buggies. The shallow craters ring the centre at 18 u, which gives a retreating unit a place to rest its bubble out of the line of fire. Each flank dome carries a `sf_dome_panel` that a script can crack for the decompression set-piece.
- **Objectives:** eliminate, hold_hill (the crater centre), protect_vip. **Vehicle corridors:** central lane 12 u, flank lanes 8 u. **First contact:** 7-11 s. **Missions and modes:** M1; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder; hosts the Quick "tutorial duel".

### 2.2 `sf_lavaworld` Magma Lounge (medium, seed 3, asymmetric)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_lava`, basalt; time 21, weather `ember_ion`, sky `ember`, fog .1, **lava plane**. **Signature features:** a lava lake with three basalt islets in a row, each carrying a holo beacon, one 6 u causeway along the south edge, three geyser vents around the middle islet, obsidian shards and basalt pillars, hull plates and cargo containers on the shores as a mining outpost.
- **Layout:** A lava lake 26 u across fills the middle, and three basalt islets stand in it in a row, each with a capture beacon. The single 6 u causeway runs along the south edge to the first two islets and is the only dry road, so the ground line is shelled on it while hover tanks skate across the open lava to the third islet. The three geyser vents ring the middle islet and erupt on a red telegraph, so the islet is a place to pass through, not to rest.
- **Objectives:** capture (three posts), hold_hill, eliminate. **Vehicle corridors:** causeway 6 u (exactly one tank wide with margin), shores 14 u. **First contact:** 8-12 s on foot, 5-7 s for hover. **Missions and modes:** M2; puzzle 2 "Light Bridge Etiquette"; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.3 `sf_reactor` Core Meltdown Cafe (medium, seed 12, sym rot)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_industrial`, hull_plate; time 20, weather `ember_ion` (ion sparks), sky `teal_strip`, fog .1, shallow **coolant** pools (teal-tinted water). **Signature features:** a central reactor core (r2.5) with three shield pylons in a triangle at 12 u, catwalks (deck cells) over the coolant pools, explosive coolant tanks beside the pylons, blast doors at the lane ends, cargo containers.
- **Layout:** The reactor core stands in the centre ringed by three shield pylons, and the whole yard is crossed by 10 u catwalks laid over shallow coolant pools. Coolant tanks sit next to each pylon, so a pylon that dies can chain into the next; blast doors close the two lane ends and open for their owner only. The rotational symmetry gives each side the same three pylons at the same distance from its zone.
- **Objectives:** destroy (core plus pylons), defend_core, eliminate. **Vehicle corridors:** catwalks 10 u (the Rex's lane), pools wadeable at x1.7 cost. **First contact:** 7-11 s. **Missions and modes:** M3; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.4 `sf_crashsite` Hull Down (medium, seed 5, asymmetric)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_crash`, sand with hull_plate; time 18 (dusk), weather `fog` (smoke), sky `dusk_smoke`, fog .3, no water. **Signature features:** a cruiser split into two halves as a cover maze, two burning engines, escape pods, junk heaps, solar arrays and cargo containers, tilted corridors (terrain ramps).
- **Layout:** The cruiser lies in two halves with a 10 u gap, the open sandy approach on the west and the hull maze on the east. Hull-plate chains cut three lanes through the wreck: two 7 u lanes for vehicles and one 4 u crawl lane for foot and cloaked cutters, lit by the burning engine at the back of the middle lane. The VIP starts at an escape pod on the sand end and the exit marker sits beyond the far half of the hull.
- **Objectives:** protect_vip, eliminate, survive_waves. **Vehicle corridors:** two 7 u lanes, one 4 u foot lane. **First contact:** 8-12 s (the enemy ambushes from the hull). **Missions and modes:** M4; puzzle 3 "Do Not Disturb"; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.5 `sf_megamall` Courtesy Plaza (large, seed 9, sym mxz)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_retail`, tile_floor; time 12, clear, sky `retail_bright`, shallow fountain pond in the atrium. **Signature features:** kiosk rows, a fountain atrium on a 1.5 u terrace (the Help Desk hill), wide lanes with conveyor-stripe decor, shop fronts along both long edges, billboard stacks and parked hovercars as cover.
- **Layout:** A 12 u terrace with a fountain pond sits in the atrium at the centre and is the Help Desk hill. Four 10 u lanes run past it between rows of help kiosks, and the shop fronts and neon towers stand on the two long edges so nothing hides the fight. The mirrored symmetry puts a billboard stack and a blast door at each end.
- **Objectives:** hold_hill, kill_general, capture. **Vehicle corridors:** main lanes 10 u, cross lanes 8 u. **First contact:** 9-14 s. **Missions and modes:** M5; puzzle 4 "Have You Tried Turning It Off? (Puzzle Edition)"; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.6 `sf_neoncity` Gridlock Boulevard (large, seed 21, sym mx)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_neon`, neon_pad with hull_plate; time 23, weather `neon_rain`, sky `neon_night`, fog .25, no water. **Signature features:** the skyline on the edges only, a 14 u boulevard, 8 u side streets, parked hovercars as cover, billboard stacks, antenna masts, a shield pylon at the plaza end.
- **Layout:** A 14 u boulevard runs the length of the arena between two rows of shop fronts, with 8 u side streets crossing it every 24 u. Parked hovercars and billboards break the lanes into pockets, and the neon towers stand outside the playable rectangle so the skyline never covers a unit. The plaza end carries the billboard stack that falls to reveal the boss.
- **Objectives:** kill_general, protect_vip, capture. **Vehicle corridors:** boulevard 14 u, streets 8 u. **First contact:** 9-14 s. **Missions and modes:** M6; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.7 `sf_jungle` Spore Hollow (large, seed 17, asymmetric)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_jungle`, glow_moss; time 19 (dusk), weather `spores`, sky `jungle_dusk`, fog .25, **acid river** (lime-tinted water: an 8 u deep channel with one shallow ford). **Signature features:** the Heart tree (r2.6) on the near bank, a canopy ring on the edges (the Teutoburg readability pattern), spore pods, egg clusters on the far bank, drop pods.
- **Layout:** The river splits the hollow into a near bank with the Heart tree and a far bank with the egg clusters, and it is 8 u wide so a 12 u blink or a hover tank crosses it in one go. A single shallow ford 40 u upstream is the only dry crossing, and the Skitter columns funnel through it. The canopy ring keeps the corridor readable and hides nothing from the default camera.
- **Objectives:** defend_core, survive_waves. **Vehicle corridors:** the ford 8 u, the banks 12 u. **First contact:** 10-14 s (the river delays the ground waves, the spitters shell from the far bank at once). **Missions and modes:** M7; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.8 `sf_hive` Sinkhole Nest (medium, seed 8, sym mx)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_hive`, resin with goo; time 12 (light falls through the open top), weather `spores`, sky `cavern_open`, shallow **bile** pools (green water). **Signature features:** resin walls forming three chokepoints, three hive spires, egg clusters, goo pools (slow), the queen's dais at one end, an open sky above.
- **Layout:** Three 6 u chokepoints through resin walls divide the nest into a front cavern, a middle cavern and the queen's dais, and the walls open when destroyed. Goo pools slow the middle cavern, the egg clusters stand along the walls as hatch points, and the sky is open so the manta's shadow and the orbital beam have a clear column. The mirrored symmetry puts a spire at each side of the dais.
- **Objectives:** survive_waves, destroy (spires), kill_general. **Vehicle corridors:** chokepoints 6 u, caverns 20 u. **First contact:** 7-11 s. **Missions and modes:** M8; puzzle 6 "Bait And Clean"; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.9 `sf_orbital` Spin Cycle Station (medium, seed 31, sym rot)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_station`, hull_plate; time 0, clear, sky `space_ring` (stars and a spinning planet through the windows), gravity x0.6, **void** gaps (deep black water) along the rim. **Signature features:** a ring deck of panels, bulkhead segments making lanes, cargo containers, airlocks, blast doors, one huge window (the hull breach), a pair of teleport pads, antenna masts.
- **Layout:** A ring deck circles a central cargo hall, with bulkhead chains cutting four 8 u lanes between the hall and the rim. The rim windows open onto deep void water that no ground unit can enter, and the huge window at the queen's end is the set-piece that vents the brood. The two teleport pads sit on opposite lanes and are decor if the rung-5 cut is taken.
- **Objectives:** kill_general, survive_waves, eliminate. **Vehicle corridors:** lanes 8 u, the hall 24 u. **First contact:** 7-11 s. **Missions and modes:** M9; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.10 `sf_iceworld` Frostbite Flats (medium, seed 14, sym mx)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_ice`, snow with ice; time 8, weather `snow`, sky `aurora`, fog .2, no water. **Signature features:** ice spires on the lane margins, ice blocks as soft cover, two cryo vents (geyser hazards), a half-buried hull as hull-plate chains, solar arrays, drop pods, wide white lanes.
- **Layout:** Wide white lanes cross the flats between rows of ice spires that stand on the margins, never in the corridor. Two cryo vents erupt near the centre on a frost telegraph, and ice blocks make soft cover around a half-buried hull. The mirrored symmetry puts a drop pod at each zone edge for the Grazer Drop god power.
- **Objectives:** eliminate, protect_vip, hold_hill. **Vehicle corridors:** lanes 10 u. **First contact:** 7-11 s. **Missions and modes:** Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder (no mission, no puzzle: the first arena the cut ladder removes).

### 2.11 `sf_chasm` Gap Year Canyon (medium, seed 19, sym mx)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_crystal`, basalt; time 21, weather `fog` (violet-lit), sky `violet`, fog .3, deep **rift** (violet water). **Signature features:** a 6-8 u rift across the middle with two 5 u deck bridges (neon_pad cells removable by `editTerrain`), force gates at the bridge ends, crystal clusters, boulders, basalt pillars.
- **Layout:** A rift 6-8 u wide cuts the canyon in two, and two 5 u deck bridges cross it, each closed at one end by a `sf_force_gate`. The crystal clusters on both rims are soft cover that glows violet, and a blink of 12 u always clears the rift while a hover tank skates over it. A script can drop a bridge for the "Gap Year" beat, leaving blink and hover as the only crossings.
- **Objectives:** capture, protect_vip, survive_waves. **Vehicle corridors:** bridges 5 u (one tank wide, no passing; vehicle rosters use one bridge) and the rims 12 u. **First contact:** 8-12 s. **Missions and modes:** puzzle 5 "Gap Year"; Quick, Survival, Daily, Builder.

### 2.12 `sf_biodome` Biodome Gardens (small, seed 23, sym mxz)
- **Theme / biome / env:** `sf_dome`, grass with tile_floor paths; time 12, clear, sky `noon_calm`, fog 0, a shallow pond. **Signature features:** a ring of dome panels and small hab domes, garden beds of glow trees and giant ferns, a pond, a pair of teleport pads, holo beacons on the paths.
- **Layout:** A compact garden under a ring of dome panels, 64 u across, with a pond in the middle and two tile paths crossing it. Glow trees and ferns line the beds at the edges and never block the paths. The teleport pads sit at the corners so a duel can be won by a hop.
- **Objectives:** eliminate (puzzles, Quick duels). **Vehicle corridors:** paths 6 u (the biodome is foot-first; a Dustpan fits). **First contact:** 5-9 s (declared exception, the enemy `holds`). **Missions and modes:** puzzle 1 "Pop, Then Hide"; Quick duels, Survival, Daily, Builder; calm by design (no set-piece).

## 3. The binding mission ladder (Medieval's and Modern's arenas.md set the precedent; part 2 builds `missions.json` from these rows)

Acts: **I "Landlords and Squatters"** (the Moon has been repossessed; both parties are confident about it: bubbles, hover, the reactor), **II "The Help Is Here"** (the appliances are very pleased to assist; the guild would like a word, quietly: cloak, EMP, the Concierge), **III "Queen Size"** (the bugs have a plan, the jungle has a hill and the sky has a noise complaint: blink, orbital, the Queen). One new mechanic taught per mission (M3, M6, M9 teach none); a star 3 tests only mechanics taught strictly earlier, or none; the finales combine two or more earlier mechanics. Star 1 = win; star 2 = win with >= 50 % of the army cost alive (generic, as Ancient); par is in ergs (`fmtCost`). The enemy cost : player budget ratio is the pressure curve (0.53 to 1.05; arithmetic in rosters.md section 8). Every mission has a set-piece package `{event, shot{from,to,hold,ease}, announcer slot, stinger, sfx}`; shots run 3-6 s in real time with the sim at 0.5x, skippable, Reduce Motion = cut. Enemy groups may be cross-faction ("hired").
