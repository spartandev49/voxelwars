# Sci-Fi: the five survival bosses (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Rules applied. Five bosses, five different silhouettes on **five different rigs** (mech4, insect1 queen, mech1, quad1 titan, dragon1 manta), **none a scaled hum1 soldier** (the plan asks for a non-scaled-hum1 silhouette per era boss; D21 allowed Sci-Fi the one suited giant and this roster does not use it). One boss per faction except Tidy Concord, the player's anchor faction. Each boss demands a different answer from the player army, which is what makes the Survival cycle replayable: **a mech you must switch off and hit from behind, a brood you must splash, a hotel you must stun and stab, a hill you must out-damage, a sky you must look up at.** Every boss carries a mechanic joke and fits `MAX_PARTS` 48 with room to spare. Triangle budgets are set against the measured Ancient references (war_elephant 36 parts: near 55,044 / far 14,168; chariot_archer 43 parts: near 32,088 / far 8,724; plan s5: boss far <= 15 K, far mesh judged at the model's own switch distance and at 80 u). Boss waves are every 5th wave in this fixed cycle (the order is also the difficulty ramp); after wave 25 the cycle repeats with the budget scaled by the normal wave formula (enemy budget 2400 + 900 n, the boss's cost comes out of it, the rest is an escort of the boss's faction). Boss hp and cost are design intent for BALANCE; bosses are judged in Survival and in their missions, not on the 30-62 % field-win-rate band.

The far-mesh rule (plan s5): the defining feature keeps at least 60 percent of its near silhouette pixels at the model's switch distance and at 80 u (`56*sqrt(lodK2)` and `56*0.35*sqrt(lodK2)`). The defining feature per boss is listed below; the `downsample2` lint is the arbiter. Heights stay under the 9.0 u builder limit (Rex 7.4, Queen 5.8, Concierge 8.6, Hummock 8.4; the Manta flies at 8 u with a body under 2 u tall). Rex, Hummock, Concierge and the Manta need the footprint exemption through `meta.bounds` (R11, same request as the Medieval dragon): spans of 6.5-9.5 u exceed the old x < 6 u test. Every boss that uses `summon_on_death` (the Queen) needs a `reveal` clip on its rig because the existing ability plays a 1.6 s reveal channel (map 03).

## 1. Rustbucket Rex (wave 5, `rummage`)

- **Silhouette:** a squat dumpster body on four stilt legs (each an upper and a rigid lower segment), a crane-arm tail with a hook, a radiator grille for a mouth with real teeth, two smokestacks, chest rocket racks, a scrap cannon over the left shoulder, mismatched panels in three colours, two headlamps. Height class XL (7.4 u). **Defining feature:** the crane tail (3 segments, 2x2 voxel cross-section) plus the radiator teeth block (3x3); the tail is the readable far silhouette. The headlamps and the rocket racks carry the team tint.
- **Signature animation:** `overclock` at 50 percent hp (steam bursts from both stacks, the lid slams, speed x1.4, a red "overclock" ground ring pulses), `stomp` (a four-beat quake with a dust ring, the point-blank aoe r4 stagger), `scrap_fire` (cannon recoil, a crumpled can of junk arcs out), `rack_volley` (four rockets pop up like toast), topple death: the Rex lists, a stilt gives way, it falls sideways into a pile of bolts and sparks, the crane tail flops last and the hook bounces. Reuse: mech4 base set (idle idle_combat walk run strike_stomp launch hit_front stagger death_back) with the gait via `plantRigidLeg`.
- **Entrance beat:** Survival wave 5: a truck horn off screen, the ground shakes, steam rolls in and the Rex clanks to a stop in the spawn zone; M3: the heap at the back of the reactor yard opens its radiator when the first pylon falls. Announcer `boss_enters` (Brutus: "A DUMPSTER! WITH LEGS! And an ambition!"); shot: low push to the radiator teeth, 3.5 s; stinger `sf_stg_rex_horn` (brass stab and a squeaky horn); sfx truck horn, steam hiss, stilt creak.
- **Demands and notes:** scrap cannon `explosive` 70 aoe r5 with a crater, cd 4, range 34; racks 4 rockets cd 6; stomp r4 cd 10; EMP stun capped at 2.0 s, which is exactly long enough to walk round; rear plate `armorFace` .20; a hover flanker is not hit by the stomp ring if it passes at speed. Strong against masses and packed lines; weak to EMP, the rear plate and blink. Ground-only cannon: air units are immune to the shell, not to the racks.
- **Death/reaction set:** topple (sideways, parts scatter), hit reactions: stagger and a headlamp flicker. **Mechanic joke:** it was a crane, a truck, three vending machines and somebody's ambition; the overclock is "what happens when you ask a dumpster to feel something".

## 2. The Hive Queen (wave 10, `skitter`)

- **Silhouette:** a tank-sized abdomen sac with glowing egg clusters, a crown of four antlers, six huge rigid legs, a small head with mandibles and a throat sac, a thorax with plates. Height class L (5.8 u). **Defining feature:** the antler crown (2x2 voxel tines) and the glowing egg clusters (3 glow parts) on the sac; the sac is also the rear weak point, so it must read from behind. Lime glow dots are the team tint carrier.
- **Signature animation:** `brood_heave` (the abdomen contracts, the egg glow pulses, ten skitterlings tumble out; this is also the `reveal` clip), `screech` (head back, mandibles wide, the scare ring r10), `acid_cone` (head forward, a green cone), `claw_swat`, idle: a tired sigh with the sac breathing; death: she topples onto her side with a goo splat and the antlers rattle once. Reuse: insect1 base (idle walk run strike_bite strike_claw hit_front stagger death_back cower) plus `reveal` and `brood_heave`.
- **Entrance beat:** Survival wave 10: three `sf_egg_cluster` props pop at the spawn edge and she hauls herself up through them; M9: the crane-up reveal on the station dais. Announcer `boss_enters` (Cassandra: "Nine thousand children. The bedroom is not big enough."); shot: slow crane-up from the egg clusters to the crown, 4 s; stinger `sf_stg_queen_rise`; sfx egg pops, wet thump, a tired roar.
- **Demands and notes:** claws `pierce` 28 range 2.6; `acid_cone` poison (ignores bubbles, so shield armies suffer) range 10 cd 5; `summon_on_death` brood once at under 50 percent hp or with three foes within 5 u (10 skitterlings tumble out winded); `screech` scare r10 cd 18; `armorFace` rear sac .10. Strong against swarming armies and shield lines; weak to splash on the sac, the Housekeeper's Deep Clean, blink flankers behind her.
- **Death/reaction set:** topple and splat; hit reactions: flinch, antler shake. **Mechanic joke:** majestic, tired, nine thousand children, none of which called; Brutus checks twice whether "queen" is a metaphor and Plato says it is still a metaphor.

## 3. The Grand Concierge (wave 15, `courtesy`)

- **Silhouette:** a ten-storey desk-and-kiosk torso, a giant TV face with a smile glyph, a bellhop cap, reverse-knee legs, a luggage cart on the back with bags, tray-lasers on both forearms, a bow-tie light, two antennas. Height class XL (8.6 u). **Defining feature:** the TV face (a 6x6 panel with a 2x2 glow glyph) and the luggage cart (a 3-voxel-thick frame); the reverse-knee legs read as "not a person" at 40 px. The bow-tie light and the tray rims carry the team tint.
- **Signature animation:** `please_wait` (a flat palm raised, the TV face shows an ellipsis, the confuse ring r8), `laser_sweep` (tray arms sweep a beam across an arc), `mortar_cough` (the luggage cart tilts and coughs a suitcase), the step (a ground ring, a servo whine, a bell "ting"), `buffering` on EMP stun (the knees fold, the face shows a spinner, the sim slows to 0.5x for the first stun in M5/M6), death: it topples like a falling hotel, the bell rings once and the face goes to a frown. Reuse: mech1 base (idle idle_combat walk strike_slam launch hit_front stagger kneel death_back).
- **Entrance beat:** Survival wave 15: a revolving-door sound, the `sf_blast_door` at the back opens and a welcome chime sounds before the stride; M6: the billboard stack at the end of the boulevard collapses and the Concierge strides out ("Mech Stomp Reveal"). Announcer `boss_enters` (Brutus: "TEN STORIES OF FRIENDLY! Bring a hat, he will want to hold it!"); shot: low boots-to-face, 5 s; stinger `sf_stg_concierge_steps`; sfx servo whine, welcome chime, step boom.
- **Demands and notes:** twin service lasers (hitscan sweep, energy 30 per tick, range 28), `lost_luggage` mortar `explosive` 60 aoe r4 cd 6 range 38, `please_wait` (confuse r8, channel 1.5 s, cd 18), bubble 600 with a 6 s delay and 40 per s regeneration; EMP stun capped at 2.5 s (the window to stab the rear plate `armorFace` .20). Strong against crowds and shield lines; weak to EMP plus a rear or cloak stab and to blink flankers.
- **Death/reaction set:** topple with the bell; hit reactions: a TV-face flinch, a shoulder shrug. **Mechanic joke:** "Welcome to the Violence Desk. Your number is: now."

## 4. The Elder Hummock (wave 20, `glowmoss`)

- **Silhouette:** a hill with legs: a mossy dome body, four thick legs, a small sleepy head, a golden lamp as big as a hut on a three-segment stalk, petals and ferns along the back, a tuft tail. Height class XL (8.4 u). **Defining feature:** the lamp on its stalk (a 3x3 stalk, a 4x4x4 glow lamp) above a round mass; the greyscale read is a huge round mid-tone with a bright point above it. The lamp ring carries the team tint.
- **Signature animation:** `wake` (it rises out of the ground: y -2 to 0 with a dust ring, moss shower, the lamp lights), the step (a ring of spores pulses outward each footfall), `bloom_aura` (the lamp brightens in a 1 Hz pulse and green sparkles drift to allies), `earth_stomp` (a quake ring r7), `trample_charge`, death: it settles down like a hill falling asleep, the lamp dims, a tiny flower sprouts on the back. Reuse: quad1 base with a new `hummock` species row (+7 clips: 3 gaits, idle, 3 deaths) and `wake`.
- **Entrance beat:** Survival wave 20: the ground humps in the spawn zone, dust rises and the hill stands up; M7 (as a fixed friendly): when the Heart drops under 50 percent the hill wakes ("The Hill Wakes"). Announcer `boss_enters` (Plato: "Hills were named after it. It disagrees with their coordinates."); shot: 6 s rising crane from the roots to the lamp; stinger `sf_stg_awaken`; sfx earth rumble, spore burst, a soft titan roar.
- **Demands and notes:** earth stomp aoe r7 blunt 45 stagger cd 8, trample (mass >= 8, 18 dps), `heal_pulse` organic aura (allies +10 hp every second within r12, up to 8 units), armour .40. It heals the escort, so burst through it (orbital, beams, Deep Clean) or kill the escort first; EMP does nothing (organic); it is slow to turn. Strong against anything it walks over; weak to focused burst and to hover kiting.
- **Death/reaction set:** settle and sprout; hit reactions: a sleepy blink. **Mechanic joke:** the lamp is a ring light somebody insisted on; the hill has opinions and shares them with its feet.

## 5. The Void Manta (wave 25, `quiet_hour`)

- **Silhouette:** a flat black manta, a nine-unit span, glowing violet edge-lines along the wings, fin ripples on the trailing edge, a small head with two cephalic fins, a tapering tail; its shadow blob covers half the screen. Height class L-A (body < 2 u, altitude 8 u). **Defining feature:** the wing edge-lines (glow voxels 2 voxels wide) and the wing thickness (>= 2 voxels so the membrane survives `downsample2`); the shadow blob is the second tell. The edge lines carry the team tint (cloaked, the rim stays drawn).
- **Signature animation:** `glide` (a slow wing ripple loop), the `cloak` cycle (6 s on, 10 s off: the outline dissolves into a dithered shimmer and returns), `hush_cone` (wings flare, a violet cone), `bomb_dive` (descends from 8 u to 6 u, drops a void bomb, climbs), banking from `vx`, death: a slow spiral, a soft plop, the manta folds up like a paper napkin and lands as a very large stain. Reuse: dragon1 base with the manta variant (flat wings, ripple fins) and `fly`, `hover`, `bomb_dive`, `death_back` clips.
- **Entrance beat:** the shadow arrives first (a blob scales from nothing to half the screen), then the manta glides in over the arena edge; M8 wave 5: it glides across the open sinkhole and the sky goes quiet. Announcer `boss_enters` (Cassandra: "A noise complaint is coming. The Quiet Hour sends it personally. Stay quiet. You will not."); shot: 6 s looking up from the pit; stinger `sf_stg_manta`; sfx manta hum, sonic shush, orbital charge.
- **Demands and notes:** void bomb `explosive` 60 aoe r4 (arc, ground only) cd 5 range 12, `hush_cone` scare r10 cd 16, bubble 400 with a 6 s delay and 30 per s regeneration, air layer 8 u, cloak cycle (detectors reveal it, lamps r10 and drones r10-12). Strong against ground-only armies, artillery and bubble lines (bombs); weak to rifles, beams, drones and anything that hits air. Remnant rule: at under 25 percent hp it descends to 6 u and stays.
- **Death/reaction set:** spiral and fold; hit reactions: a wing ripple. **Mechanic joke:** it files noise complaints with its entire body.

## 6. Acceptance per boss

Part count and triangle budgets measured by `tools/modelstats` at build; `downsample2` lint passes on the defining feature; the `reveal` clip exists where `summon_on_death` is used (Queen); `DEFAULT_META` rows match the baked clips; the entrance and death beats fire their five channels (ER20) in Survival and in the mission; EMP caps, bubble delays and the cloak cycle are data and tested (negative controls: cap removed shifts the Rex/Concierge duel by a stated minimum); a Survival run reaches wave 25 under the bot ladder with the cycle intact; no boss is a recolour of another (ER3 silhouette hash). If mech4 is cut by the gait spike, the Rex becomes a `tank1` variant and keeps its tail and teeth (rosters.md section 5).
