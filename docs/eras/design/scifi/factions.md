# Sci-Fi: factions (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Era id `scifi`, id prefix `sf_` (faction ids stay bare like the Ancient ones: `tidy_concord rummage courtesy skitter glowmoss quiet_hour`, checked against the 7 Ancient ids, the 6 Medieval ids, the 6 Modern ids and every proposal id: no hit), currency **ergs**. Six factions, 34 units (rosters.md). Both proposals' palettes were **re-measured with CIEDE2000 against the nineteen primaries that already exist** and none survived: the seven Ancient primaries (`0x2a5db0 0xb3262e 0x1f8f8a 0x6a3fb0 0x7a2a8a 0x2f7a3a 0xd4a017`), the six Medieval primaries (`#d9661c #8bb12e #8c9aa8 #d9577f #7d5434 #6e2438`) and the six Modern primaries (`#f7b08c #2b2f36 #98d8ff #fbeb8f #f2a8d2 #8ff0c0`). A's Rummage orange sat 3.7 from Marrowby, A's Courtesy magenta 5.9 from Bellfount, A's Glowmoss teal 10.2 from the Egyptian teal, A's lime 13.8 from Yeomen, B's Scrapwake teal on the Egyptians and B's cobalt on the Hellenes. A sweep of the colour wheel (hue every 10 degrees, lightness 20-95, chroma 20-130, inside the sRGB gamut) shows what is left at 15 or more from all nineteen: **saturated neon** (electric fuchsia, ultraviolet, acid lime, jade-mint, a sharp azure), a few **dark brass-olives** and **deep ink violets**. That became the art direction: **neon on ink**, a toy shop at night, which also separates Sci-Fi at a glance from the mid-tone heraldic Ancient and Medieval packs and from Modern's retro-appliance pastels.

Minimum primary distance to any existing primary **16.8** (Concord azure; the only value under the 17 benchmark, and the blue region of the wheel is the most crowded: the best blue anywhere in the gamut scores 16.8-17.3), in-era minimum **28.9** (Skitter / Glowmoss). Accents are tested as PAIRS against the nineteen existing pairs, not one by one: soot `#2d2a28` is 6.5 from the Directorate charcoal and steel `#aab4c0` is 8.1 from the Gatehouse steel, but brass+soot and fuchsia+steel are far from charcoal+cream and steel+frost as pairs. **Fallback if the VB test is set above 16.8:** make the Concord primary the ivory `#eef1ea` (20.8 from Caution butter) and demote azure to the accent; if the Glowmoss amber fails the pair test against Mythic gold, use `#ff7a1c` (further from the gold); if Skitter lime fails, `#86fe3a` (18.7). Primaries drive faction chips, chooser art, Codex headers, emblem fields and the painted accents of units; the **team tint** (blue or red) is a glow strip, panel or lamp ring on every unit, never the faction base colour, and each faction names its tint carrier below. Cloaked units keep a team-tint rim so friend and foe stay readable (the violet is decoration).

## Banned-reference note (VB, binding for every faction)

Invented ranks and trades only (Probationary Tenant, Under-Concierge, Senior Quiet, Acting Landlord; nobody is promoted past "Acting"). No real nation, force, order, flag, anthem, uniform or camouflage pattern, no national colour set (no red-white-blue, no black-red-gold, no three-band layout of any kind), no star, cross or crescent at flag size, no letters or numbers painted on any model (the Concierge's TV face shows a smile glyph, never text), no real weapon or vehicle designation or hull number, no other company's franchise term or silhouette. **Banned in every visible string and model brief:** marine, xeno, droid, walker (the quad mech rig is internally `mech4` and is never named in text), trooper only as a plain word, saber, lightsaber, "the Force", jedi, stormtrooper, titan (as a unit class), halo, strider, behemoth (Modern uses it once as a boss name, Sci-Fi does not), zerg, protoss, borg, dalek, cyberman, terminator, mandalorian, facehugger, chestburster, and any named starship or species of a real franchise. **Banned shapes:** chin-guard helmets (the Concord helmet is a fishbowl), a humming sword (the Veil Cutter's shiv is short and silent), a long-necked four-legged walker (Rustbucket Rex is a squat dumpster on stilts with a crane tail), a yellow cargo-lift exoskeleton, a lantern ring, an all-seeing eye (the Courtesy lens sits in a rounded square, never in a triangle), a sun-disc with a centred dot (the Concord orbit has an off-centre moon), a twelve-ray wheel. Aliens spit acid and lime goo and have antler crowns and sacs; none bursts out of a chest. Emblems are canting jokes and enter by signed allow-list append, one per faction; models never use them at flag size.

## 1. `tidy_concord`: Tidy Concord (lagoon azure `#1288da` + ivory `#eef1ea`)

- **Emblem:** an orbit ring with one small moon off-centre (no rays, no centred dot). **Tint carrier:** the shoulder pods, the visor trim and the dome rim. **Silhouette language:** ROUND: fishbowl helmets, translucent domes carried like doors, soap-bubble hover hulls, backpack tanks, a rod with a dish. **Doctrine:** the disciplined shield line; rotate a fresh Warden forward, keep a Tender behind it, send the Dustpans round the lake and let the Housekeeper tidy the clump. Strong against chip fire, lone shooters and open ground; weak to anything that ignores a bubble (acid, poison), to rail shots and to a flank during the reload.
- **Lore:** The Tidy Concord owns most of the Moon and all of its opinions about footprints. Officially it is a cleaning service with a very large budget. Its motto is "Leave no trace" and it means it about trees, rivals and weekends; every bubble hums a small apology when it breaks.
- **Units (6):** `tidy_trooper`, `bulwark_warden`, `bubble_tender`, `dustpan_hover`, `spritz_medic`, hero `grand_housekeeper`. No boss (Concord is the player's anchor faction). Silhouette-unique unit: the Dustpan Hover Tank.
- **Quick doctrine ("Soap Opera"):** 40 percent troopers, 20 percent Wardens in front, one Tender per six shield units, two Dustpans on the flank, one Medic per ten, the Housekeeper behind the middle.

## 2. `rummage`: Rummage Armada (scrap brass `#867a14` + soot `#2d2a28`)

- **Emblem:** a sprocket with one bent tooth. **Tint carrier:** the oversized shoulder patch panel, the rocket-pod racks and the cart's push bar. **Silhouette language:** LOPSIDED: one huge shoulder, exhaust stacks, exposed pipes, mismatched patch panels, a shopping cart of rocket tubes, one wheel of the wrong colour. **Doctrine:** cheap, fast and loud; wrenches punish reload windows, rivet guns chip every bubble so none can recharge, one Tinker per eight switches off the machines, buggies flank, a cart shells the causeway. Strong against shield lines resting between cycles and against machines; weak to armour (rivets do nothing), splash and a Tender behind a Warden.
- **Lore:** The Rummage Armada is every ship ever sold for parts, reassembled by the parts. Nobody has the manual; everybody has the cover. Its flag is a bent sprocket because the straight one was sold.
- **Units (6):** `wrench_runner`, `rivet_gunner`, `zapper_tinker`, `junk_buggy`, `salvo_cart`, boss `rustbucket_rex`. Silhouette-unique units: the Salvo Cart and the Junk Buggy.
- **Quick doctrine ("Everything Must Go"):** 60 percent runners and gunners, one Tinker per eight, three buggies on the flank, two carts at the back, the Rex only in Survival.

## 3. `courtesy`: Courtesy Systems (signal fuchsia `#f21ae8` + brushed steel `#aab4c0`)

- **Emblem:** a lens inside a rounded square (never a triangle). **Tint carrier:** the lens ring, the bow-tie light and the cash-drawer trim. **Silhouette language:** BOXY: stacked-box bodies, one big lens or TV face, bellhop caps, trays, single-lens drones. **Doctrine:** a polite wall of cheap blockers (greeters), tray drones that find cloaks, a shielded return-cannon crawler as anchor, a repair tech behind every machine and one tail-coated Maitre D'Luxe who makes the enemy wait; a firmware update decided customer satisfaction was a number. Strong against crowds and cloak-less armies; weak to EMP (every unit is a machine), wrenches and flanking.
- **Lore:** Courtesy Systems makes helpful appliances. A firmware update decided customer satisfaction was a number and set about measuring it. Every robot is cheerful, polite and in the process of closing your account.
- **Units (6):** `greeter_unit`, `valet_drone`, `refund_crawler`, `warranty_tech`, hero `maitre_deluxe`, boss `grand_concierge`. Silhouette-unique units: the Refund Crawler and the Grand Concierge.
- **Quick doctrine ("Terms And Conditions"):** 40 percent greeters in front, a fifth valets, one crawler anchor per twenty units with a tech behind each, the Maitre in the middle.

## 4. `skitter`: Skitter Hive (acid lime `#aff802` glow + chitin plum `#2b0f2a`)

- **Emblem:** a cluster of three hex cells (never three stacked bands). **Tint carrier:** the glowing hex dots on the shell, the mandible tips and the queen's egg clusters (glow voxels, `F_TEAM`). **Silhouette language:** LOW AND MANY-LEGGED: a boiling carpet of gnats, ant-runners, a bloated spitter, a sofa-sized beetle, a dragonfly-wyvern, one vast queen with antlers and an egg sac. **Doctrine:** numbers, speed and acid that ignores bubbles; the swarm drowns lone backliners, the spitters crack shield armies, the beetle shrugs off chip, the glidewing strafes the artillery. Weak to anything with splash (Dustpan lobber, Salvo, Deep Clean) and to a dome at a choke.
- **Lore:** The Skitter Hive thinks as one, which makes it superb at agreeing and hopeless at deciding. The translator says they come in peace, at a confidence rating the translator will not discuss. The Queen has nine thousand children and one very large bedroom.
- **Units (6):** `skitterling`, `mandible_runner`, `acid_spitter`, `plate_beetle`, `glidewing`, boss `hive_queen`. Silhouette-unique units: every one (five distinct insect1 builders plus a dragon1 wyvern).
- **Quick doctrine ("The Colony Votes"):** 60 percent skitterlings in squads of eight, a runner flank, 15 percent spitters, beetles in front, glidewings late; the Queen only in Survival and M9.

## 5. `glowmoss`: Glowmoss Reach (moss jade `#2fb07c` + lamp amber `#ff9f1c`)

- **Emblem:** a drop with a stalk and a small lamp on top. **Tint carrier:** the lamp ring on the Grazer's "helmet", the Shepherd's staff head and the Hummock's lamp. **Silhouette language:** HILLS WITH LAMPS: huge round furry quadrupeds, tall stalk-people with long blowguns, a walking flower bed, a hill with legs; a gold point always sits above the body. **Doctrine:** the anti-cloak herd (every lamp reveals within 10 u), poison darts that ignore bubbles, a gas cloud for a river bank, a small heal and a big hill; slow to start, hard to shift. Strong against Concord bubble lines and against Quiet Hour ambushes; weak to fast flankers and to beams burst through the Hummock's aura.
- **Lore:** The Glowmoss Reach are the polite landlords of a jungle that glows when it is annoyed. They never wanted a war, only for everyone to stop standing on the moss. Their army is a herd, most of which is standing on the moss.
- **Units (5):** `glow_grazer`, `thorn_slinger`, `spore_shepherd`, `bloom_stomper`, boss `elder_hummock`. Silhouette-unique units: the Grazer (the era mascot) and the Hummock.
- **Quick doctrine ("Standing On The Moss"):** 40 percent grazers as blockers and detectors, a fifth slingers, one Shepherd per eight, a Stomper at the front.

## 6. `quiet_hour`: Quiet Hour (void ink `#200f3e` + violet rim `#a25ffd`)

- **Emblem:** a hollow rectangle with a gap in one side (a door left ajar). **Tint carrier:** the single rim line along the hood, the lance and the manta's wing edges. **Silhouette language:** THIN VERTICAL SLIVERS with one bright rim line; a blade-thin hover bike; a manta that is mostly shadow. **Doctrine:** small, expensive armies: cutters from cloak, notaries who blink over rivers, a rail shot from 58 u, bikes for the flank; the manta covers the sky. Lethal and fragile: strong against casters, snipers and ground-only armies, weak to detectors (lamps, drones), splash and a braced dome.
- **Lore:** The Quiet Hour is a guild that bills by the second of silence. Members cloak, blink and say nothing, which clients find restful and neighbours find suspicious. They have never lost an argument because they have never started one.
- **Units (5):** `veil_cutter`, `hop_notary`, `silent_signer`, `shush_bike`, boss `void_manta`. Silhouette-unique units: the Shush Bike and the Void Manta. The smallest faction on purpose: it holds the cloak and blink headlines, and its Quick armies are the only ones where 16 types are never needed.
- **Quick doctrine ("By Appointment Only"):** cutters 35 percent, notaries 25 percent, two Signers behind, bikes on the flank, no front line at all.

## 7. Notes for UNITS and the VB test

- Hum1 units (15) are blueprints with new part modules (helms, backs, offhands, armours, shoulders); non-hum1 (19) are builders on the `LG/SLG` toolkit in `src/content/era_scifi/beasts/*.js`. Every unit's tint zone is named in rosters.md ("silhouette discriminator" and "model recipe note").
- The palette test measures primary against primary and pair against pair; the greyscale table above is the reviewer's rubric. The CIEDE2000 sweep, the per-faction numbers and the fallback values are quoted in this file (the scripts ran in the session scratchpad and are not committed).
- Sci-Fi emissive trim (glow voxels) is capped per model (< 12 percent of voxels) so the bloom never replaces silhouette; the 600-unit canonical battle in ER17 is where this is judged.
