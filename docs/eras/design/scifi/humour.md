# Sci-Fi humour bible (binding for COMEDY-SF and the COMEDY-EDITOR)

Owner: DESIGN-ERA-SCI-FI part 2. Companion files: `feel_sheet.md` s8 (the three engines are named there), `rosters.md` (unit jokes, bark roles, scout codes), `boss_table.md` (boss mechanic jokes), `missions_outline.md` (briefings, set-piece lines, star text, rewards), `god_powers.md` (tooltips and the intern gag), `first_three_minutes.md` (beats and toasts), `sound_music.md` (sound hooks of the barks). Ancient's bureaucratic understatement is NOT primary here: this era's comedy is a flat technical register applied to trivial things (E1), a movie trailer applied to a parking space (E2) and customer-service cheer applied to destruction (E3). Forms and stamps survive only as Courtesy Systems lore and as chrome; at most one line in five may be about paperwork.

Voices (unchanged across eras, spec/humor.md s2): **Brutus** (play-by-play; bombastic; one ALL-CAPS emphasis word per line, a place or a title card may add a second; in this era he also reads trailer taglines), **Plato** (colour; rhetorical questions; undercuts Brutus in six words; ends on a question or a measured understatement; unmuted since the end of Modern), **Cassandra** (analyst; short flat sentences; "I said"; right and ignored; in this era she is, for the first time, occasionally pleased). The commentators have been dragged through time by Zeus's intern, who stays offstage.

Tone law: jokes punch at situations, hubris, equipment, paperwork, appliances, trailers and the three commentators; never at people, scientists, soldiers or any real force or agency. Deaths are comic: humans are **knocked out** (a tidy flop, circling dots, a fade), machines **power down** (sparks, a droop, a "pdddt"), aliens go **splorp** (lime goo, no scream); no pleading, family, pain or blood. The violence is sparks and goo; the mood is a very expensive school play performed in a toy shop at night.

## 1. Three comedic engines

### E1 Deadpan Technobabble With Numbers (units, Codex, Plato, Cassandra, tips, the alien translator, results lessons)

Rules: (1) every joke names a number, a tell or a rule of this game; if it names none it is cut; (2) the delivery is flat: the technical register is applied to the trivial and never winks; (3) no filler jargon ("reverse the polarity", "recalibrate the flux"); the jargon is the real mechanic (bubble, delay, regeneration, break window, lock line, channel, detection radius); (4) the alien translator is a running subtitle gag with a visible **confidence rating** that is always low and an **alternate reading** that is a worse pun; at most once per battle per faction and never twice in 90 s; (5) numbers are small, specific and true at tuning (BALANCE re-verifies); (6) the target is the instrument, never the person who reads it.

1. [Codex, `tidy_trooper`] "Shield: 40 points. Regrows after three seconds of not being rude to it."
2. [P, `shield_pop`] "A shield is a promise made of light. This one has just been broken, and it regrows in three seconds, unlike most promises."
3. [C, `emp_freeze`] "The machines are off for three seconds. I said so. Nobody asked how long."
4. [translator, Skitter taunt] "[Translator: 'We come in peace.' Confidence: 11 percent. Alternate reading: 'We come in pieces.']"
5. [Tip] "Rivets do not hurt a bubble. They interrupt it. The timer starts again every time."
6. [C, `shield_chain`] "Seven bubbles in two seconds. That is not a coincidence. That is a schedule."
7. [P, `blink_hop`] "Twelve units in a fraction of a second. The distance still exists. It was merely not consulted."
8. [Codex, `acid_spitter`] "Thirty poison over five seconds. Ignores shields, armour and manners."
9. [Results lesson] "Our bubbles popped {n} times and regrew {m}. The difference is called a bill."
10. [Codex, `silent_signer`] "Asks for a signature from sixty metres. Silence counts as consent."

### E2 Drive-In Trailer Voice (Brutus, posters, taglines, loading lines, genre bingo)

Rules: (1) trailer hyperbole applied to trivial stakes: the Moon is a parking space, a lease is a destiny, a bubble is a character; (2) **genre bingo**: the commentators name every cliche at the moment it happens (the twist, the quiet one, the reveal, the second act, the sequel hook) and never name a real film, franchise or studio; the cliche is always generic; (3) one ALL-CAPS emphasis word per Brutus line; a **title card** (the battle-start opener and the poster taglines) may be all caps, once per battle; (4) ratings and stamps are jokes about the booth ("Rated G, for Glowing"); (5) Plato and Cassandra puncture it: Plato by taking the cliche literally, Cassandra by knowing the next reel; (6) never a voice-over deeper than the line deserves ("Somebody call the trailer guy").

1. [B, `battle_start`] "IN A GALAXY FAR FROM THE SNACK BAR, two armies fight over a parking space on the Moon! RATED G, FOR GLOWING!" (the title card; once per battle)
2. [B, `shield_pop`] "POP! That was a bubble and now it is a memory! Recharging in three, two, one, rude!"

4. [B, `kill_streak`] "Five in a row! Somebody call the trailer guy, we need a deeper VOICE!"
5. [B to P, `battle_start`] "Are lasers just very LOUD javelins?" [P] "They are quiet, Brutus. The shouting is yours."
6. [C, `hero_down`] "In the original script, the quiet one lives. This is not the original script."
7. [B, `lead_change`, genre bingo] "The TWIST! I do not know what the twist is! But the music went up, so it is a twist!"
8. [P, `boss_enters` Hive Queen] "Brutus checked twice whether 'queen' is a metaphor. It is still a metaphor."
9. [B, wave break, `intermission`] "INTERMISSION! Please visit the snack bar, silence your robots and enjoy a short film about bubbles!"
10. [B, `victory`] "Roll the credits! No, wait, there is more! There is always more! It is a SEQUEL!"

### E3 Cheerful Menace (machines, results screens, UI errors, over-helpful prompts that narrate themselves)

Rules: (1) customer-service positivity applied to destruction, never to pain: the menace is procedural; (2) devices read their own status out loud and take instructions personally; (3) apologies are procedural and arrive after the damage; (4) the props of the register are the survey, the receipt, the queue, the warranty sticker and the hold line; (5) the victim of the menace is a bubble, a battle or a schedule, never a person's suffering; (6) the Courtesy voice speaks on the defeat screen only when the enemy was Courtesy (otherwise the defeat is Cassandra's).

1. [`greeter_unit` taunt] "Welcome! Please remain calm and within range."
2. [`refund_crawler` death] "Your return has been accepted. Please rate your explosion."
3. [`valet_drone` taunt] "May I take your shield? Thank you. It is now my shield."
4. [Loading line] "Calibrating the cheerfulness of the robots (currently: too high)..."
5. [Defeat screen, Courtesy voice] "Thank you for your defeat. Your satisfaction is important to us. Please take a survey. The survey is another battle."
6. [Shield bark, low bubble, any shielded unit] "Shield at twelve percent. Please enjoy the remaining twelve percent."
7. [`grand_concierge` entrance] "Welcome to the Violence Desk. Your number is: now."
8. [`warranty_tech` taunt] "Out of warranty? Fixed. In warranty? Fixed. Yours? Void on arrival."
9. [`maitre_deluxe`, `please_wait`] "This way, please. No, not that way. The other way. The round way."
10. [UI, Deploy while no army is placed] "Your army has been placed in a queue. You are number one. The queue is the battle."

## 2. Per-surface style guides

Every guide gives the rule, the limit, a good example and one thing to avoid. Limits match spec/humor.md (bubbles <= 12 words, announcer <= 22 words, tips <= 18 words).

| surface | rule | limit | example | avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **unit blurb** (codex, hover) | what the unit DOES in the rules, then one joke that names the mechanic; present tense | <= 14 words | Tidy Trooper: "Fires three-round bursts behind a 40-point bubble that regrows after three quiet seconds." | adjectives with no mechanic ("loyal, bright") |
| **unit lore** (codex) | the faction joke, one real number allowed | <= 35 words | Rummage Armada: "Every ship ever sold for parts, reassembled by the parts. Nobody has the manual; everybody has the cover." | real agencies, real programmes, real kit |
| **death line** (last words) | 3 or more per unit; kit, bubbles, lamps, warranties, receipts; humans are knocked out, machines power down, aliens splorp; the unit never begs | <= 12 words | Tidy Trooper: "I am fine. I am just horizontal." / Greeter: "Please rate your experience of destroying me." / Skitterling: "Splorp." | pleading, family, pain, "tell my wife" |
| **taunt** | an in-character brag or complaint, 2 or more | <= 10 words | Wrench Runner: "If it hums, hit it." | insults aimed at the player's real traits |
| **announcer line** | one beat, caps on exactly one word for Brutus; slots allowed ({unit}, {n}, {arena}); once-per-battle where it is a set piece | <= 22 words | [C] "He was there the whole time. I said so. Look: a violet outline, a very polite one." | meta jokes about the interface; two jokes in one line |
| **tip** | half true hints (counters, controls, rules), half jokes that also teach; every claim true of the rules at tuning; no balance numbers | <= 18 words | "Acid and poison ignore bubbles. Shielded armies hate spitters." | numbers BALANCE may change |
| **briefing line** | three voices, one line each; Brutus names the setting, Plato asks, Cassandra warns | <= 30 words | see `missions_outline.md` | spoilers for the star condition |
| **results lesson** (Cassandra) | a true statement of what happened, with {n}; the fix is a plain second line | <= 20 words + fix <= 14 | "{n} of our bubbles popped in one second. A bubble is a promise. Ours was kept by nobody." / fix: "Rotate a fresh unit to the front." | claims that can be false (padding lessons never print {n}) |
| **kill verb** | transitive phrase that takes the victim; 6 or more per cause; environmental causes also get 3 `solo` phrases | 1 to 4 words | energy: "zapped", "sparked out" | gore; anything that sounds painful |
| **wave name** | a nuisance or a social event, plural or "of", Title Case | <= 5 words | "The Recall" | the Ancient, Medieval and Modern names; real people |
| **loading line** | present participle ending in "...", an offstage task for a ridiculous object | <= 10 words | "Polishing the shield bubbles. Please do not poke..." | full sentences, questions |
| **translator subtitle** (NEW, alien taunts only) | `[Translator: 'X.' Confidence: N percent. Alternate reading: 'Y.']`; N <= 60; Y a worse pun | <= 22 words | "[Translator: 'We spit with love.' Confidence: 40 percent. Alternate reading: 'We spit; the floor is dissolving.']" | a rating above 60; a Y that is cruel |
| **shield bark** (NEW) | the unit reads its own status flatly; the number is the live percentage | <= 10 words | "Shield at twelve percent. Please enjoy the remaining twelve percent." | a number that is not the live value |
