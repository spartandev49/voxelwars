# Sci-Fi campaign: the nine missions (binding design, DESIGN-ERA-SCI-FI part 2)

Status: binding for CAMPAIGN-SF (`missions.json`), COMEDY-SF, AUDIO, UI (teaching data) and the MS lint. It expands the **binding ladder** of `arenas.md` section 3 (mission ids, titles, objectives, arenas and seeds, rosters, budgets, enemy composition, taught mechanic, star 3, set-piece id, reward) into full mission blocks, and uses only unit and faction ids from `rosters.md` / `factions.md` (34 units, six factions: `tidy_concord rummage courtesy skitter glowmoss quiet_hour`), arena ids from `arenas.md` (12) and prop ids from `props.md` (38). Unit, arena and prop ids are therefore **final**, not provisional: part 1 was on disk before this file was written (proposal ids that did not survive the merge, such as `lens_marksman`, `loader_suit`, `maitre_prime`, `hop_adept`, `eldertusk`, `bubble_warden`, `sf_hop_on_pop`, were not used). Where this document differs from the ladder or the roster, section 0.1 lists the difference and the reason. Written against plan.md v3.1 sections 4 (modules), 5 (rigs), 8 (MS schema and curve rules), 10, 11, 13.

Synthesis: the ladder, arena set, rosters and teaching order are part 1's (A's skin and set-pieces, B's discipline: one new tell per mission, a star 3 that tests an earlier mechanic, a blind-bot column, the lull-between-two-waves shape of mission 1). Star-3 encodings, set-piece packages with camera numbers, three-voice copy, teaching beats beyond the ladder's eleven, the progression threads and the reward ledger are this document's.

Sci-Fi needs the whole module chain #1..#19 (E-FREEZE = S-FREEZE). Its three own modules are M4 shields (#17), M5 cloak (#18) and M6b EMP (#19); the rest it shares with Medieval and Modern. It never touches M9 cover, M11 mines or `SUPPRESS`.

## 0. Conventions used in every block

### 0.1 Differences from the ladder and the roster files (each with its reason)

1. **Brutus's one-caps rule.** Five binding teaching-beat texts of `arenas.md` 3.4 carry two or more ALL-CAPS words. Ids, triggers, voices and hints are kept; the texts are reduced to one emphasis word per line (the voice law of `spec/humor.md` s2): `sf_b_bubble` "Watch the BUBBLES flash when they are hit! They come back! Hit points do not!"; `sf_b_hover` "The floor is LAVA! Your tanks have never touched the ground, so they win by default!"; `sf_b_emp` "ROBOTS! Switched off! Please hold!"; `sf_b_blink` "He was HERE! Now he is over there! The distance between is a rumour!"; `sf_b_air` "Something is ABOVE us! It has a shadow the size of a bedroom!" Every other beat text is verbatim. The same rule is applied to the briefings and set-piece lines below (the title-card exception of `humour.md` s1 is used only for the poster tagline of M1).
2. **Star-3 helpers.** The ladder names helpers `aliveAtLeast`, `cloakKills`, `strikeKills` that already exist in the closed vocabulary under other names. They are encoded as `keptAlive(def, n)`, `usedMechanic('cloak', n)` and `usedMechanic('strike', n)` with the counters of section 0.2, so the era registers **four** proposed helpers, not six: `ownBreaksAtMost(n)`, `vipShieldNeverBroken()`, `coreHpAtLeast(f)` and the shared star-2 helper `aliveCostFrac(f)`. `shieldsBroken`, `suppressed`, `gateBrokenBy`, `vipSurvived`, `killedFirst`, `blinkCasts`, `empHits`, `airKills` and `noFriendlyFire` are in the vocabulary or the ladder's list but not used by any star here and are not registered.
3. **God powers** (`god_powers.md` s2): `sf_off_switch` (EMP) is disabled in M1 to M4; `sf_gravity_burp` has `propDmg` patched to 0 in M3; every other power is on throughout. The orbital power stays on from M1 because it behaves as the Ancient meteor; the M8 beat `sf_b_strike` keys on a UNIT's `strike_call`.
4. **Cameos before their lesson** (the only exceptions to "an untaught mechanic is absent or inert", each declared in the mission's `teaching.exceptions`): the Warden's dome and the Tender's aura are part of the M1 bubble lesson, not exceptions; `junk_buggy` (`bailout`) and `salvo_cart` (craters) in M2 are first-sight toasts of machines the Rummage player already knows; the god power slot 2 in every mission before M8 (ring and crater, declared in item 3); the Spritz Medic's `detect` lamp in M3 (inert: no cloaked unit exists before M4); the Valet Drone's `detect` in M5 (inert for the same reason); the Void Manta's cloak in M8 (cloak taught in M4); the Hummock's heal aura in M7. Nothing else.
5. **Plus-items** (rewards beyond the ladder's list) are marked `+`; every ladder reward appears unchanged. Quick-unlock items name arena presets and a boss-rush preset and are valid only if Quick locks presets (CU11 reward ledger; if it does not, the substitution column of section 3 applies, no hollow reward).
6. **Currency** is ergs (`fmtCost`). **Seeds and times** are the ladder's. **Reference armies** are the ladder's sketches against the `rosters.md` costs (85 to 100 percent of budget, <= 16 types; each recomputed here from the master table: 2,980 / 4,110 / 6,200 / 4,060 / 5,810 / 8,260 / 7,830 / 8,740 / 12,060); CAMPAIGN-SF fixes them against `stats.js` after BALANCE.
7. **Rex and Concierge wake-ups.** The ladder says the Rex "sleeps at the core until the first pylon falls" and the Concierge "holds the plaza end" then strides out at 25 s. Both are encoded as a placed group with `order: hold` plus a script `order` op (request R2); the fallbacks that need no new op are: the Rex is spawned by the existing `spawn` op from `rex_bay` on the first `prop_destroyed` of a pylon, and the Concierge uses `style: guard` with an `aggro` radius of 28 u and the billboard collapse is presentation only.
8. **Weather names.** `arenas.md` names the render weather rows `ember_ion`, `neon_rain` and `spores`; `M.md` 3.3 closes `WEATHER_KINDS` to `clear rain storm snow sandstorm fog smog ion_storm ash`. Request R7 adds the three to the closed list; the fallback mapping is `ember_ion -> ash`, `neon_rain -> rain`, `spores -> fog`. The M4 blackout uses `smog` (lamp reach x0.8).
9. **M1 set-piece trigger and time.** `sf_sp_lunch_served` fires from a `MissionRuntime` timer (content layer, CAMPAIGN-owned, the `ZeusStrikes` pattern), so M1 needs no M14 and runs in the plumbing slice. The ladder says 45 s, but `arenas.md` 3.5 itself places the end of wave 1 (the lull and its lesson beat `sf_b_lull`) at about 45 s of battle time; a 4 s shot at the same moment would defer the lesson of the mission. The timer therefore fires at **30 s**, in the middle of the first exchange (contact is at about 9 s, so the shot is never over the first volley); everything else in the package is the ladder's.
10. **Names**: factions are written in prose as the Tidy Concord, the Rummage Armada, Courtesy Systems, the Skitter Hive, the Glowmoss Reach and the Quiet Hour; data uses the ids.

### 0.2 Schema choices (MS fields this document defines once; identical to the Medieval and Modern packs so the lint has one meaning)

- **par**: `{type:'cost'|'time'|'none', value}`. `cost` = the spend ceiling of the thrift star (briefing shows "Spend at most X ergs"); the ladder's par column (2,250 / 3,400 / 5,600 / 3,200 / 4,500 / 7,200 / 6,400 / 8,000 / 11,000) is stored as `par {type:'cost', value}` and displayed as "Reference army: N ergs"; **only M1 uses it as a star**. No Sci-Fi mission uses `time` par (the `underTime` star of M5 carries its own seconds).
- **Star 1** = win. **Star 2** = `{id:'half', helper:'aliveCostFrac', args:[0.5]}` (the Ancient generic test: at least half the army by cost alive at the end; summoned and bailed-out units are excluded from both numbers). **Star 3** = the mission's own test.
- **Closed vocabulary used**: `usedMechanic(id, n)`, `thrift(par)`, `underTime(s)`, `keptAlive(def, n)`. **Proposed helpers (4 of the allowed 6)**: `aliveCostFrac(f)` (star 2), `ownBreaksAtMost(n)` (your own `shield_break` events, counted over the battle; <= n), `vipShieldNeverBroken()` (the VIP's bubble never emits `shield_break`), `coreHpAtLeast(f)` (the defended core's hit points at the end, as a fraction of its maximum, >= f).
- **`usedMechanic` ids** and the counter `n` counts (each is a generic `MissionTracker` counter that reads sim events; the same counters feed the achievements in `mutators_achievements.md`):

| mechanic id | taught in | counter |
|---|---|---|
| `shield` | M1 | `shield_break`: enemy bubbles broken by the player's side |
| `hover` | M2 | `hover_liquid_kill`: kills by hover-layer units of the player standing over deep water, lava, goo or a rift |
| `cloak` | M4 | `cloak_kill`: kills made by a player unit that was cloaked when its attack began |
| `emp` | M5 | `emp_hit`: distinct enemy machines stunned by a player-owned pulse |
| `blink` | M7 | `unit_blink`: blinks cast by the player's units |
| `strike` | M8 | `strike_kill`: kills by a player `call_strike` or the orbital god power within 0.6 s of the explosion |
| `repair` | (first-sight beat only, M3) | `unit_heal` events by the Medic, the Tech, the Shepherd, the Hummock's aura and the Nano Spritz (events, not hit points) |
| `air` | (first-sight beat only, M8) | `air_kill`: kills of air-layer units by the player's side |

- **Set-piece package** = `{id, sim event, camera shot {from, to, hold, ease}, announcer slot, three sample lines, stinger kind, sfx[]}`. Slots are the ladder's (`campaign_<missionId>/mid`, `/emp`, `/boss`, `/win`, `/finale`), priority 5, `bypassAlternation`. Shots run in real time while the sim runs at 0.5x; possession, aim, photo mode and cinematic mode defer the shot; Esc or click skips it; Reduce Motion turns every dolly into a cut; the R16 flash limiter applies to white flashes. Camera anchors are named (markers or runtime-resolved units), written `anchor + (dx, height, dz)` in units; markers are **derived from the generated arena** (`arenas.md` s1), never typed as absolute coordinates, so blocks give marker ids and roles. Stinger kinds (AUDIO taxonomy): `comic`, `hit`, `swell`, `dread`, `fanfare`; each ducks music 6 dB for 700 ms; only the M9 finale fanfare bypasses the limiter.
- **Voice rules for every line below**: Brutus = one ALL-CAPS emphasis word per line (a place or title may add a second), exclamation marks; Plato = ends on a question or a measured understatement; Cassandra = short flat sentences, "I said". Briefing lines are the three-voice opener; set-piece lines are the live announcer lines for the moment.
- **Teaching beats** use CU5 fields; `basics:true` beats belong to the once-ever layer (`seen.basics`, the same five ids as every era) and are skipped for a returning player; every other beat is an era beat (`seen.beats[id]`, once per era). Sci-Fi beat ids are `sf_*`; the eleven ladder beats (`sf_b_*`) keep their ids.
- **Marker types** are the validator's (`hill exit vip_start general_spawn waypoint`) plus M14's `capture` and `core`; positions are inside |x|,|z| <= 32 (small), 48 (medium), 64 (large). **Zones**: player at -x, enemy at +x; medium zones are 14 u wide centred at +-35, large 16 u wide at +-48 (`arenas.md` s1).
- **Enemy `groups` fields**: `{defId, n, at{marker|x,z}, order advance|hold, squad, formation}`; "hired" groups belong to another faction by contract (`arenas.md` s3).
- **Env fields** used: `{time, weather, fog, theme, sky, gravity}` as in `arenas.md` 2.x.

### 0.3 Constraints this ladder puts on rosters, props and the sim (the `untaught` lint; checked by CAMPAIGN-SF and BALANCE)

The MS lint gets one rule, `untaught(mission)`: a def carrying a field of mechanic X must not appear before the mission that teaches X unless the mission lists it in `teaching.exceptions`. Field sets: `bubbles` = `eshield`; `hover` = `layer: hover` (a god power or a drone counts only if it is a unit); `cloak` = ability `cloak` or `detect` with a cloaked unit present; `emp` = `cc_field effect emp` or a `machine` boss with an EMP cap; `blink` = `dash kind blink`; `strike` = `call_strike`; `air` = `layer: air`.

| mission | what is absent or inert by construction |
|---|---|
| M1 | the bubble is the lesson. No hover (the Tender, Warden and Trooper are ground; the enemy is hum1 only), no cloak (`detect` appears nowhere), no EMP source, no blink, no unit-borne strike (the god power 2 is declared), no air (the Trooper's `hits air` is inert: nothing flies) |
| M2 | hover is the lesson; bubbles are tested. The enemy `junk_buggy` and `salvo_cart` are machines but nothing in the field is an EMP source (inert); no cloak, no blink, no air |
| M3 | bubbles and hover combined. The `spritz_medic` carries `detect` and `layer hover`: its lamp is inert (no cloaked unit exists before M4) and its hover is the M2 lesson; the Rex's EMP cap and rear plate are first-sight toasts (no EMP source in the player's roster) |
| M4 | cloak is the lesson. The `shush_bike` (hover) and `bubble_tender` VIP appear (hover M2, bubbles M1); no EMP source, no blink, no air |
| M5 | EMP is the lesson. The valet drones are hover (M2) and carry a `detect` lamp that is inert (no cloaked unit in the field); the crawler's armour faces are a first-sight toast; no blink, no air |
| M6 | cloak + EMP; the Concierge's bubble is M1; the Maitre's `please_wait` is a first-sight toast; no blink, no air |
| M7 | blink is the lesson. No hover unit and no EMP source in either roster; the Skitter acid (poison ignores bubbles) is a first-sight toast; the beetle's carapace is a bubble (M1) |
| M8 | the unit-borne orbital strike is the lesson; air is a first-sight beat (`sf_b_air`) and the manta's cloak was taught in M4; no EMP source, no blink |
| M9 | nothing new |
