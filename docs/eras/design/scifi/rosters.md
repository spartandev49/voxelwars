# Sci-Fi: the final 34-unit roster (binding, synthesizer part 1)

Currency ergs (Ancient hoplite = 100). All numbers are design intent for BALANCE and are never measured here; directions of counters are written BEFORE measuring (plan ER7: asserted rows need >= 200 battles per pair, Wilson 95 % lower bound >= 55 %, point >= 65 %, mechanic-off control). Ids are unique within the era and distinct from the 43 Ancient ids (checked against `src/content/era_ancient/stats.js` by script) and from the Medieval and Modern rosters (which already avoided every Sci-Fi proposal id, including the ones kept here); every id is lower_snake and immutable once shipped. The roster merges proposal A (32 of its 34 units; four renamed or merged: `bubble_tender`, `elder_hummock`, `maitre_deluxe`, `hop_notary`) with B (the Bulwark Warden and the Silent Signer, the shield-aura Tender idea, and its whole counter-matrix and scout-code scaffolding) under the fixes in feel_sheet.md section 2.1: no suited giant, no suppression, no mines, no low cover, no heli, no unit that needs a mechanic outside plan s4.

Legend. **Role** (sim role): melee ranged cavalry siege support hero monster beast swarm; vehicles are the role plus the tag `vehicle`; the air layer is the tag `air` and the ability `layer air`; **hover** (drones, hover tanks, bikes) is the ability `layer hover` and the tag `hover`. **`machine`** = anything not made of meat or goo (robots, drones, vehicles, mechs, emplacements): EMP stuns and shield-wipes `machine` only, `repair` heals `machine` only, `heal_pulse` (organic) heals `organic` only; acid, poison and gas are damage-over-time and ignore bubbles. **`eshield cap / delay / regen`** is the M4 def key. **Height class**: S under 2.0 u, M 2.0-3.0 u (a hum1 soldier is about 2.3 u), T 3.0-4.5 u (power armour, antenna packs, staffs), L 4.5-7 u, XL over 7 u; an `A` marks the air layer (altitude 3-8 u on top). **HP class**: XS <= 70, S 71-140, M 141-250, L 251-450, XL 451-900, XXL over 900; `+sh` = also carries a bubble. **Speed class**: crawl <= 1.5 u/s, slow 1.6-2.2, mid 2.3-3.0, fast 3.1-4.2, rush 4.3-6.5, air (flyers). **Cost class**: S <= 100, M 101-180, L 181-300, XL over 300. **Model recipe**: BP = hum1 blueprint through `compileSoldier`; BB = bespoke builder on the `LG/SLG` toolkit (`src/content/era_scifi/beasts/*.js`); lite crew = 6 parts per crew member (D12). **Tracer** T = built early: all M1 plumbing-slice units plus one unit per rig family (the unit for a rig that needs a later module is marked "needs M7" and so on). **Joke engine**: E1 Deadpan Technobabble With Numbers, E2 Drive-In Trailer Voice, E3 Cheerful Menace (feel_sheet.md section 8). `[B]` = survival boss, `[H]` = hero. First-mission codes follow the binding ladder in arenas.md section 3 (an unmarked code is the player's roster, "(enemy)" the enemy roster).

## 7. Barks, composition, termination, risks

- **Bark roles** are the nine existing roles of `humor/barks.js`; the sets silent today and needed here: **vehicle** (buggy, bikes, crawler), **drone** (medic, valet), **machine** (powering down, polite), **alien** (translator subtitle), **officer** (Housekeeper, Maitre), **crew** (cart). Rows in the master table name the nearest role.
- **Composition (armygen, plan M7 AA guarantee):** any army that meets an enemy with the air layer contains >= 1 unit with `hitsAir` per 20 units; any army that meets Quiet Hour contains >= 1 detector (Medic, Valet or Grazer) per 24 units or the scout report says `no_detector`; cloaked units are <= 25 percent of an army and never cloak in the first 6 s; shielded units are <= 55 percent of an army cost.
- **Termination:** air bosses descend to attack altitude; if only cloaked units remain they are revealed after 20 s of no combat (detection radius becomes global); the M4 watchdog counts damage to hp or shield; unhittable remnants (air with no air-capable foe) end the battle by the M7 rule; S11 fuzz per era with air, hover and cloak, 2,000 matchups.
- **Risk register (merged from both proposals):** (1) shield stalls: delay 3.0 s, regen 9/s on 40-90 pools, pool <= 45 percent of effective hp, poison/acid bypass, chip denies regen, EMP and `sf_warranty_void`; (2) cloak unfairness: the player always sees a rim, only AI targeting is blinded, detectors are common (three units), attacking breaks it; (3) air remnants: all non-artillery shooters hit air, AA guarantee, M7 termination; (4) recolour: five insect1 outlines and the ER3 silhouette hash, hum1 hooks (back rod, hood, dish, tool arms); (5) glow readability: the palette test, the greyscale rule, towers on edges, flash limiter; (6) draw calls: shields are a layer, hover shadows are blobs, swarm shares geometry; (7) audio storm: burst template cooldown 60-90 ms, maxVoices 5-6, shield absorb thinned, hover an aggregate bed; (8) IP: banned list in factions.md; (9) comedy that is only technobabble: every joke names a number or a tell; (10) Ancient with lasers: the ER27 dimensions; (11) overlap with Modern: no pin, no cover, no mines, indirect <= 10 percent; (12) boss feel: readable phases, EMP caps, shields with long delays, a topple or a splat as the end beat; (13) part and triangle budgets: mechs <= 32 parts, drones <= 8, hover <= 10, crew on the cart 2 lite.
