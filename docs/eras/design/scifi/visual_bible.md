# Sci-Fi visual bible (binding; `spec/VB` row for DESIGN-ERA-SCI-FI)

Owner: DESIGN-ERA-SCI-FI part 2. Consumers: UNITS-SF, PROPS-SF, RENDER, UI, the VB palette distance test, the reviewer rubric. Inputs (all binding, read before this file): `factions.md` (the six measured primaries and accents, the banned-reference note, the greyscale 40 px table, the neon-on-ink art direction), `rosters.md` (tint carriers, silhouette discriminators, model recipe notes, flagged parameters), `feel_sheet.md` s5 to s9 (the bubble cycle and its tells, silhouette language, chrome tokens, never-feel-like). The look is a **toy shop at night**: signage neon on ink, glass bubbles, brushed-steel appliances, jade fluff with a gold lamp. It is the only one of the four eras whose faction primaries are saturated neons and deep inks, and that is deliberate: the sweep in `factions.md` showed those are the only regions of the wheel at least 15 CIEDE2000 away from the nineteen Ancient, Medieval and Modern primaries, and it is also what makes a Sci-Fi screenshot readable as Sci-Fi at 40 px. Never grim, never grey-corridor, never a real agency or a real franchise.

Measurements in this file were made with a CIEDE2000 script (`de.py`, scratchpad; it reproduces the six `factions.md` numbers to one decimal: 16.8, 19.1, 19.9, 17.4, 17.2, 18.1) and are design checks for the VB test, which re-runs them from the registry.

## 2. Banned reference palettes and shapes (VB test list; extended only by signed append)

A faction primary + accent pair, a body colour set, a decal or a prop is rejected if its two or three dominant colours sit within CIEDE2000 20 of a reference below (pair or triple, either order) AND the layout is a band, split, check or plain charge. For shape references the test is the shape rule.

| reference (do not approach) | sample colours or shape |
|---|---|
| national flag sets: red-white-blue; red-white; black-white; black-red-gold; blue-yellow; green-white-red; orange-white-green; red-yellow; sky-blue and white banded; blue and white | `#b22234 #ffffff #3c3b6e`; `#d62828 #ffffff`; `#000000 #ffffff`; `#000000 #dd0000 #ffce00`; `#0057b8 #ffd700`; `#008c45 #f4f5f0 #cd212a`; `#ff9933 #ffffff #138808`; `#da291c #ffc72c`; `#75aadb #ffffff`; `#0d5eaf #ffffff` |

## 3. Emblem allow-list ideas

Emblems are canting jokes (a pun on the name) and enter by signed append to the emblem registry. Sci-Fi has no flags, banners or pennants: emblems live on pods, panels, bezels, shells and rims, never at flag size.

| faction | emblem (cant) | voxel size and where |
|---|---|---|
| `tidy_concord` | an orbit ring with one small moon off-centre ("Leave no trace": even the moon is tidying itself away) | 8 x 8, azure on ivory, shoulder pod, backpack tank, Dustpan scoop |
| `rummage` | a sprocket with one bent tooth ("the straight one was sold") | 8 x 8, soot on brass, shoulder patch, cart push bar, buggy door |
| `courtesy` | a lens inside a rounded square ("we are watching your satisfaction") | 8 x 8, fuchsia on steel, TV-face bezel, tray, crawler drawer |
| `skitter` | a cluster of three hex cells ("the vote is the sneeze") | 8 x 7, lime on plum, beetle shell, queen's crown plate, spire props |
| `glowmoss` | a drop with a stalk and a small lamp | 6 x 10, amber on jade, Grazer helmet band, Shepherd staff head, Hummock lamp |
| `quiet_hour` | a hollow rectangle with a gap in one side, a door left ajar ("the client may enter") | 6 x 8, violet on ink, cape hem, bike fairing, manta belly |

Gag decor allowed on models and props (objects, never beasts-on-shields): a feather duster, a tray, a bell, a ticket stub, a clipboard, a sticky note (blank), a squeeze bottle, a bow tie, a hard hat, a shopping cart, a receipt ribbon, a luggage cart, a desk lamp, a cup holder, a popcorn bucket (the drive-in frame), a rubber stamp, a lamp on a stalk, a door ajar. Banned emblem classes: any star at any size, cross or plus-shaped charge, crescent; any bird of prey; laurel; roundel or target rings; paired lightning bolts; letters and numbers; anything that reads as a real unit patch, space-agency mission patch, squadron badge or franchise faction mark.

## 4. Model rules

1. **No letters or numbers** on any model, decal, prop or sign. Signs in the world are pictograms (a smile, a bell, a lamp, a door ajar); text appears only in the UI layer. No hull numbers, no barcodes, no serial plates.
2. **No cross, star or crescent at flag size** (a face or panel of at least 3 x 3 voxels), including a contrasting plus formed by a horizontal bar and a vertical rib that span 60 percent of the face. Hatch bars on crates and blast doors avoid a centred plus; the Tender's dish rod and the Tech's tool arms are off-axis.
3. **No three-band flag layouts and no flag-sized two-colour splits** in the pairs of section 1. Hazard chevrons are diagonal and upward.
4. **No camouflage, no khaki or olive-drab uniform, no real helmet silhouette.** The Concord helmet is a **fishbowl**: a clear sphere on a collar, rim colour azure, a visor glare; no chin guard, no T visor, no faceplate. Soldiers wear bright toy-shop colours; hum1 skin tones come from the shared `SKIN_TONES` set in `blueprints.js`, unchanged (no era re-weights it, and no skin tone is ever the joke).
5. **Weapon silhouettes copy no famous model.** The pulse rifle is a slab with a rounded barrel shroud and a glow-rail; the rivet gun is a belt-fed box with a drum; the rail rifle is a long 2-voxel-thick bar with a lens at the muzzle; the pistols are boxes with a pip; the blowgun is a plain tube; the shiv is **short and silent** (2 x 2 x 6, edge glow only). No energy sword, no hilt-and-blade glow weapon of any length, no ring weapon, no kinetic-sphere thrower shaped like a known ball.
6. **Vehicles are appliances, not scale models.** Dustpan = a scoop-nosed bowl; Refund Crawler = a cash register on caterpillar pods; Junk Buggy = a cart with a door for a spoiler; Salvo Cart = a shopping cart; shush bike = a blade. No real hull profile is traced, no known chassis, no road-wheel count that spells a famous vehicle.
7. **Mechs** are squat or tall appliances: the Concierge is a desk-and-kiosk with a TV face and reverse-knee legs; Rustbucket Rex is a dumpster on four stilts with a crane tail. Banned: a long-necked quad walker, a cargo-lift exoskeleton, a cockpit in the chest, a head turret like a known walker. Mech legs are rigid and read in silhouette; the topple is a comic collapse with part scatter and a bell, not a fall of a burning hulk.
8. **Aliens** spit lime goo and acid and wear antler crowns and sacs; none bursts from a chest, none has an inner jaw on an elongated skull, none bleeds acid. The Skitter outlines are five different builders; the ER3 silhouette hash must see five.
9. **Aircraft and drones** carry only a faction emblem on the underside; no roundel, no tail flash, no stripes. Drone frames are discs or rounded squares, never a plus from above. The manta is mostly shadow with violet edge lines; its wings are at least 2 voxels thick.
10. **Spinners and wheels** use straight arms with symmetric paddles; no arm ends in a right-angle hook. The spinning dish of the Tender and the Valet's rotor arms obey this.
11. **Silhouette and far mesh** (plan s5): rails, barrels, lens hoods, antennae, dish rods, lamp stalks and staff heads are at least 2 x 2 voxels; cloth and capes at least 3 wide; the Warden's carried dome is a **sparse hex lattice of glow voxels at 30 to 40 percent fill** on a 16 x 16 x 6 offhand, so the soldier reads through it (a solid dome fails the far-mesh test and the tint floor); the unit-wide bubble is the instanced R3 layer, never unit geometry; the TV face is at least 10 x 10 voxels with a 2-voxel frame; the manta's wings and the glidewing's membranes keep at least 60 percent of their near silhouette pixels after `downsample2`; the Hummock's lamp is at least 4 x 4 x 4 solid. The `downsample2` lint is the arbiter; the defining feature keeps at least 60 percent of its near silhouette pixels at the model's own switch distance and at 80 u.
12. **Glow and tint.** Emissive voxels stay under 12 percent of a model; every unit carries a team-tint zone (>= 15 percent non-hum1, >= 30 percent hum1) as a glow strip, panel or lamp ring, in the TEAM colour, never replaced by the faction colour. A cloaked unit keeps a one-voxel team-tint rim (friend and foe stay readable; the violet rim line is decoration and is drawn in addition).
13. **Dark bodies need an edge.** Skitter plum and Quiet Hour ink are the two dark armies: each has a one-voxel highlight edge (lime tips, violet rim) on its upper silhouette, and look-dev records the worst contrast pair against `regolith`, `basalt`, `neon_pad` and `asphalt night` tiles. A body that vanishes on any of the four fails.
14. **Budgets**: <= 48 parts (gun1 lite crew 6 parts per member, hover1 <= 10, drone1 <= 8, mech1 and mech4 <= 32, manta <= 22, queen <= 26), voxel 0.1 for units and small props, 0.2 for buildings and anything over 6 u, <= 35 visible (type, variant) prop batches per recipe; vehicles <= 22 K near triangles, bosses <= 55 K, boss far mesh <= 15 K.
15. **Tone**: no wound detail, no blood, no gore, no scorched corpse. Knock-out is a tidy flop, circling stars drawn as a ring of round dots (never a five-point star), and a fade after 6 s; machines spark, smoke, puff oil and droop; aliens splat lime goo with a happy "splorp"; mechs topple with a bell. Muzzle flashes are round puffs and tracers are dashes, not star bursts. The colour grade of every arena theme stays saturated and light-on-dark (a toy shop at night), never desaturated mud.
16. **The bubble** has one look in all four armies of shielded units: a hex-ripple film in the TEAM colour (thin at 100 percent, cracked amber hexes below 25 percent), a pop ring on break, three falling hex shards, a grey wireframe for the 2.0 s SHIELD DOWN window, a clockwise sweep ring while it regrows. The faction colour never tints a bubble.

## 5. Three named reject examples (the reviewer rubric quotes these)

1. **"The Plastic Patrol."** A `tidy_trooper` in glossy white armour plates with black joints and a black chin-guard helmet with a T-shaped visor slot, a belt of white pouches and a short white carbine. Rejected: the chin-guard helmet, the white-plastic-and-black-joint armour set (a franchise trooper), the black faceplate. Fix: the fishbowl helmet with a visor glare and an azure rim, an ivory suit with round azure pods, a white tank backpack and the slab pulse rifle.
2. **"The Humming Blade."** A `veil_cutter` holding a one-metre-long single-colour glowing blade that leaves a trail, with a hooded black-and-red robe and a red-lit chin vent. Rejected: a humming sword (franchise weapon), a dark-order robe palette (ink + red), the red-lit mask. Fix: a hooded ink sliver with a violet rim line, a short silent 2 x 2 x 6 shiv with an edge glow only, a cape hem with the door-ajar emblem.
3. **"The Dome with Teeth."** A `plate_beetle` or `hive_queen` with a glossy black ribbed elongated skull, a second jaw inside the first, a tail and acid blood, ribs showing through the chest. Rejected: the biomechanical creature silhouette (elongated dome with an inner jaw), acid blood, ribbed chest. Fix: a plum shell with hex shimmer and a tiny head (beetle), an egg-sac abdomen with an antler crown and six huge legs (queen), lime glow tips, and a death that is a splorp of goo.

Watch list (not rejected on sight, flagged for the reviewer): a Concord pod emblem drawn as a filled blue disc; a Valet Drone seen from above with a plus-shaped frame; the Concierge's TV face showing anything but a smile or a frown glyph; the Shepherd's lamp staff head drawn as a green ring; the Tender's spinning dish with more than six beacons (a ring of dots reads as stars); a Rummage exhaust stack with horizontal black-and-yellow bands; a Courtesy lens inside a triangle in any LOD; a Manta wing with a symmetric swoosh and stars on the belly; a pastel or white hull that vanishes on `ice` or `deck tile`; the Hummock's lamp painted with radiating rays from a centred disc (a sun-disc read).

## 6. Reviewer rubric (applied to every model, decal and prop; answers recorded in the model checklist)

1. Does the silhouette read as the faction adjective at 40 px in greyscale (ROUND, LOPSIDED, BOXY, LOW AND MANY-LEGGED, HILLS WITH LAMPS, THIN VERTICAL SLIVER) and as the unit's role (a dome carried like a door is a Warden, a cart of tubes is a Salvo Cart)?
2. Does the defining feature survive at the switch distance and at 80 u (rail, lamp, dish rod, wing, TV face)?
3. Is the tint carrier where the eye goes, at or above the floor, and does a dark or bright body still read against the four floors?
4. Do all colours pass the palette distance test and avoid every banned pair, layout and shape of section 2?
5. Is there any letter, number, star, cross, crescent, roundel, bird of prey, hooked arm, humming blade, chin-guard helmet, lantern ring, cargo-lift suit, long-necked walker, eye in a triangle, inner-jaw skull, or camouflage in the model?
6. Is it funny without a caption (could the blurb be guessed from the model: the Salvo Cart is a shopping cart, the Concierge is a hotel desk that learned to walk)?
7. Is the knock-out, power-down or splorp readable and kind (a flop with stars, a droop with sparks, a goo splat), never a wound?
8. Is the bubble the R3 layer and not geometry, and is the glow under 12 percent of the voxels?
