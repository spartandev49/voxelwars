### Q7 (MAJOR) Per-move-class flow fields blow the declared +0.5 ms envelope on their own

### Q14 (MAJOR) Flat-trajectory weapons never lead their targets; the lead code is keyed on gravity; the plan has `noLead` but no accuracy model

### Q15 (MAJOR) An independent turret is cosmetic as specified, because the AI re-faces the whole hull to the target every tick
**Question**: M8 slews `u.aim` and gates firing on `aim`. What makes the hull move differently from the turret?

**Failure scenario**: The tank "turret" is a model part that always points where the hull points; flanking a tank from the rear is useless because it rotates its hull; hit-and-run `whileMoving` fire (jeeps, hover bikes) still stops to face.
**Required plan change**: Add the vehicle movement model: hull faces its movement direction (flow field / formation slot) with a turn-rate limit, turret slews to target at `turret.rate`, firing gate uses `aim` vs target within `arc`, a "present the front" behaviour when engaged, and decide reverse movement. Make this a branch taken only for `def.turret` (Ancient unchanged), with tests for rear shots and strafing.

## Verdict

Converged: **no**. The architecture direction (registry, def-gated primitives, goldens first) is sound, but five items would make the plan fail its own gates (Q1 ordering, Q2 counters impossible with the targeting scorer, Q3 hash vs G1, Q4 Ancient-frozen vs default-on fixes, Q5 merged defs leaking into Ancient modes) and the air layer, shield watchdog, armour model, projectile pool and draw-call arithmetic (Q7 to Q13, Q20) are specified at a depth that does not survive the code. Re-issue the plan with: a corrected dependency table, a per-change Ancient policy table, a targeting and role-model module, a layer audit table, a per-mechanic budget table with real gate thresholds, and goldens G1 (matrix), G7 (armygen/waves/counters) before the first engine commit.
