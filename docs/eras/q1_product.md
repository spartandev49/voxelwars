Reviewer stance: I am the user opening the hosted link after this turn. I walk title -> era chooser -> map -> briefing -> placement -> battle -> results -> next, then Quick Battle, Survival, Daily, Puzzles, Codex, Workshop, Arena Builder, Settings, Credits. Everything below was checked against `e.md`, `plan.md`, maps 01..08 and the source (probes were read-only; one scratch node script in the scratchpad parsed the announcer templates). Where q1_scope.md already covers an engineering facet I say so and keep only the player-facing facet.

Method note: a "grep plan.md" finding means the word is absent from the plan. Absent words that the user expects to exist: `set-piece`, `difficulty curve`, `pacing`, `tutorial`, `onboard`, `accessib`, `mobile`, `phone`, `touch`, `Take Command`, `orders`, `scout`, `cvd`, `selection card`, `time warp` (only as a cut-ladder item).

Ranking: BLOCKER = the plan can pass every gate and still ship a product the user calls a failure. MAJOR = a named user expectation or a core flow is unplanned or wrong. MINOR = polish that separates studio finish from "done".

## BLOCKERS

### Q1. BLOCKER. The plan contains no campaign design at all: 27 missions, zero titles, zero objectives, zero mechanic assignments, zero difficulty curve.
**Question.** Which nine missions per era, in what order, teaching which mechanic in which mission, with what finale, and who signed that off before any engine work starts?
**Evidence.** plan.md section 8 (C1..C12) is entirely plumbing (factories, validators, script registries). Section 13 says "design bibles (parallel)" in P0 and nothing defines their content or acceptance. The Ancient campaign (checked by importing `campaign.js`) is a designed arc: eliminate -> hold_hill -> kill_general (act I), protect_vip -> eliminate -> kill_general (II), destroy -> kill_general -> survive_waves (III), budgets 3000..15000, time limits 150..420 s, one joke title per mission ("Goat Across the Nile"). e.md section 5 promises "a mission exists for each mechanic, with a star that rewards using it". The plan has star-test helpers (C4) but no mechanic-to-mission matrix. The new objective types (capture, defend_core, escort) are listed in C2/M14 but no mission is said to use them, so M14 is built on speculation.
**Failure scenario.** Content agents invent missions in parallel per era. Medieval gets three `eliminate` missions in a row with new skins, Sci-Fi mission 4 requires EMP before the player ever met a machine, the finale is just a bigger budget. All gates green (the validator checks shape, bots check beatability), user says "these are the Ancient missions with different hats".
**Required plan change.** Add a P0 deliverable `docs/eras/design/<era>.md` with a 9-row table per era: id, joke title, act, objective type (each era must use at least 5 of the 7 types, and every new type must appear in at least 2 missions across eras or it is cut from M14), map recipe, enemy faction, mechanic introduced (teach), mechanic exploited (test), star-3 test, set-piece (see Q2), reward (see Q19), music beat, par budget and expected duration. Make the table a gate input: `campaign_validate` rejects a mission whose id is not in the signed table, and M14 modules are only built for rows that need them. Add a difficulty curve target (expected first-try win rate for a competent human per mission, for example 90/85/80 in act I, 70/60/50 in II, 50/40/30 in III) and map it to the bot bands in V4.

### Q2. BLOCKER. The set-piece moments the user will remember (dragon over the keep, tank column on the bridge, mech duel, alien queen) have no work item in any phase.
**Question.** Who builds the 27 set-pieces, with which mechanism, in which phase, and what does a set-piece consist of beyond "a unit appears"?
**Evidence.** e.md section 5: "Every mission has a set-piece moment ... that the camera, the announcer and the music treat as an event". plan.md: grep "set-piece" returns nothing. The only related text is C3 "`script_beat` event forwarded to announcer and camera hint" and M14 "script events `strike spawn prop weather beat`". **Failure scenario.** The dragon spawns by `script.events spawn`, flies in, and the camera stays on the minimap-scale orbit view looking at 400 knights. The announcer says a generic line. The player misses the entire moment. The same happens for 26 other missions. That is the difference between "a campaign" and "a campaign with moments", and it was the sentence in e.md that the user would quote back.
**Required plan change.** Define a set-piece as a 5-channel package (script event, 3 to 6 s camera shot with shot list, announcer line with slot, music stinger or mood switch, sfx hit) and add a `setpiece` field to the mission schema validated for all five channels. Add work items: R-side `CameraRig.shot(spec)` (dolly to a target, hold, release, honours Reduce Motion and the killcam cancel), `script_beat` -> `audio.music.stinger`, `meta.js onEvent` fan-out. Budget: 27 packages, one per mission, each with a screenshot filmstrip (4 frames) as acceptance evidence. Order the sim support for the three named ones (dragon M7, tank column M2+M8, mech duel, queen) before the campaign agents start, not after (q1_scope Q2 covers the dragon ordering).

### Q4. BLOCKER. Verification measures structure and bot win rates; nothing in P0..P8 measures whether a human would enjoy the thing, and the only subjective check (V10) is the last step of P9.

## What the plan got right (do not churn)
- Golden tests before engine edits, the `rng2` second stream and frozen Ancient ids/text: the Ancient player's trust is protected.
- Additive save design (flat `progress.stars`, `progress.eras`, no version bump) and the daily-era-from-a-separate-stream idea.
- Tracer-bullet per unit family, completeness contract per unit, and the honest "unverified" section.
- Counting asset licences, synth fallbacks and sprite packs before the audio volume.
- Draw-call buy-back (R12) before content lands, and per-era `perf_assert`.
- Naming the dragon, trebuchet, tank, heli and mech rigs explicitly with part tables and model budgets.
- Per-era humour premise being the same three commentators (continuity), and banning real nations.

## Verdict
Converged: NO. The engine and risk machinery are strong, but the product layer is mostly missing. The plan has no campaign design, no set-piece mechanism, no definition of era feel, no teaching design, no player-control/HUD design for the new mechanics, no decision on Time Warp, god powers and the reward economy, and a verification stack that can pass while the game is dull. Plan v2 must add: the design bibles with the 27-row mission tables (Q1), the set-piece package (Q2), the era feel sheets (Q3), a vertical-slice gate (Q4), a tutorial system (Q5), and the traceability table from e.md (Q7); then close the majors on tone, controls, god powers, Time Warp, announcer neutrality, flash limiter, audio mix and the reward ledger.
