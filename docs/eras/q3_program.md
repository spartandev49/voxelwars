## Assessment of the lens questions

- **Provenance executable with `build.mjs`?** Yes. `build.mjs` runs `gen-registry`, bundles with esbuild, bakes `__VW_BUILD__` from `new Date()` (the only non-determinism; one-line `VW_BUILD_DATE` override), embeds manifest, credits, core audio and clips inside the deflated `vw-pack`, and keeps CSS outside it. Comparing the inflated pack (JS, manifest, credits, clips) plus the `<style>` text is the right unit; whole-page equality is impossible because the Artifact tool wraps the fragment. Remaining gaps are residuals 3 and 10.
- **Fingerprints and amber/red vs `_lib.mjs`:** consistent; one real defect found (the existing hash misses `src/sim/abilities`, residual 4) and one real gap (inertness proof, residual 5).
- **Tiers vs ER list:** T-fast 4 min is credible only with the closure cache and a quiet box (map 08 estimates 170-190 s for tests alone at 3 lanes); T-era 8 and T-full 15 are tight; the plan's mechanism (every ER carries a tier and a measured time) is right, but it needs a fifth class for the criteria that are structurally heavy (residual 6).
- **ER coverage:** every ER has a named script owner only once `spec/VF` exists (assigned, final at P0 end, acceptance "every ER has a script row"). Two holes in what the ERs cover: visual-bible conformance and hidden-era leak (residual 7).
- **WBS method:** `wbs.csv` plus `wbs.mjs` is a credible P0 deliverable (the generating agent works from the plan and the final specs; ~600 rows; size classes 1/2/4). The 550-700 figure is imported from the q2 re-derivation, not derived by a WP rule written in v3 (v3 states only size classes and the split rule), and the duration and phase arithmetic do not reproduce (inconsistencies 3, 4; residual 16).
- **Calibration table:** has the right shape and the right terminal action; middle band dead (6) and checkpoint 2 unmeasurable as defined (7).
- **Staged releases:** feasible and honest about origin and rollback; fix what a partial release does to aggregate surfaces (residuals 12, 13, 21) and the DoD wording (17).
- **Cut ladder capacity:** about 35-37 sessions after Time Warp left, about 6% of the estimate, which is why stop-and-ask is the real lever; the claim in the plan is stale (5).
- **Spec deliverable table:** columns acyclic, acceptance-level graph cyclic around MS (10, 14); roles not all defined (8); `spec/UC` missing (9).
- **Is P0 plausibly finishable?** Yes, in about 3-5 calendar days. Resource-bound: about 100 sessions x 1.3 h at c x u 2.5-3.5 is 1.5-2 days. Path-bound: AR -> M -> RA/W -> rosters -> MS -> CU -> S-slice -> VF -> wbs is 8 levels at about 2.5 h per level (draft, review, fix) = about 20 h, with provenance (3-session timebox), G1..G12 and gate engineering in parallel. The two real risks are one REVIEWER role serialising 13 documents (residual 30) and the goldens waiting for provenance (timeboxed, with fallback). **Exit condition:** the checklist of residual 2, machine-checked, whose single most important line is "`engineHash` equals the tag's `engineHash`, G1..G12 and ER1 green on the baseline, and every spec row final or `draft until <gate>`".
