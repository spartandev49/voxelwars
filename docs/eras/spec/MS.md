## 1. Purpose and scope

The plan's mission table (six prose columns) could not be authored or checked (q2_product Q2, BLOCKER): the Ancient mission has about forty fields, star 3 is a JS predicate over counters that did not exist, `par` meant money in one mission and zero in eight, and nothing checked the design against the build. This document fixes, in executable form:

1. **The schema** of one era's campaign: nine missions and six puzzles (sections 3.2 to 3.4, 3.10, 3.12), the full Ancient shape plus `setpiece`, `teaches`, `tests`, `combines`, `starTests[{id,helper,args}]`, `rewardId`, `requiresModules`, `attempts`, `firstThreeMinutes`, `blind`, `inputs`, `caps`, `powers`.
2. **The closed vocabularies:** 25 star helpers with exact definitions over the BattleSummary and 31 tracker counters (3.7), 25 per-era mechanic ids with their counters and modules, 9 objective types, 7 marker types, 9 script ops and 5 trigger kinds (3.6), and the accept/reject verdict on every helper, op, trigger and counter the three missions outlines asked for.
3. **The curve rules** of plan section 8 as lint rules, with a stated unit for the module margin (3.8).
4. **The back-fill** of the Ancient campaign as proof, and the exact **build step** that makes `campaign.js` data come from (new eras) or stay validated against (Ancient) the JSON (3.14, 3.15).
5. **The lint** (3.16), its context sources (3.13), and the hand-off table that tells every other owner what they must provide (3.17).

Out of scope: copy (spec/H), balance numbers (BALANCE; every number in the outlines is design intent), sim internals (spec/M), screen wireframes (spec/CU), the 27 real mission files (CAMPAIGN-x work packages), the solver output of the puzzles (`puzzle_solutions.js` stays generated by `solve_puzzles.mjs --era`).

**Ancient policy (AP).** This work changes **no** Ancient path: `src/content/era_ancient/**` is read, never written. New files only: the seven files above. `campaign.js`, `campaign_text.js`, `puzzles.js`, `campaign_run.js` and `campaign_validate.js` stay the runtime truth and keep their JS predicates (G6 frozen); `docs/eras/design/ancient/missions.json` is VALIDATED against them by `MS-T04` and never generates them. `missionHash` (`campaign.js:21`) is unchanged and remains the key of `feasibility.json`; new eras use `msHash` (3.2). Policy: bit-identical opt-in; no AP row is added by this file (3.18).

## 3. Detailed specification

### 3.1 Files, tools and commands

```
node tools/ms_lint.mjs [--era=<id|all>] [--file=<missions.json> --context=<context.json>] [--json] [--warn-fail]   exit 0 ok, 1 errors, 2 usage
node tools/ms_lint.mjs --check-doc | --sync-doc           the generated tables between the ms-table sentinels of this file
node tools/ms_context.mjs --era=<id|all> [--write] [--check] [--summary]   context.json (Ancient: --check is a gate step)
node tools/ms_backfill_ancient.mjs [--check|--print]       missions.json of Ancient (deterministic; --check = "no diff")
node tests/campaign/ms.test.mjs                            1.8 s; MS_SLOW=1 adds nine real battles (about 40 s)
node tools/lib/negctl_lite.mjs MS-T01 ... MS-T10           proves the negative controls
```

`docs/` is read from the tree, or from `VW_MAIN_ROOT` inside a negctl copy (which has no `docs/`); code is always read from the tree itself.

### 3.5 Enemy, fixed units, overrides, powers

* `enemy.groups[].at`: `{x,z}` (absolute), `{u,v}` (zone-relative, `zoneSpot`) or `{marker, dx?, dz?}`. `order` is `advance` (default) or `hold`. `formation` is one of `FORMATIONS` of `sim/formations.js` (`block line phalanx wedge column skirmish circle hollow`). `hired:true` marks a contract unit of another faction (information; `enemy.hired[]` lists the factions).
* `override` (D-MS-13): `disarm` (no weapon: parked trebuchets), `passive` (unarmed, untargetable, excluded from `startCount/startCost/aliveRoster` and from elimination; an air unit flies the cameo path from `at` to the far arena edge and is withdrawn; hand-off H11), `disableAbilities` (ability ids removed from the def: the castellan's `call_strike`), `hp`, `speed`, `name`, `ai.style`. `disarm` and `passive` are exclusive.
* `fixed[]` free units (the goat, the Float, the Parcel Van, Plumbly, Dennis): `vip:true` makes the unit the objective's VIP; `selectable:false` removes it from selection and possession (Dennis); `free:true` documents that its cost is not charged (`battleSummary` already subtracts it from `spent`).
* `powers.override` fields: `cd delay r dur dmg propDmg structDmg heal count`; the interpreter (M15) reads the patched value for this mission only.

### 3.8 Curve rules, the module margin and the untaught rule

* **C01** the nine missions use >= 5 distinct objective types (Ancient 6, Medieval 8, Modern 8, Sci-Fi 8 in the outlines).
* **C02** each mechanic is taught at most once (`teaches` has <= 1 id per mission, enforced by the schema) and every mechanic of the era is taught or `firstSightOnly`.
* **C03** every id in `tests[]` is taught in a strictly earlier mission. MS-K04 ties `tests[]` to the helper (`usedMechanic('brace',4)` tests `brace`; `usedAll` tests every id listed).
* **C04** missions 3, 6 and 9 of a new era `combines` >= 2 mechanics, each taught in this or an earlier mission.
* **C05/C09** a taught mechanic has a beat with `mechanic: <id>` in its mission; mission 1 holds exactly the five basics beats; beat ids are unique and era-prefixed.
* **C06** the hard modules, **closed over the DAG deps of `spec/M.md`** (a mission that needs M12 needs M7 and M10), must contain the modules needed to teach its mechanic (`x-vocab.mechanics[..].modules`). The modules behind the star-3 counters that are not listed become the information `MS-V01` "star-only dependency" (Modern M8 needs M11 only for its star).
* **C07 margin = `eFreeze.position - max(position of the hard modules)`**, positions read from the `modules` block of `spec/M.md` (Medieval 13, Modern 16, Sci-Fi 19). The mission that first teaches a headline mechanic must have margin >= 1, else `marginWaiver {ref, reason}` (the COORD waiver of `cuts.md`; the outlines predict Modern M6 mines, Sci-Fi M5 EMP and M6). A waiver whose margin is now >= 1 is reported as information.
* **C08 untaught.** A unit that carries mechanic X (`context.units[id].carries`, computed from the real def by `carriesOf`: `armorFace` = armour, `eshield` = shield, `layer air|hover`, `ranged.arc high` = arc/shells, `suppress.amt > .1` = pin, `lay_mine`, `cloak|detect`, `cc_field emp`, `dash blink`, `call_strike`, `aura banner`, `summon_on_death bailout`, `heal_pulse`, fire type/payload) must not appear in a mission before the one that teaches X (enemy groups, waves, spawn ops, fixed units, roster) unless `teaching.exceptions` lists `{kind:'unit', id, mechanic}`. Until `src/content/era_<id>/stats.js` exists `carries` is absent and the rule reports `MS-V01` with the number of unit uses it could not check.

### 3.11 `attempts` and the median-human proxy

`attempts.computed` (GENERATED, `tools/ms_attempts.mjs`, owner TOOLS-VERIFY, not built here): for each seed 1..N (N >= 30) the records of the two bots of the VF 3.9 ladder, `autofill` (`generateArmy balanced normal` over the roster) and `counter` (the authored reference), for the same `missionHash`/`msHash` and engine tag; `p_k = 0.5 * p_autofill,k + 0.5 * p_reference,k` where `p_1` is the win rate and `p_3` the rate of earning star 3; `star_k = 1 / p_k` (null when `p_3 = 0`); `ci95` = `1 / (0.5 * (hi_a + hi_r))` .. `1 / (0.5 * (lo_a + lo_r))` from the Wilson 95 percent bounds of each bot; `bot: 'human_median'`; `runs` = N; `engine` node or chromium; `at` ISO time. No human data exists (single-file artifact, no telemetry), so the proxy is a **calibration device with a stated midpoint**: the median player is between a bot that knows nothing and a bot that knows the mission. MS-Y02 checks `star = 1/p` and that the point estimate lies in its interval; MS-Y03 warns when `missionHash` differs (stale) or the measured star-1 is outside `[design/2 - 0.5, 2 * design + 0.5]`. Used for: the design curve, BALANCE retuning lists, and the sizing of the assist ladder discussion. Not used for: anything the player sees.

### 3.14 Build step for a new era (exact)

1. CAMPAIGN-x edits `docs/eras/design/<era>/missions.json`; `node tools/ms_lint.mjs --era=<era>` must print `0 errors`.
2. `node tools/ms_gen.mjs --era=<era>` (WP-MS-GEN, not built here) writes, deterministically (sorted keys, no clock; header = source hash):
   * `src/content/era_<id>/campaign_data.generated.js`: `export const RAW = [...]` = `toLegacyMission` output **with `starTests` kept as data** (no function; `createCampaign` compiles them with `src/content/shared/ms_helpers.js`), `repeat` expanded, set-piece triggers compiled into `script.events`, `override` compiled to def patches, `counters` = the era's canonical table filtered to its mechanics plus the mission-local rules;
   * `campaign_text.generated.js`: `ACTS, CAMPAIGN_TEXT, MISSION_ORDER, TEACHING_BEATS (per mission), TEACHING_SKIP, REWARD_PARTS` with the Ancient names (AR 3.6.1 `cfg.text`);
   * `puzzles_data.generated.js`; `setpieces.generated.js` (the CU3 rows; AUDIO reads stinger and sfx ids from here); `counters.generated.js` (the SIM rule table).
3. `createCampaign({era, raw, text, statTable, helpers, mutatorStars, validate})` (AR 3.6.1) consumes them; `campaignApi.hash` is `msHash`.
4. Gate: step `ms` = `ms_lint --all` + `ms_context --check` + `ms_backfill_ancient --check` + `ms_lint --check-doc` + `ms_gen --check --era=all` ("regeneration produces no diff"); T-fast, about 4 s.
5. For Ancient there is no step 2: `tests/campaign/ms.test.mjs` is the validation.
