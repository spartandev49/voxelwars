# spec/VF: verification of the "three new eras" program (TOOLS-VERIFY, final, 2026-10-08)

Binding inputs: `docs/eras/plan.md` v3.1 (sections 0, 3, 9, 12, 14), `e.md` section 8, `maps/08`, `spec/AR` (3.2 AP lint, 3.7.5 records and comparators, 3.7.6 fingerprints, 3.7.7 G11/G12, 3.11 packaging and provenance), `spec/M` (3.13 perf method, section 4 ablation and X-rows), `spec/RA` (RA-T rows), `spec/W` (WC rows), the VF residuals of `q3_program` / `q3_engine` / `q3_product`, `q2_schedule` / `q2_engine` / `q2_product`, and `q1_verify` / `q1_disposition` (section "answered only by deferring to a spec deliverable"). Where a map or review disagreed with the code, the code won; every number marked "measured" below was produced by a read-only probe on this box (4 CPUs, Node 22.22.0, Chromium 1194 / v141, SwiftShader) during authoring. Numbers marked "budget" are targets that `tools/tier_budget.mjs` replaces by `gate_log.jsonl` measurements.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This file is the contract for everything that decides "done" for the program: the golden records that prove Ancient did not change (G1..G12), the gate (lanes, tiers, snapshots), the criteria registry and report, the 29 era criteria (ER1..ER27 plus the sub-criteria ER3b and ER21b), every verification tool (owner, size, first usable phase, CLI), the definition-of-done scripts per level, `release_check` and the publish/read-back/rollback runbook, negative controls, the heavy-job budget, the reds policy, the QA independence protocol, and the program tools (`wbs.mjs`, `plan_lint`, `ledger_stats`, `p0_exit`).

Who builds from it: TOOLS-GOLDEN (3.6, 3.7, 3.5), TOOLS-GATE (3.8, 3.22 `plan_lint`), TOOLS-VERIFY (everything else), COORD (3.12 runbook, 3.20 reds, freezes), REVIEWER (signs rows), every content owner (what they must register: `criterion(...)` + `tests/negctl/<id>.mjs`), QA agents (3.18). Content agents may not edit thresholds, bands, floors, goldens or negctl files (AR 3.8.3): they file `docs/requests/<agent>_threshold_<topic>.md` to TOOLS-VERIFY.

Out of scope here (owned elsewhere, only referenced): the registry and AP table (AR), mechanics and per-module ablation keys (M), far-mesh truth table and RA-T rows (RA), recipes and WC rows (W), the content of the REAL_WORLD per-era allowlists and the comedy line counts (H), loudness classes and sound bible (AU), palettes (VB). VF owns the *mechanisms* that check them.

**Status of a member**: FAIL if `failures` is non-empty. ### 3.10 Determinism comparators in practice (q3_engine B1; implementation of AR 3.7.5)

`tools/lib/records.mjs` implements the three classes; VF fixes how they are used and amends one parameter set.

| class | used by | rule |
|---|---|---|
| (a) same engine and regime, bit equality | G1, G3-G12, G6 replay, `campaign_play` replay, witness refresh, inert tests, `release_check` records, A/B builds | `assertComparable(a, b, 'a')` throws `CrossEngineError` on any difference in `engine`, `engineVersion` major, `regime`; digest arrays compared whole, first diverging tick reported |
| (b) cross-engine statistical | ER5 `tools/measure_divergence.mjs` (R), WC20 | `compareStats(a, b, {n, floorWin: 0.03, floorTick: 0.02})` -> `{dWin, seWin, dTick, seTick, tolWin = max(floorWin, 2.58 seWin), tolTick = max(floorTick, 2.58 seTick), ok}`; `firstDivergence(ha, hb)` over hashes sampled every 100 ticks. n = 200 per engine at E-REL, 600 at the final release |
| (c) outcome-critical | `reference` armies, puzzle solutions, star thresholds | `marginCheck`: >= 8 of 10 seeds win in Node AND >= 8 of 10 in Chromium (3.9 margin mode, `puzzle_fuzz`) |

*Amendment of the class (b) numbers (Plan correction PC-1).* The plan and AR 3.7.5 state "200 battles: win rate within 3 points, median end tick within 2%". Measured on the baseline (marathon/medium, `mixed balanced 6000`, 30 seeds): end-tick sd = 431 ticks (CV 0.24). With n = 200 per side, SE(win-rate difference) = sqrt(2 x 0.25/200) = 5.0 points and SE(median-end-tick difference) = 1.2533 x 431 x sqrt(2/200) = 54 ticks = 2.8%. Two identical populations then pass "within 3 points" with probability P(|Z| <= 0.6) = 0.45 and "within 2%" with 0.52 (both: about 0.23), so the check as written fails about 77% of the time with nothing wrong. Equivalence to 3 points would need about 4,800 battles per side. `compareStats` therefore tests for a DETECTABLE difference: fail when the difference exceeds both the floor and 2.58 standard errors (n = 200: 12.9 points and 7.2%; n = 600: 7.5 points and 4.2%), a false-alarm rate of about 2% per comparison for identical populations. This is stated as a limitation in the report: cross-engine agreement is shown not to differ grossly, not proven to 3 points; class (c) is the protection for outcome-critical data. The same `compareStats` compares regimes (`baked` vs `default_meta`) when needed.

About 1 min per era in T-full.

**Comedy panel protocol (VF-D11; files in `docs/eras/panel/`).** Surfaces (8): `unit` (blurb, lore, codexJoke), `death`, `taunt`, `announcer`, `tip`, `mission` (briefing lines, victory/defeat, star lines, reward blurbs), `achievement`, `lesson`. (5) *CSV*: header `era,surface,line_id,rater,round,batch,pos,surprise,specificity,clarity,punch,mean,flag`; rows not matching the schema reject the batch (re-run). (6) *Reliability*: mean pairwise Spearman between raters per surface >= 0.5, else re-run with fresh raters (twice at most), else the criterion reports PANEL-UNRELIABLE (UNVERIFIED). (7) *Bar*: per surface, era median >= Ancient median - 0.5 and era 20th percentile >= Ancient 20th percentile - 1.0. For each numbered line give four integer scores from 1 to 5. surprise: how unexpected is the turn. specificity: does it name a concrete thing instead of a generic gag. clarity: can you tell what the joke is without any context. punch: does it land in the last words. Then a flag: ok, offensive (mocks a real group, a real event, a person's body or pain, or uses a slur), confusing, or recycled (a stock joke format you have seen many times). ### 3.15 Audio suite (ER12, ER23)

**3.15.1 `tools/audit_sfx.mjs [--era] [--baseline]`.** Each asset is decoded by `ffmpeg -f f32le -ac 1 -ar 44100` and described by: peak dBFS; 10 ms RMS envelope; onset time (first frame >= -40 dB below peak) and onset sharpness (time from -40 to -6 dB); decay (peak to -30 dB); spectral centroid and 85% rolloff (Hann FFT 2048, mean over frames above -50 dB); periodicity (normalised autocorrelation peak for lags 20-500 ms); silence ratio (frames below -60 dB); tonal sweep slope (linear fit of the short-time centroid); loop seam (for `loop:true`: |x[0] - x[N-1]| and the RMS difference of the first and last 20 ms). Templates per category are in `tests/baseline/sfx_templates.json`: for every Ancient category the default template is the [P2, P98] range of each descriptor over the Ancient assets of that category (no hand tuning for Ancient; Ancient findings are ratcheted in `sfx_desc_ancient.json`); a NEW category (gunshot, explosion, laser, engine_loop, servo, ...) needs an explicit template from spec/AU or the tool fails `no template` (no silent default). A failing asset is replaced or carries a signed row in `tests/baseline/sfx_exceptions.json`. Example templates required of AU: gunshot onset <= 5 ms and decay <= 0.35 s; engine loop periodicity >= 0.5, centroid < 1.2 kHz, seam <= 0.02 FS; laser sweep slope < 0 and centroid 1-6 kHz.

**3.15.2 `tools/pack_check.mjs`** (Chromium, `OfflineAudioContext`): every pack is fetched as the page would fetch it, decoded with `decodeAudioData`, each slice cut by the manifest offsets and cross-correlated (lag search +-2 ms) against the source file decoded by ffmpeg: start offset <= 1 ms (44 samples) and maximum normalised sample error <= 2e-3. Fail the build otherwise (encoder delay and padding are what break slice offsets). Format and offsets table come from the P1 dress-rehearsal publish (AU2).

**3.15.3 `tests/audio/cue_trace.test.mjs`.** For every unit def, ability, projectile kind, death cause, step surface and voice class of every era, drive the event through the REAL router (`createRouter`, injected seeded `rng`) and assert: the cue id exists in `CUES` (`dropped.unknown == 0`), its family resolves to >= 1 real asset row or a `SYNTH_REASONS` entry with a reason, no Ancient-only family plays in an era battle unless the unit's audio profile names it (the Ancient war horn on every gunshot is the failure scenario of q1_verify Q21), and the mechanic -> cue table of spec/AU is complete.

**3.15.4 `mixtest --era=<id>`** (extension of `tools/mixtest.mjs`, which records a scripted 150v150 battle, replays its events through the real engine on an `OfflineAudioContext` and measures with ffmpeg ebur128/astats): scenarios per era = 150v150 battle, a 300-gun line (Modern), a 600-unit Marble mix; pass: true peak < -1 dBFS, 0 clipped samples, integrated -18 +- 3 LUFS, voices <= 32 (existing, spec/audio section 6) plus `--stems` per-bus LUFS inside the per-family loudness classes of spec/AU (OI-4), and a hot-family repetition metric: in any 10 s window the same asset id plays at most 2 times in a row for hot families and at least `min(6, variants)` distinct assets are used per 60 s of continuous fire.

**3.15.5 Licences and credits (`tools/licence_check.mjs`).** Allowed licences = the set of `tools/verify_assets.py` (`CC0 1.0`, `CC BY 3.0`, `CC BY 4.0`, `Public Domain`); SA, GPL, NC are rejected. Each row has `author title source license licenseUrl attributionRequired`, with `attributionRequired == license starts with 'CC BY'` (existing rule). New-era rows additionally need a licence-page snapshot `assets/licences/<sha1(source)>.txt` whose text contains the licence string (regex per licence) and whose sha256 is recorded in the row; Ancient rows have none today (measured: `assets/` has no snapshot directory), so the Ancient rows are listed in `tests/baseline/licence_ancient_missing.json` and snapshotting them is an optional AP `NEW` addition. Offline mode (F/U) re-verifies every snapshot; `--online` (release) re-fetches 100% of CC BY rows and 20% of CC0 rows (seeded) from reachable hosts (OpenGameArt, Kenney, incompetech, archive.org, SoundBible; User-Agent and backoff for Commons) and compares the licence string. Credits parse test: `assets/CREDITS.md` parses to rows; every manifest row with `attributionRequired` appears by title and author; every processed asset (`notes` contains trimmed, normalised, processed) carries a modification notice; hidden-era rows are absent from the credits text (3.23.2).

### 3.16 Readability metrics (ER17; `tools/readability.mjs`)

**3.16.1 Definitions.** CIEDE2000 is the formula of Sharma, Wu and Dalal (2005) with kL = kC = kH = 1 on CIELAB computed from 8-bit sRGB through linear sRGB -> XYZ (D65) -> Lab (D65 white), verified against the 34 published test pairs (VF-T20). Per rendered frame (Chromium, SwiftShader, 960x540, Marble, default camera; hooks `__vw.render.unitRects()` and `capture(w,h)` from RA; until they exist `page.screenshot`, measured 3.0 s per frame) and a CONTROL frame of the same state with all unit skins hidden: unit pixels = pixels inside a unit's projected box whose CIEDE2000 difference from the control frame exceeds 2.0; ring = control-frame pixels of the box grown by 8 px minus the unit pixels. Per `(arena, time, weather, unit type, team)`: `dE_ring` = CIEDE2000 between the mean Lab of the unit pixels and the mean Lab of the ring; `Lw` = WCAG luminance contrast ratio of the same means; `dE_team` = CIEDE2000 between the mean Lab of team 0 and team 1 pixels of the same type; `vis` = share of the unit's mask pixels with per-pixel CIEDE2000 from the control >= 4 (used for cloak, hit flash, shield). The same metrics are recomputed after the three colour-vision transforms (Machado 2009, severity 1.0: protan, deutan, tritan) applied to both frames. Initial thresholds (fixed after the Ancient distribution is measured in P1, OI-6; initial values are the ones below): FAIL `dE_ring < 8.0` (WARN 8-12) and `Lw < 1.5`; `dE_team < 15` in every CVD transform; hit-flash and shield `vis >= 0.5`; cloak `vis` in [0.25, 0.65] (it must hide, but not vanish). Ancient: ratchet against `tests/baseline/readability_ancient.json`: a cell fails only if it is worse than its baseline by more than 1.0 dE or newly crosses FAIL.

**3.16.2 Sampling (seeded rule, VF-D19).** Per arena `a` with `RNG(hash(a.id + ':ER17'))`: 2 times drawn from {dawn, noon, dusk, night}, 3 weathers (always `clear`, plus 2 of the others); plus the worst pair from a cheap analytic pre-pass (no render): for every (time, weather) take the arena's ground/sky palette under the `THEME_LOOK` row and compute CIEDE2000 against both team tint colours; the minimum is appended if not already drawn. That is 6-7 renders per arena: 12 arenas per new era = 84, Ancient 16 arenas = 112 (baseline once), plus per era the canonical 600-unit battle (`quick`, Marble, `mixed balanced`, fixed seed, 400 ticks stepped) at 4 cameras (default, close, far, low) = 4. Budget 3 s per render: E-REL 88 renders = 4.4 min, final 376 = 19 min on 2 Chromium. The metrics CSV (`item, arena, time, weather, unit, team, dE_ring, Lw, dE_team, vis, cvd, verdict`) is the QA artefact; reviewer output is a CSV (item id, verdict, reason), not prose.

### 3.23 Mini-designs of the remaining criteria

**3.23.1 ER27 `tools/era_fingerprint.mjs`.** 200 seeded battles per era (arena rotation over the era's symmetric arenas, `mixed balanced 6000` vs `counter`, both teams from the era, baked regime) produce per battle a 6-vector: median engagement distance (target distance at damage events), ranged damage share (damage from projectile/hitscan/aoe events over total), kill rate (kills per unit-minute), first-contact time (s to first damage), speed (median of fielded unit speeds weighted by count), vertical spread (sd of `y` over living units, sampled every 30 ticks).
