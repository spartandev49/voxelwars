# requests/comedy_sim_events.md: sim event payloads the booth reads wrongly or cannot read (COMEDY-EDITOR -> SIM)

HUMOR already adapted the announcer to the payloads as they are (see `docs/lifetime_stats.md` section 4, which now states the real semantics). Nothing here blocks anything; each item makes a line funnier or removes a workaround.

1. **`big_swing.ratio` is team 0's power over team 1's, not the gaining team's** (`sim/power.js pulse()`: `r = p0 / p1`, `team = gain`). `docs/requests/humor.md` and the original contract said "that team's power over the other". The announcer used to infer "who is behind" from the documented meaning, which broke comeback detection whenever the player was team 1. It now reads the real meaning. If you would rather change the sim to the documented meaning, say so and flip two lines in `announcer.js` (`big_swing` case).
2. **`big_swing.flank` is `center` in most real swings** (`power.js _flank`: the cluster of recent deaths usually sits inside 25% of the army's width because armies clash front-on). 66 real battles in Node (11 setups x 6 seeds): 207 `center`, 56 `left`, 23 `right` (72% / 20% / 8%). HUMOR wrote proper `center` lines (the old copy said "the center flank folded" most of the time). If you want left/right to matter more (they feed the `flank_fold` lesson and the "YOUR left flank is folding" lines), narrow the centre band from `ext * 0.25` to about `ext * 0.15`.
3. **`trample` always has `count: 1`** and is emitted per trampler per 8 ticks, so the announcer branch `if (pl.count >= 3)` never fires; only the `unit_kill` route reaches the `elephant` category. Either aggregate (emit `count = victims this second`) or leave it; the lines no longer print a number. Add `team` (the victim's team) so the "Trampled" lesson stops counting a scared elephant flattening its own side (see `comedy_lessons.md`).
4. **`unit_rally` has no `team`** (`{ id }` only). The "THEY ARE BACK" line is therefore team-neutral. Adding `team: u.team` lets it say "YOUR soldiers returned" / "THEY returned".
5. **`lead_change` has no `flank`**, though the old template `lead_change_ratio` wanted one (that line could never speak; it was dead since the first draft and is gone). Nothing to do.
6. **`kill_streak` fires at 5, 10 and 20 only** (`world.js:365`). The announcer's streak lines are written for that; just noting that a streak of 7 is silent.
7. **`stalemate_warning` fires at 12 s idle, a forced advance at 18, Zeus at 30, ragequit at 44** (`consts.js`). The booth line "In six seconds someone moves. In eighteen, Zeus does." is timed from the warning on exactly these numbers. If you retune `stalemateAdvance` / `stalemateZeus`, change that line (`stalemate_zeus`) and the two tips (`tip_stalemate`, `idle_filler_zeus`).
8. **`ability_cast.ability` for the abilities the booth now reacts to**: `dot_cloud` (locusts), `war_horn`, `execute`, `net`, `chain_lightning`, plus the `crowd_roar` event. They are mapped in `announcer.js ABILITY_SUB`. `abName(ab)` is `effect || kind || id`, so an ability that gains a `kind` field would change its reported name; keep these five ids as they are.
9. **`battle_end.stats`** (`startCount`, `startCost`, `alive`, `aliveCost` per team) is what makes "FLAWLESS", "won with half their army" and "SO close" possible; `battle_start.teams` (`count`, `cost`) makes duels, "outnumbered 3 to one" and "a boutique battle of 14 soldiers" possible. Both exist today; please keep them in the payloads.
10. **`throne_sit.sitting`** (1 = sits, 0 = leaps up and shouts Retreat) is now read: the same event used to trigger "XERXES sits down!" when he fled. Keep the field.
