# spec/humor.md — Comedy bible, announcers, copy quotas, systemic gags (owner HUMOR)

## 1. Tone rules
- Joke at: institutions, bureaucracy, myth, military absurdity, battlefield physics, game UI, the player's questionable tactics, the soldiers' regrets, anachronisms (a Roman complaining about taxes while being hit by a boulder).
- NEVER joke at: ethnicity, religion (as belief), nationality stereotypes, real tragedies, slurs. The "barbarians" are a faction name; the joke is that they are very polite/well-organised/surprisingly into pottery, not that they are savages. Persians/Egyptians/Carthaginians are as competent and as silly as everyone else. Gore stays cartoon (confetti, wine, puffs).
- Specific beats generic. Surprise beats puns. Short beats long (bubbles <= 12 words, announcer lines <= 22 words, tips <= 18 words). No "LOL random". No meme references that age. No fourth-wall overload; one wink per screen at most.
- Running gags (callbacks across systems): (1) the goat is always the real hero; (2) Cassandra is always right and always ignored; (3) Plato keeps trying to define "battle" and gets interrupted; (4) Brutus's fake sponsors ("brought to you by Pompeii Pizza — now with 20% more ash"); (5) a laminated "Terms of Conquest"; (6) someone, somewhere, is "just here for the free grapes"; (7) the Immortals are not; (8) Zeus has a bad day.

## 2. Voice sheets
- **Brutus Maximus** (play-by-play): bombastic, always at 11, mangles Greek names ("Sparta-cus!"), adores a good flank, tragic over a lost shield, sponsor reads. Tags: `hype`, `sponsor`. Tell: ALL CAPS on one word per line.
- **Plato the Dry** (colour): deadpan philosopher, asks rhetorical questions, footnotes violence as metaphysics ("Is the shield defending the man or the man the shield?"), undercuts Brutus in 6 words. Tell: ends lines with a question or a measured understatement.
- **Cassandra** (analyst): weary prophetess; her predictions are correct and ignored; speaks in short flat sentences, past-tense "I said." Tell: "As foretold." / "Nobody listens." Triggers off `big_swing`, `army_low`, `lead_change`, `stalemate_warning` (she states the prediction *after* the fact). Her signature line is a legal slotted template, `"I said the {flank} flank would fold. Twice."` (`big_swing_fold`; `{flank}` is the `big_swing.flank` of spec §8.2 printed as `left`, `right` or `center`), and she is the natural voice of the lifetime callbacks ("Zeus has left {lifetime:zeusRagequits} times now. He always comes back. As foretold.").

## 3. Announcer system (pure selector and templates `src/content/era_ancient/humor/announcer.js`; UI driver `src/ui/hud/announcer.js` + the `announce` event of `Game`; stats contract `docs/lifetime_stats.md`)
HUMOR modules live in `src/content/era_ancient/humor/*.js` (picked up by the generated registry as `HUMOR_MODULES`; `content.js` collects the `UNIT_TEXT` export of `units_text.js`; the rest reaches the UI as `ctx.content.humor.*`, app_contract §2): `announcer.js`, `achievements.js`, `barks.js`, `killverbs.js`, `names.js`, `tips.js`, `units_text.js`, `ui_text.js`, `results_text.js`, `mutators_text.js`, `credits_text.js`; campaign, lesson, wave and sim-bark copy sits beside them in `content/era_ancient/` (`campaign_text.js`, `lesson_text.js`, `wave_names.js`, `sim_text.js`).
**Line** = `{id, cat, who:'brutus|plato|cassandra', text, cond?, weight?, once?, cd?, chain?:[lineId...], follow?:true}`: `once` = at most once per battle; `cd` = minimum seconds between uses (persistent callbacks use 480); `chain` names the follow-up beats, which are lines with `follow:true` and are never picked alone.
**Template slots** (a line is eligible only when every slot resolves; braces are never printed; the first letter is capitalised): `{unit}` `{unit2}` (display names of the event's two units; `{killer}` is a soldier's personal name for custom soldiers), `{team}` `{team2}`, `{faction}` `{faction2}`, `{arena}`, `{mission}`, `{prop}`, `{n}` (count in the payload), `{ratio}` (power ratio, "3" for 3:1), `{pct}`, and the three that feed the callbacks:
- `{flank}`: `left`, `right` or `center` from `big_swing.flank` / `lead_change.flank` (spec §8.2);
- `{streak}`: `kill_streak.count`;
- `{lifetime:<stat>}`: the value of the lifetime stat `<stat>` (a key of `docs/lifetime_stats.md` / `src/save/stats.js`: `kills`, `wins`, `kicks`, `chickenKills`, `chickenDefeats`, `goatKills`, `friendlyKills`, `zeusRagequits`, ...), read from the `stats` object at pick time.
Filters: `{unit|pl}` plural, `{unit|a}` / `{unit|A}` indefinite article ("an Immortal", "Hannibal": proper names and custom soldiers take none), `{unit|the}` / `{unit|The}` ("the Hoplite", "Hannibal"), `{x|cap}` capitalise, `{unit|up}` and `{unit|lc}` case, `{n|ord}` ("third"), `{n|words}` ("three"), `{n|num}` ("1,234"). **Never type "a {unit}" or "the {unit}" by hand** ("a Immortal", "the Hannibal"): a test fails the build. More slots: `{secs}` `{mins}` (battle length), `{nth}` (session counters), `{theirs}`.
**Conditions** (`cond`; every key that matches adds to the line's weight, so specific lines beat generic ones): `sub` (the moment variant, e.g. `chicken`, `draw`, `gain`, `loss`), `def`, `def2`, `faction`, `team` (`player|enemy`), `minN`, `maxN`, `arena`, `mission`, `flank`, `ratioMin`, `ratioMax`, `stat {name, min?, max?}` (or an array of them) and `milestone {name, at:[...]}`.
**Persistent callbacks from lifetime stats** (H8): lines whose `cond.stat` or `{lifetime:<stat>}` look back at the player's career. `cond.stat {name:'chickenKills', min:25}` is eligible while the lifetime value is >= 25; `cond.milestone {name:'wins', at:[10, 25, 50, 100]}` is eligible only while the value equals a milestone (the battle that just landed on it); all of them carry `cd:480`, and the milestone lines are `once`. There are at least 12 of them. Example: Plato, "{lifetime:chickenKills} kills. At what number does a chicken become a general?".
**Who selects**: the content module is pure (it imports only `../stats.js` and `./units_text.js`; no `window`, no `save/`, no `Math.random`). `createAnnouncer({rng, stats, templates?, config?})` returns `{onEvent(type, payload, ctx), tick(dt), nextLine(), reset(), setStats(s), setSpeed(x), debug()}`; `ctx = {speed, arena, factions, teamNames, playerTeam, mission, unitName(defId, plural), nameOf(unitId)}`. **Announcer selection runs in the UI layer**: it creates the instance, feeds it the sim event stream (`world.events`), calls `tick(realDt)` every frame, pulls `nextLine()` results (`{id, cat, sub, who, text, pri, at, dur, head, chain}`) into the HUD announcer and `Game.announce`, and passes the lifetime stats as the `stats` argument: a plain `{statKey: total}` snapshot from `ctx.save.stats.get()` (`setStats(obj)` after the app replaces it). On `battle_end` the order is: `save/stats` accumulates, achievements are checked, then the announcer sees the event, so callbacks read the already-updated totals (lifetime_stats.md §1). The Node tool passes synthetic snapshots (`--stats veteran|novice`).
**Categories** (priority 1-5 in `CATEGORY_PRIORITY`; every category has >= 3 lines): `battle_start`(5) `first_blood`(5) `kill_streak`(4, 5 from a streak of 10) `hero_down`(5) `friendly_fire`(4) `rout`(4) `charge`(3) `brace`(4) `volley`(2) `boulder`(4) `misfire`(5) `misaim`(4) `chicken`(4) `goat`(4) `philosopher`(4) `senator`(4) `trojan`(5) `medusa`(4) `elephant`(4) `kick`(4) `immortal`(4) `throne`(4) `hazard`(3) `lead_change`(4) `comeback`(5) `big_swing`(4) `army_low`(4) `stalemate`(5) `zeus`(5) `victory`(5) `defeat`(5) `timeout`(5) `mass_death`(3) `prop_destroyed`(3) `god_power`(4) `wave`(4) `idle_filler`(1), plus `campaign_<missionId>` for the nine missions (priority 5; `start`/`win`/`lose` moments, 3 lines each). **Quota: >= 120 templates with >= 3 slot kinds in use** (H8; H1's ">= 90 lines" is a subset of it); the file holds 486 after the comedy editor pass (473 standalone + 13 chain beats; Brutus 184, Plato 153, Cassandra 149; 47 categories, 88 sub-moments), most of the growth being lines that only speak in a context they are true of (see "Context" below).
**Event routing** (examples; the full mapping is `onEvent`): `first_blood` -> `first_blood`; `kill_streak` -> `kill_streak` (`{streak}`); `big_swing` -> `big_swing` (`{flank}`, `{ratio}`); `lead_change` -> `lead_change` or, after a long deficit, `comeback`; `army_low` -> `army_low`; `stalemate_warning` -> `stalemate`; `intervention` -> `zeus`; `chicken_tantrum` -> `chicken`; `catapult_misfire` -> `misfire`.
**Context** (comedy editor pass; the booth is fed the real event stream and has to be TRUE of it): `battle_start.teams` gives army sizes, so a 1v1 is a *duel* (only `sub: 'duel'` lines may speak at the start and the end, and leads, swings, routs and low-army talk are suppressed; `EXCLUSIVE` in `announcer.js`), "outnumbered 3 to one", "a boutique battle of 14". The announcer keeps a **session** (it outlives `reset()`): the second battle on one arena (`rematch`), the third in a row (`loyal`), two losses or three wins in a row (`losing`, `winning`), battle ten (`tenth`), plus **career** callbacks from lifetime stats (the very first battle, first victory, first defeat, round-number battles). `battle_end.stats` gives `flawless`, `tiny` (won with half their cost), `close` ("SO close") and `crush`; `perDef` gives `last_man`, `goat`; `reason: 'rout'` gives `rout`. Sub-moments are used most of the time, not always (`maybe()`), and their lines carry `weight: 3` so a rematch loop does not become a loop of four jokes. `first_blood` knows how early (`early`, <= 6 s) or late (`late`, >= 25 s) it was. A new category `ability` reacts to `ability_cast` of `dot_cloud war_horn execute net chain_lightning` and to `crowd_roar`; `unit_rally` is `rout/rally`; `throne_sit.sitting` separates Xerxes sitting from Xerxes fleeing. Anything still queued when `battle_end` arrives and not about the ending is dropped (a first-blood line never follows the defeat line). Facts about the payloads the booth depends on are in `docs/lifetime_stats.md` section 4; asks to SIM are in `docs/requests/comedy_sim_events.md`.
**Selection rules** (`DEFAULT_CONFIG`, tested by `tests/humor/announcer.test.mjs`): global minimum gap 3.5 s between line starts (1.5 s for priority 5), about one line per 8 s in battle; per-category cooldown 20 s (45 s for `idle_filler`); the last 14 line ids are never repeated and a line heard in the last 840 s (a whole first session) is strongly avoided, so **silence beats a repeat** except for priority-5 moments; the voice that spoke last is skipped (no voice twice in a row except inside a chain); voice shares are balanced toward one third each; a category silent for 2 minutes gets a boost so rare gags are heard; chains play as a 2-3 beat exchange 1.1 s apart; above 2x speed only priority >= 4 speaks; subtitles on unless disabled; optional TTS per audio.md. `tools/humor-sim.mjs` (`--minutes 60 --speed 1|4|both --seed n --stats veteran|novice --fresh --json`) replays 10 recorded event logs for **60 simulated minutes** and `tests/humor/sim.test.mjs` asserts: no line repeats inside its 14-id memory, < 8% repeats within 5 minutes, every category used, each voice 25-45% of lines, 8-15 lines per battle, and a per-template histogram in which no template exceeds 4% of all lines with >= 120 distinct templates heard in the hour.

## 4. Other copy quotas (all pass the rubric)
- **Unit text** for all 43 units (see units.md; the `UNIT_TEXT = {unitId: {name, plural, blurb, lore, deaths, taunts, codexJoke, proper?, <moment keys>?}}` export of `humor/units_text.js`; `proper: true` marks units whose name is a person's (Hannibal, Xerxes, Medusa; custom soldiers always); the moment keys (`kick rage horn revive misfire elephant_panic throne_retreat tantrum cluck monologue filibuster bribe taunt`, plus `engage hurt rout cheer` for the dog and the goat) are the bubbles the sim actually emits, and `world.bark()` reads `u.def.text[key]` first; `name`/`plural` feed the announcer slots `{unit}` and `{unit|pl}`): `blurb` (<= 14 words, the codex card), `lore` (<= 35 words, deadpan history), `deaths[3+]` (<= 12 words each, last words, shown as bubbles at low frequency: max 1 bubble per 1.2 s globally, hero/boss always), `taunts[2+]`, `codexJoke` (the stat-block footnote).

- **Name generator** (`humor/names.js`, `randomName(rng, opts)`): titles (`Sir`, `Dame`, `Lord`, `Lady`, `Citizen`, `Admiral`, `Doctor`, `Probably`, ...), first names (Greek/Latin/Egyptian/Persian/Celtic), epithets ("the Mildly Concerned", "of the Second Lunch", "Who Forgot His Shield", "the Unbothered", "Slayer of Chickens (Allegedly)", ...) >= 60 epithets.
- **Setting/quality/label jokes**: graphics names Potato/Papyrus/Marble/Olympian; "Corpses: Stay / Fade / Pretend They're Napping"; gore style labels; difficulty "Easy: Peasant Mode / Normal: Citizen / Hard: Consul"; "Rematch" button "Again, but smarter".
- **Kill-feed verbs** (`humor/killverbs.js`; the feed composes "<killer> <verb> <victim>"): one pool per `unit_kill.cause` (spec §8.2: melee, ranged, aoe, fire, fall, trample, magic, stone, kick, gore, drown, lava, spikes, geyser, poison, lightning, execute, misfire, bribe), each with >= 6 transitive `by` verbs and, for environmental causes, >= 3 `solo` phrases ("<victim> stood in lava"); e.g. "bonked", "perforated", "yeeted" (kick), "philosophised at" (confuse/magic), "gored", "trampled", "mildly inconvenienced", "turned to stone", "tax-audited" (bribe), ...

## 7. Editor rubric (every line is graded; rewrite or delete below 3/4)
1. Is it specific to this unit/moment? 2. Does it surprise? 3. Is it short enough to read in 2 seconds? 4. Could it be funnier by cutting a word? Delete anything that is only "random". Cut at least 30% of first-draft lines. Run the sensitivity sweep (no ethnic/religious punchlines) before final.
