# spec/rigs.md — per-rig part tables of every non-humanoid rig (owner BEASTS; linked from spec.md §4.2; replaces the never-written `docs/beasts_rigs.md`)

**Source of truth = the builders in `src/content/era_ancient/beasts/`** (the function is named in every section). The tables below are the builder output (`ModelDef.parts`) read on 2026-10-06 at 03:54 UTC, while BEASTS was still editing: **part ids, parents, rest rotations and pivot semantics are frozen (spec §0.8)**; grid sizes, pivots and origins follow the builder and this file is regenerated by BEASTS whenever a builder changes them. If a table and a builder disagree, the builder wins and the table is stale. `hum1` and `hum_lite` are in spec.md §4.1.

## 0. Conventions (all rigs)
- Coordinates are **voxels** (0.1 u) unless a unit is written. `origin` is where the part's pivot sits relative to its **parent's pivot**; `pivot` is a point in the part's **own grid** (voxel coordinates, may be fractional); `grid` is the part's size in voxels. A part rotates about its pivot; `rest` is a static rotation applied after the animated one (spec §4: `local = T(origin + poseT) * R(pose) * R(rest) * S(poseS)`).
- `root` = the ModelDef root = the instance origin, on the ground under the model. +Z is forward, +X is the creature's LEFT, +Y is up. Rotation signs follow spec §1: positive `rx` rotates +Y toward +Z, so a part that hangs along -Y swings **forward with negative rx**; euler order `Ry*Rx*Rz`.
- **Root rotations** (instance pitch/roll/yaw from a clip's `root` tracks, e.g. rearing, rocking, falling) rotate about the Animator's `info.pivot` = the origin of the base group's `body`, `frame` or `chassis` part, else its first root part (`animator.js buildInfo`). For the rigs below that is: quad1 `body`, elephant1 `body`, chicken1 `body`, chariot1 `body`, catapult1 `frame`, ballista1 `frame`, trojan1 `base` (first root part).
- "Clip axes" below lists what the clips are expected to move. Axes the builder's header states are quoted from it; the others follow the §1 convention from the pivot placement and are marked "(§1)"; the authored clips (ANIM) are the final word on amplitudes.
- Composed units attach sub-models with `composeModels`; prefixes are listed in §9. Every shipped ModelDef has <= 48 parts (VoxSkin `MAX_PARTS`).

## 1. quad1 (horse, camel, hound, goat; centaur body) — builders `buildHorse, buildCamel, buildHound, buildGoat` in `beasts/quad1.js` (`assembleQuad(id, species, meta, {skip})`)
Parts (ids frozen): `body, neck, head, tail, legFL, legFR, legBL, legBR` + static children `saddle` (all species), `mane` (horse, camel), `ears` (all), `horns` (goat), `barding` (barded horse). Species differ only in proportions; ids and parents are identical.
**Frames (from the `quad1.js` header)**: root on the ground under the body centre; `body` origin `[0, legLen, 0]` with the pivot at the **belly line on the centre line** (grid y 0 is the belly); `neck` child of body, pivot at the neck base (inside the shoulders), the neck is baked rising forward/up; `head` child of neck, pivot at the poll (top of the neck), the face is baked pointing forward/down; `tail` child of body, pivot at the tail root, hangs along -Y; `legFL/FR/BL/BR` children of body, pivot at the **top of the leg** (2 voxels inside the barrel), hang along -Y (so the older spec phrase "legs pivot at the top" is right and the body pivot is the belly line, not the back); `saddle` and `barding` are children of body in the same frame as the body (origin `0,0,0`, same physical pivot), `mane` is a child of neck (same frame), `ears` and `horns` are children of head (same frame).
**Clip axes**: legs `rx` swing about the hip pivot (forward = negative rx) plus a `t.y` lift for the swing foot (`anim/gait.js quadLeg(u, po, A, duty, lift, out)` returns `[rx, lift]`; legs are single rigid parts, the lifted foot is faked by translation + swing); `meta.gait = {hipH, pace, walk, trot, gallop}` with `{amp, duty, stride}` per gait (leg swing amplitude in rad, stance duty, stride in u per full leg cycle, `stride = 2*hipH*sin(amp)/duty`; `pace: true` = lateral leg pairs move together, camel); `body` motion (bob, pitch, roll, rearing) is carried by the instance **root tracks** (§0); `neck` and `head` `rx` (raise, lower, nod) and `ry` (turn) (§1); `tail` `rx`/`rz` swish (§1); `saddle, barding, mane, ears, horns` are static (the mane and ears may take small secondary `rx`). Mount/rider/attack clips: spec §7.3 (mount base group plays `walk/gallop/trot/rear/idle`, the rider plays `ride_*`).
Per species (builder values): horse `legLen` 1.0 u, `hipHeight` 1.2 u, gait stride walk 1.48 / trot 2.51 / gallop 5.3; camel 1.4 / 1.6 u, strides 1.98 / 3.35 / 7.07, `pace: true`; hound 0.6 / 0.8 u, strides 0.99 / 1.67 / 3.54; goat 0.7 / 0.9 u, strides 1.11 / 1.88 / 3.98. Attach points on every species: `saddle` (on the saddle part), `head_top`, `mouth`, `feet` (voxel coords below).
